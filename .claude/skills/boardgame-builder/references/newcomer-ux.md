# Making a heavy game learnable

> Read `board-first-play.md` first. Where this page suggests adding text to the dock (roadmaps, wizards, advisor panels), show it on the board instead. Text-heavy guidance was what made the games "confusing".

The user called Nebula Aces and Shipwreck Isle "very confusing… the rounds and steps need to be planned better". Here is what fixed it, and what a newcomer review found in every game.

## Run a newcomer review before delivery
Ask an agent to play the game as a first-time player, through screenshots and the dock only, and to list every moment it didn't know what to do or why something happened. Fix each item, then repeat. It found 10–20 items per game.

## Patterns that worked
- **Round roadmap:** a strip in the dock showing every phase of the round (Planning · Activation · Combat · End) with the current one lit.
- **One thing at a time:** the game's own phases (events, weather, night) appear as phase cards, each with one result sentence and a Continue button. Not ten log lines at once.
- **Planning wizards** for worker-placement-like turns, for example Shipwreck Isle's 4 steps:
  1. needs and threats;
  2. place one pawn at a time, each with a recommended job;
  3. risks;
  4. confirm.
- **Recommended move + "why":** a suggested move, a ghost preview (Nebula Aces' maneuver template) and an advisor showing what each option gains. A "Suggest" button that follows the day's priorities.
- **Explain losses and blocks:** "You lost your action because you were stressed", "Bumped: no action". Announce bumps, stress and damage in the dock.
- **Turn order with reasons:** a queue showing who acts next and why (pilot skill, bids).
- **End-of-round summary,** plus "tomorrow's top priority" in co-ops.
- **Warnings before danger:** an incoming storm or beast, spoiling food, "a rival is about to win".
- **Story framing:** an opening briefing, chapter cards and radio chatter. They help players remember the goal.
- **Guide full/light** toggle, so veterans can turn the help down.
- **Labelled numbers everywhere:** "3 stress", not a bare "3". Icons always have labels.
- **The first turns must be clean.** Don't stack the tour, questions and the computer's moves; queue them.
- **Skip empty prompts:** don't ask "anything to play?" when nothing can be played.

## Rules drawer
Write it in your own words, in the order a player meets things, with a short "How a round goes" section first. Link to it from the dock where a rule is used.
