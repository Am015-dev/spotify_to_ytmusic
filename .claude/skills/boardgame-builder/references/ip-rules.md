# Copyright and trademark rules

Game rules and mechanics are generally not protected by copyright; their *expression* is. Build with that line in mind.

## Allowed (copy faithfully)
- Turn structure, actions, costs, numbers, dice faces, track lengths, hand limits, victory conditions.
- Board *topology* (which areas connect), redrawn by you with new names. Better still: design your own map of similar size and connectivity.
- Generic genre words: dice, stronghold, monster, energy, city, hunt.

## Not allowed (replace)
- Game title; faction, character, place and card names; card, flavour and rulebook text.
- Board art, card art, icons, logos, character designs, miniatures, box art.
- Licensed IP inside the game (Tolkien, Marvel, recognisable kaiju like Godzilla or King Kong, etc.).
- Anything that combines into a recognisable character even when renamed (same silhouette, colours and role).

## How to rename
- Keep the *role*, change the identity: hidden ring-bearer → "Wren, the Bearer"; Nazgûl → "Wraiths"; a giant ape → an original creature with a different body plan.
- Short, pronounceable, consistent names across the game.
- Write every card effect in your own words; numeric effects can stay identical.
- New title (a huge area-control epic → "Shardfall").

## What to tell the user
One line: "Names, text and art are original for copyright reasons; the rules follow the rulebook." Don't lecture. If they push for real names, explain once and keep going with the faithful-mechanics version.
