# Redline Rally lessons spec (scenario tutorial)

- Create redline-rally/lessons.js (ES5, IIFE, no original game names, no emoji, goal <= 12 words, one-sentence fail messages naming cause and effect). Wait until typeof G, startGame, UI, GXST exist (poll like cauldron-fair). Register GXST.init({game:'redline-rally', scenarios, settled, keepClear:['.gx-bar','#panel','#hand','#acts','#gears','#goB','#bq','.slipq'], qParent: a hidden slot div like cauldron-fair}) and a click handler for [data-lessons-open] like cauldron-fair. If GXST.init supports a phoneSlot option use it, otherwise ignore (it does not exist in this checkout). Lessons never overwrite the saved game and must not show the goal card: start with UI.fast=false but window.AIDELAY small, startGame({track:'practice',laps:1,hum:1,opp:2,diff:'easy',seed:7,sup:false,tut:false}) and skip goalCard while GXST.active() (wrap goalCard like cauldron-fair). Each setup(): start a fresh game, then stage with a helper stage({pos,gear,eng,hand,top,rivals:[{pos,cards}]}) copied from tutStageRound (car 0 = you, rivals cars 1 and 2 play gear 2 with the two given cards via submitPlan, rivals always BEHIND you unless said otherwise so you never get adrenaline), then drawPlan(). Count the player's moves in a wrapped commit() (L.n++). settled() = G && !UI.busy && !G.pend && G.phase==='plan' (or G.over/you finished) and no boost/slip/discard panel open (#bq, .slipq absent).

- Six lessons in this order, ids and exact staging (practice track: 30 spaces, corners at space 10 limit 5 and space 15 limit 4, finish at 30, slipstream = +2 spaces; engine 6 heat = full). Judge from real car state c=G.cars[0] only after settled and after the round has resolved (record start values at setup): (1) id gear 'Gear and speed': pos 17, gear 1, eng 6, hand ['4','3','1','2','3','1','2'], rivals pos 15 and 14 playing ['1','1']; goal 'Move 7 or more spaces this turn.' success c.pos-17>=7; fail 'You moved N spaces. Gear 1 plays one card, gear 2 plays two: more cards, more speed.' par 1. (2) id corner 'Corner limit': pos 6, gear 2, eng 6, hand ['4','4','1','2','3','1','2'], rivals pos 3 and 2 ['1','1']; goal 'Pass the corner without losing any heat.' success c.pos>=10 && c.eng===6; fail when c.eng<6: 'Speed X at a limit-5 corner burned N heat. Keep your speed at 5 or less.' (3) id spin 'Spin out': pos 6, gear 2, eng 1, same hand and rivals; goal 'Pass the corner without spinning out.' success c.pos>=10 && c.stats.spins===0; fail when c.stats.spins>0: 'You spun out: speed over the limit with too little heat loses all your heat.' (4) id cool 'Cooldown': pos 17, gear 1, eng 3, hand ['H','H','H','2','3','1','4'], rivals 15 and 14 ['1','1']; goal 'Refill your engine to full heat.' success c.eng>=6; fail when settled and c.eng<6: 'Only gear 1 cools down 3 heat. Gear 2 cools 1, gears 3 and 4 none.' (5) id slip 'Slipstream': pos 17, gear 2, eng 6, hand ['4','3','2','1','2','1','3'], rival A pos 22 playing ['1','1'] (it ends at 24), rival B pos 10 ['1','1']; goal 'Land beside a rival and use the slipstream.' success c.stats.slips>0; fail when settled and none: 'No rival was on or just ahead of your space, so no slipstream.' (6) id boost 'Boost': pos 24, gear 2, eng 4, hand ['3','2','1','1','2','3','4'], top ['4'] (next card flipped by a boost), rivals pos 22 and 20 ['1','1']; goal 'Cross the finish line using a boost.' success c.fin truthy; fail when settled and not finished: 'One space short. A boost costs 1 heat and flips a card to add its speed.' Each with par, 2 hints (nudge question; show = {say, target: fn returning the real element, e.g. gear button '#gears .gb[data-g="2"]', a card, '#goB', '#boostY'}) and a debrief line. Put the rule-to-lesson table at the top of the file like cauldron-fair. Export window.RRLessons={open}.
