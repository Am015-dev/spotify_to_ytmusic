## Summary

### 1. Chapters
- **Ch1 Driftwood Dawn**: survive to end of day 3, eat every night. WON 3 stars. Easy: recommended jobs plus Plan for me covered food; shelter was built on day 2 by the helper.
- **Ch2 A Roof Overhead**: build the Shelter by day 5. WON 3 stars on day 2. Plan for me put 2 pawns on the shelter as soon as wood hit 3.
- **Ch3 Beyond the Treeline**: explore 4 tiles by day 6. WON 2 stars on day 3. Lost the "≤5 wounds" star without noticing (sleeping out on day 1, thicket mishap).

### 2. What each taught
- Ch1: day loop (event → plan → actions → weather → night), 1 food per person per night, 2 pawns = certain, a missing shelter = a wound.
- Ch2: wood → Build Shelter, put 2 pawns on it; rain clouds ruin food and wood.
- Ch3: tap ❔ tiles to explore, far tiles need +1 pawn, adventure cards on ❓.

### 3. Top 8 confusions
1. Every chapter opens with "CHAPTER 1 · MAROONED / The Signal on the Hill" instead of its own chapter (c1-9345/008-error.png, 101.png, 164-Start.png)
2. The map's place sheet won't let me add a 2nd pawn ("No job possible here"), but the job list does (182-236_110.png, 252-179_104.png)
3. "Find food" assigned a "Threat: Wade Out for the Crates" (023-Find_food.png)
4. Ch2 and ch3 both end with "Rescued! ... going home", and then the next chapter continues on the island (153-Continue.png, 271.png)
5. Night text says "Huddled under the shelter", but the summary says "sleeping in the open" (220.png vs 226.png)
6. "Day four" or "Day seven" in the story text vs D1 / "DAY 1 OF 12" on the HUD (101.png, 012-Continue.png)
7. The Hammer and Nails "+" on Explore does nothing (199-347_675.png)
8. Plan for me silently overwrote my own job choices and gave the reason "the computer's pick" (255, 259.png)

### 4. "What just happened?"
- Food went up just from tapping Plan for me (066).
- Food numbers didn't add up after rain (091). The 🍖 counter mixes food and dry food (133).
- Grubs, Fallen Tree and Wild Goat appeared with no explanation (072, 124, 232).
- Food spoiled overnight with no warning beforehand (133).
- The wounds star failed even though the heart showed 12/13 (272).

### 5. Unreachable/hidden
- The "Not possible yet (1)" reason is below the fold in the place sheet (185).
- The intro goal text was cut off behind Continue (009–011).
- The driver timed out on some taps (008-error, 184-error).

### 6. Want next chapter?
- After ch1: yes, the stars and the map are inviting.
- After ch2: mildly. It ended on day 2 with no real decision, and the "Rescued" ending confused me.
- After ch3: yes for Act 2's curse theme. But the repeated wrong intro and the copy-paste ending make the story feel broken.

### 7. Fun verdict: 3/5
Chapters split the rules into digestible goals and are much friendlier than being thrown into a 12-round survival game. But Plan for me wins every chapter for you, and the wrong-chapter intro and identical endings undercut the story.
