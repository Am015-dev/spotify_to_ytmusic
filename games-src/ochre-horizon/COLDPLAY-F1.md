# Cold play (F1, independent tester, verdict as-is)

Build tested: pre-fix F1 build. Fixed after it: the hint no longer says "Sell" when good plays exist, the score reads "N from rating", Done says "you go again", the research step says marked cards are our pick.

1. Could you play? Mostly. No blocker; Learn-to-play steps were clear. Gaps: "Pick your company" screen not understood; unsure when to place a tile (two-step tap then confirm); unsure what "Done" vs "Pass" really does. (Most time was lost to my own script, not the game.)
2. Fun: 3.5/5. Best: Moonlit Ledger +4 credit production, gen 7 rich; PC win 92-74. Worst: many generations of "Sell cards you cannot use" and repeating "Power plant" for 11 credits; feels grindy, the hint loops.
3. Would I play again? Probably once more, to try another company.
4. Confused by: hint "Sell cards you cannot use" with 25 credits and 16 playable cards; round-start "Buy 4 cards Pay 12 credits" with pre-marked "Keep" not explained; Tide Tax Office must come before oceans, nothing told me; gauges vs points vs rating ("You · points rating 26" next to "29"); "Try this" covers the map bottom; plants/heat are buttons, learned only from the hint; "Pass" changing to "Done turn comes back" unexplained.
5. Goal: raise heat, air and oceans to their goals and score more points than the rival by the final generation (17 rounds).
6. Dead taps ~6 (Played-tab card, closed overlays, hex before confirm appeared); lost moments ~4 (0 credits, what Awards mean, "Actions 0 ready").
7. Phone vs PC: phone tight but fully usable, 44px+ buttons, long card scrolling. PC uses the width well (hand left, rival right), readable, all clickable; map small in the middle column.
8. Results: phone (Learn, Easy/Normal) rival won 80-65 at gen 17 (rating 44 v 39, milestones 0 v 15, awards 5 v 10). PC (New game) I won 92-74 at gen 17.
