# Ochre Horizon: rules notes

Rules follow the original top-ranked terraforming card game exactly (base game with its corporate-era cards, standard 2-player
rules), written from the builder's own knowledge. Items marked [unverified] could not be checked against a rulebook in this session.

## Setup (2 players)
- Globals: temperature -30°C, oxygen 0%, oceans 0 of 9. Rating (TR) 20 each. Corporate-era play: no starting production.
- Each player is dealt 2 corporations (the beginner corporation is also offered) and 10 project cards. Keep any cards for 3
  credits each, paid from the corporation's starting credits. The beginner corporation keeps all 10 free.
- First player alternates each generation.

## A generation
1. Research (skipped in generation 1): each player sees 4 cards and buys any for 3 credits each. Draft variant (option):
   draft the 4 cards one by one, passing the rest to the rival, then buy.
2. Actions: in turn order each player takes 1 or 2 actions, then the other player; a player who passes is out until next
   generation and the other keeps taking turns. Actions: play a card; standard project; 8 plants → greenery; 8 heat → +1 temperature;
   claim a milestone (8 credits); fund an award (8/14/20); use one card action per card per generation; pass.
3. Production: all energy becomes heat; gain credits = credit production + TR; gain every other production.

## Globals and bonuses
- Temperature in 2°C steps to +8°C (19 steps), oxygen to 14%, 9 oceans. Each step raised: +1 TR for whoever raised it.
  A maxed global cannot be raised (no TR), but cards that would raise it can still be played.
- Bonus steps: -24°C and -20°C → +1 heat production; 0°C → place an ocean; 8% oxygen → +1 temperature step.

## Tiles
- Oceans only on the 12 ocean spaces. Greenery: next to one of your own tiles if possible; raises oxygen. Cities: never next to
  another city. Reserved space (Rift City Site) and the two off-map spaces only by their card. Special tiles go on any free land space unless
  the card says (volcanic, isolated).
- Placement bonuses printed on the space (steel, titanium, plants, cards) and 2 credits for each adjacent ocean.

## Cards
- Pay the cost in credits; steel pays 2 for building-tag cards, titanium 3 for space-tag cards (effects can raise those).
- Requirements (global min/max, tags, production, cities) must hold when played. Event tags count only at the moment of play.
- Negative production: you must have it (credit production floor -5). Rival attacks take what the rival has.
- Automated cards act once; active cards keep an effect or a once-per-generation action; card resources (microbes, animals,
  science, fighters) live on cards and often score VP.

## End and scoring
- When all three globals are maxed, the generation finishes (production included), then each player may convert plants into
  greenery one last time, then score: TR + milestones (5 each, max 3 claimed) + awards (leader 5; 2 players: no second place, a tie
  pays both) + 1 per greenery + 1 per greenery next to each of your cities + card VP. Tie: most credits.

## Simplified on purpose (interface only, never rules)
- Payment is chosen automatically (titanium/steel first, never overpaying beyond one unit); [the original lets you pick the mix].
- "Decrease any production" always targets the rival; the original also lets you target yourself if you want [unverified edge].
- Card effects that trigger on your own tags don't trigger on the card that creates them [unverified: believed to match the original, where only cards that say "including this" count themselves; rules-test asserts it].

## Not in this version
- Solo rules and the 3-5 player game (this is a 2-player game: you vs a computer rival). No expansion content.
