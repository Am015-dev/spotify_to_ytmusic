# Nebula Aces: tutorial coverage (the prologue "Convoy Run", one real two-round battle vs the real AI)

The tutorial is one complete short game: fixed seed, fixed dice and fixed enemy dials, played from setup to the victory card, then a real game or Story chapter 1. Four chapters, 23 steps; every `wait` step needs the player's real tap, the others advance with Next. Leave and resume: the menu offers Restart or Exit for a half-done lesson. Story beat = the bubble's reason line (speaker in brackets).

## Not covered by doing (honest)
- Barrel roll, boost, K-turn mechanics beyond Slate's turn, bumping, flying off the mat, initiative ties, torpedo, scenarios: explained in the Rules drawer ("How a turn works" first) and the in-game hint bulb, not played in the prologue.
- Chapters are not separately resumable: leaving restarts the lesson (about 5 minutes).
