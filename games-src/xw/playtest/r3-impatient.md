## Summary

**1. Goal / round, in my words.** Destroy every enemy ship. Each round: secretly pick a move on a dial (speed 1-4 plus turn; green is easy, white normal, red gives stress), then ships move from lowest to highest pilot skill. After moving, each ship takes one action (Focus, Lock, or nothing). Then ships shoot in order, highest skill first, at targets inside the front wedge at range 1-3. Hits minus evades = damage, which comes off shields (◈) first and then hull (♥).

**2. Why I won or lost damage.**
- R2: I shot Knifepoint at range 1 and got 3 hits, but it rolled 3 evades, so 0 damage. It shot back with 2 hits and my 2 defence dice were blank, so I lost 2 shields.
- R3-R6: Slate sat behind me at range 1 every round. Focus turned my defence dice into evades, so I took 0. I couldn't shoot because nobody was ever in my wedge.
- R5-R6: the suggested K-turn stressed me, so I got no action for 2 rounds.
- R8: Plasma Torpedoes (I hadn't known I had them, and I think my lock from R3 enabled them) did 3 damage and destroyed Knifepoint.
- R9: I hit Slate for 2 at range 1 (Slate 3 to 1) and took 2 back.
- R10-R14: we kept bumping and circling, and neither side did damage.

**3. Top 10 confusions**
1. Ship labels overlap and you can't see which way ships face (032, 036, 093).
2. The move preview is hidden under the name tags (039, 029).
3. The suggested moves often pointed me away from the enemy or caused bumps or stress (042, 069, 102, 120).
4. Stress is only explained after you fly the red move (051).
5. There are two focus buttons, "focus→evade" and "Spend focus", and the weaker one is starred (043, 088). That free conversion even shows up when I didn't take Focus (033).
6. Torpedoes appeared out of nowhere (074).
7. "0 hits + 2 crits = 3 damage" (076).
8. The board shrinks and grows at almost every step (009, 020, 024).
9. "Range 4+" didn't match the gap I could see, and there's no range ruler (011).
10. The dial legend ("! hits…") is cut off (015, 029).

**4. "What just happened?"**
- Focus tokens vanished at round end (012).
- Shields came back 0→1 and later 1→2 with no message (032, 069).
- The button said "Next: Knifepoint may shoots" but Slate shot instead (054). Another time it said "Next: Slate may shoot" and nothing was shown (064).
- Whole rounds resolved in one tap with nothing shown (069, 120).
- The asteroid message was cut off, and I couldn't tell if Slate took damage (113).

**5. Visible but not reachable.** I couldn't tap the ship card by its text and had to use coordinates (005). The card also moved between rounds (013). The dial legend and the bottom combat messages were cut off. On the first screen, the faction pickers sit at the bottom edge.

**6. Too much on screen.** Combat cards stack on top of each other (022, 089). The setup text is long. The top bar has 5 unlabeled icons. Clusters of ships make the board unreadable.

**7. Fun verdict: 2/5.** Destroying Knifepoint with torpedoes was a real high point. But about 10 of 14 rounds were circling with no shots, and following the starred advice often hurt me. I might press Play again only if the ships and ghosts were readable.
