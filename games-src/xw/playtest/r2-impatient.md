## Wrap-up

**1. Goal / round.** Destroy every enemy ship. Each round: secretly pick a maneuver on a dial (arrow + speed; green easy, white normal, red hard = stress), lock in; ships move from lowest pilot skill up; after moving you take one action (Focus, Lock); then combat from highest skill down: if an enemy is in your front cone at range 1-3 you roll attack dice, they roll defence dice, evades cancel hits; shields go first, then hull. Repeat.

**2. Why fights went the way they did.**
- R2 (002-020): 2 hits + crit at range 2 all cancelled. Log showed Knifepoint had focused and turned a focus into a 3rd evade. I'd never have known without the log.
- R4: 2 hits at range 3 vs Slate cancelled (range 3 = extra defence die).
- R5: 3 hits at range 3 got 2 through, Knifepoint 3->1. Slate hit me for 1 shield.
- R6: lock reroll + focus = 3 hits, all cancelled again (no defence dice shown).
- R7: range 1 gave me 4 dice, Knifepoint died. Range 1 is the trick.
- R8: Slate shot me from range 1; my pilot ability turned a focus into an evade, I lost 1 shield.
- R10: range 1, 4 dice + reroll + focus killed full-health Slate in one go. Won in 10 rounds at 3/3 hull.

Slate went from 3 hull to dead and the game cut to the victory screen (125-wait.png). "Stress" shows in summaries but was never explained.

**5. Seen but not reachable.** Pilot-ability/Done buttons in defence (below the fold). "Skip action" (y=774). The reroll checkbox list. The dial legend ("! hits ...") is cut off. My defence-dice row sits under the sticky Done button.

**6. Too much on screen.** The start screen (online, factions, three modes). The bottom panel during combat: dice, "right now" line, legend, can't-shoot note and button all in a short scroll box. The Guide toggle makes it taller. Board labels overlap ships at the edges.

**7. Fun verdict: 3/5.** Rounds 1-4 were dull, with nothing landing and dice I couldn't see. Once I learned "get to range 1, focus + lock", the reroll-then-focus chain felt great and the win was satisfying. Yes, I'd press Next sortie, but fix the off-screen buttons first.
