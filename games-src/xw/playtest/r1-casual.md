## Wrap-up

**1. Goal / round, in my words:** Destroy every enemy ship. Each round: everyone secretly picks a maneuver on a "dial" (arrows by speed); then ships move lowest-skill-first and each takes one action (Focus, Lock); then ships shoot highest-skill-first, rolling attack dice vs defence dice (crits/hits vs evades); then a round summary. Asteroids damage you and cost your action if you fly through them; shooting through them gives the defender +1 die.

**2. Top 10 confusion moments**
1. ★ Recommended U-turn flew me through an asteroid and killed me — pt/a/041-Kael_Varro.png → pt/a/049-Log.png
2. ★ Recommended bank in R3 also flew through an asteroid (lost action, no on-screen notice) — pt/a/030-Kael_Varro.png, pt/a/034-wait.png
3. Red "!" on every dial arrow, never explained — pt/a/030-Kael_Varro.png
4. "Spend focus" button hidden under the sticky "Done: take the damage" bar — pt/a/025-wait.png
5. Cockpit Fire crit card only visible in the Log — pt/a/038-Log.png
6. End screen "Iron Armada wins: every enemy ship is destroyed" reads like I won — pt/a/045-wait.png
7. Out-of-context "Planning:" tooltip popped up during Combat and ate my Done taps — pt/a/019-Done.png, pt/a/021-Done.png
8. ★ recommended far target (range 2) over close one; 2 crits still missed vs 5 defence dice — pt/a/017-Focus.png, pt/a/023-120_662.png
9. Board has no ship names/HP; can't tell Knifepoint from Slate, or my hull — pt/a/016-wait.png, pt/a/026-195_733.png
10. "Says nobody in arc" while enemies sit on the arc edge; range units unclear — pt/a/010-wait.png, pt/a/011-Focus.png

**3. "What just happened?"**
- R3: skipped from Lock straight to being shot; never got an action or my own shot (pt/a/034-wait.png).
- R3 summary 0 damage taken, but log says cockpit fire "rolls hit" (pt/a/037-wait.png vs pt/a/038-Log.png).
- R4: game simply ended during movement with no visible hit (pt/a/045-wait.png).
- Slate "may shoot" announced then silently skipped (pt/a/026 → pt/a/028-wait.png).
- Placement started at "asteroid 2 of 6" (pt/a/004-Continue.png).

**4. Too much on screen**
- Dice result panels + dense dice legend, with the key line/buttons covered by the sticky button (pt/a/018, 025).
- Round summaries' "What happened" always cut off behind the button (pt/a/012, 028, 037).
- Dial ghost fan drawn over enemy ships + asteroids (pt/a/030).

**5. Fun verdict: 2/5.** Dice rolls and the R2 swoop felt exciting, but I followed every recommendation and still died in 4 rounds having dealt 0 damage, mostly to asteroids I wasn't warned about. Would I press Play again? Maybe once, "Fly it again", out of spite — but if the stars betray me again, no.
