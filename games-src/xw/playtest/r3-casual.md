## 1. Goal and a round
Kill every enemy ship. Each round: secretly pick a maneuver ("dial") for your ship, all ships move (lowest skill first), each picks one action (Focus/Lock), then whoever has an enemy in its front cone at range 1-3 rolls attack dice vs defence dice; hits minus evades = damage to shields then hull. Red moves give stress = no action next time.

## 2. Why I won/lost damage
- R4 kill: Recommended move put Slate right in front of me at range 1 (+1 die), I had Focus, rolled 3 hits + focus -> 4 hits vs 1 evade -> Slate dead.
- R4 hit taken (3): Knifepoint was also at range 1 and fires extra dice there; I had already spent my focus on attack.
- R5/R10 hits: Knifepoint sat beside/behind me (in its arc, not mine); in R10 my red K-turn stressed me so I had no focus to dodge.
- Every other round: evades cancelled hits, or ships bumped and nobody fired.
