## Summary

**1. Goal / round.** Fly a squad of starfighters and destroy every enemy ship. Each round you secretly pick a maneuver on a dial (speed + direction; green easy / white / red stress). Then ships move from lowest to highest pilot skill, and each takes one action (Focus / Target lock). In Combat, ships with an enemy inside their front arc at range 1-3 roll attack dice against defence dice. Hits strip shields, then hull. The round ends with a summary. My battle: 1 ship (Kael Varro) vs 2 Iron Armada fighters. I was dead in 5 rounds after doing 2 damage.

**2. Top confusion moments**
1. b/003-Continue.png: "Place asteroid 2 of 6" when I had placed none; the blue squares were never explained.
2. b/014-Use_recommended.png: "Use recommended" only selects the move. You still need Set, then Lock in (3 taps for 1 ship).
3. b/022-Done.png: tapping Done popped a stray "Planning:" tooltip over the dice in mid-combat.
4. b/026-wait.png: the ★ recommended "focus→evade" button sat under the sticky Done button.
5. b/027-focus_evade.png: I had already spent focus on my attack, yet could spend it again on defence. Can't tell how many tokens I have.
6. b/036-wait.png: the recommended hard turn left me facing empty space with Knifepoint stacked on my ship.
7. b/050-wait.png: the recommended turn again pointed me at the board edge. The ship sprite and the arc seem to face different ways.
8. b/044-Lock_onto_Slate.png: no marker shows that my lock happened.
9. b/051-Focus.png: Knifepoint shoots me from beside me while I can't shoot it. The board doesn't make that clear.
10. b/053-take_the_damage.png: "Iron Armada wins: every enemy ship is destroyed". That "enemy" ship was me, which reads backwards.

**3. "What just happened?"**
- b/018-wait.png: both enemies jumped across the board while I waited. I never saw them move.
- b/024-195_667.png: 3 hits became 2 damage. The defence dice were cut off below the fold.
- b/025-Continue.png: the AI's focus turned into a hit "while modifying", with no explanation.
- b/037-Focus.png: combat was skipped with no screen. Only the summary told me about the bump.
- b/053: I died from what I thought was 1 shield + some hull. I never saw my hull or shield total on the board.

**4. Too much on screen**
- The ghost "spider-web" of maneuver previews covers my ship, the enemies and the asteroids (b/004, b/032, b/046).
- Combat panels: the dice, the legend paragraph, the sticky Done button and the result log all compete. The key "X hits get through" line ends up hidden (b/026, b/052).
- The mission briefing is a wall of lore (b/002).

**5. Fun verdict: 2/5.** The dice moment of round 2 was satisfying. The rest was 3 rounds of whiffs: I followed the game's own ★ recommendations and still had nobody in my arc, then died suddenly without ever seeing my health. I might press "Fly it again" once to see if my own steering does better, but I wouldn't come back without visible HP and better recommendations.
