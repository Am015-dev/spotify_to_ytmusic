## Wrap-up

**1. Goal / round.** Kill every enemy ship. My one heavy fighter (3 hull, 2 shields) against two light fighters. Each round: secretly pick a move on a dial (I always hit Recommended), lock it in, ships move (lowest skill first), I take one action (Focus, almost always), then everyone shoots: roll red attack dice, the defender rolls green dice, evades cancel hits, and what is left comes off shields and then hull. Then a round summary.

**2. Why damage happened.** Game 1 R2: I hit Slate at range 1. Spending focus gave me 3 hits, but it only took 1 damage. The Log told me Slate rolled 2 evades. Knifepoint did 1 to my shields; it was "obstructed" and I got an extra die from the asteroid. Game 2 R2: my 3 hits on Knifepoint all got cancelled (agility 3 = lots of evade dice). R4: I hit it for 2 (3->1). R5: missed again; Knifepoint did 2 to my shields because I could only see the pilot ability button after I found it by accident. Several rounds ended 0/0 because "Recommended" pointed me away from the enemy or into a bump.

**3. Top confusion moments**
1. Hard stuck. My defence step had no visible button because the pilot ability / Done were below the fold, with no scroll hint (r2-9403/025-wait.png, 026, 047). I had to reload and lost the game.
2. Same stuck spot again in game 2 (r2-9403/097-wait.png). I only got out by text-tapping "Change" (099-Change.png).
3. Out of nowhere, a "Planning: every pilot secretly picks..." tooltip popped up in combat when I tapped Done (018-Done.png, 101-Done.png).
4. I can never see the defender's dice. 3 hits turned into 1 or 0 damage with no explanation on screen (019-195_638.png, 066-defender_rolls.png).
5. My own defence dice are hidden under the "Done: take the damage" button (022-wait.png, 068, 085).
6. Recommended moves turned me away from the enemy, bumped me into an enemy so I lost my action, or flew me to the map edge (056, 075, 109).
7. Round 7 skipped my action with no reason given (115-wait.png).
8. Unlabelled settings: two bare "On" toggles plus "normal" and "Auto · Low" (034-362_22.png).
9. The Continue preview said "Next: Knifepoint may shoot", then Slate shot (085-wait.png).
10. Ships and labels pile on top of each other and get clipped at the map edge (062-wait.png, 115-wait.png).

**4. "What just happened?"** My shields went from 1 back to 2 between rounds with no message (091-wait.png, 109). The "!1" tag on Knifepoint only made sense later, when "cleared a stress token" appeared (111). Slate silently didn't shoot in some rounds (071, 105). 3 hits did 1 or 0 damage (019, 066, 095). Focus in round 1 left no visible token (008).

**5. Seen but not reachable.** The pilot ability ("change focus to evade") during defence (025/097). The rest of the "What happened" text on round summaries, which is cut off under the Continue button (058, 075). Setup hint text below the Auto-place button (003). "Skip action" sitting half off-screen (007).

**6. Too much on screen.** The combat panel: goal line, explanation, attack dice, defence dice, ability, Done, legend and result all stack below a fixed half-screen map. Turning on Guide adds more text and pushes the buttons further down (039). The board gets crowded when 3 ships plus labels are within range 1.

**7. Fun verdict: 2/5.** Rolling dice and spending focus feels good and the expected-damage hints are nice. But I got hard-stuck twice on my own defence roll, never saw why my hits were cancelled, and "Recommended" moves wasted about half the rounds. After 7 rounds the fight still wasn't over. I'd maybe press Play again only if the stuck-button problem was fixed.
