## Summary
1. **Goal/turn:** Be first to 20★ (or last alive). Each turn you roll 6 dice up to 3 times, keeping what you like: three-of-a-kind numbers = stars (3s=3★, 2s=2★, 1s=1★, extra dice add more), claws hit others, hearts heal (not in Downtown), lightning = energy to buy cards. One monster holds Downtown: +2★ per turn start but takes every claw and can't heal; when hit it can yield.
2. **Won** 20★ in round 10 (♥3). Stars: 3★ (3×3, R2), 2★ (3×2, R3), +1 entering Downtown (R4), +2 start in Downtown (R5), 1★ (3×1, R5), 3★ (3×3, R6), Sky Needle +4 (R8), +1 entering Downtown (R9), 3★ (3×3 via Smoke reroll, R10) = 20. Hearts: lost to Downtown holders' claws (Glacyx R1, Boltbox R4, Shroomhulk/Boltbox/Glacyx while I sat in Downtown), big −4 from Shroomhulk (unexplained bonus). Energy −2 in R5 from Glacyx's Cyclone (found only in recap line 11).
