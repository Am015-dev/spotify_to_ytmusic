# Kaiten Kitchen: rules checklist

Every rule of the original rulebook, in our own words, numbered. Each number is one assertion in `logic-test.js`
(real engine, state built, action taken through `KK.apply`, exact result checked). This file is generated by `node logic-test.js --md`.
The base game has no optional rules or variant menu; rule 81 records that.

## Setup

1. The deck is 108 cards: 14 Crispy Prawn, 14 Fish Slice, 14 Steam Bun, 12 two-icon, 8 three-icon and 6 one-icon rolls, 10 Sunset, 5 Moon, 5 Sun nigiri, 6 Fire Paste, 4 Twin Sticks, 10 Custard Cups
2. 2 diners: each is dealt 10 cards
3. 3 diners: each is dealt 9 cards
4. 4 diners: each is dealt 8 cards
5. 5 diners: each is dealt 7 cards
6. The game is played over exactly 3 rounds, then it is over

## Drafting and passing

7. Picks are secret: after one diner picks, nothing is on the table, the hand is intact and other seats see no pick
8. Picks are revealed together: when the last diner picks, every table gains exactly the card picked
9. After each turn every hand passes one seat to the left (seat i gets seat i-1's remaining cards)
10. Passing is left in every round (checked on every turn of rounds 1, 2 and 3)
11. A round lasts exactly as many turns as the hand size, then all hands are empty
12. A diner cannot pick twice in a turn, cannot pick a card they do not hold, and a bad seat is refused
13. At round end a new full hand is dealt from the deck, which is never reshuffled (5 diners use 105 cards, 3 stay in the deck)
14. Every card except Custard Cups is discarded at round end and tables are emptied; Custard Cups are kept

## Crispy Prawn (5 per pair)

15. Two Crispy Prawns score 5
16. A single Crispy Prawn scores 0
17. Three Crispy Prawns score 5 (the odd one scores nothing)
18. Four Crispy Prawns score 10

## Fish Slice (10 per three)

19. Three Fish Slices score 10
20. Two Fish Slices score 0
21. Five Fish Slices score 10 (the extra two score nothing)
22. Six Fish Slices score 20

## Steam Bun (1, 3, 6, 10, 15)

23. One to five Steam Buns score 1, 3, 6, 10, 15
24. Six or more Steam Buns still score 15

## Seaweed Roll race (6 / 3)

25. Most icons score 6, second most score 3, the rest 0
26. Cards carry 1, 2 and 3 icons and the icons add up (3+2+1 = 6 beats five 1-icon rolls)
27. A tie for most splits 6 (two tied: 3 each) and nobody scores second place
28. A tie for most among three splits 6 rounded down (2 each)
29. A tie for most among four splits 6 rounded down (1 each)
30. A tie for second splits 3 rounded down (two tied: 1 each)
31. A tie for second among three splits 3 (1 each)
32. Zero icons never score: a lone roller gets 6 and no one gets second place
33. Zero icons never score: with nobody rolling, nobody scores
34. Roll scores count only the current round (icons do not carry over)

## Nigiri

35. Sun Nigiri scores 1
36. Sunset Nigiri scores 2
37. Moon Nigiri scores 3

## Fire Paste

38. Fire Paste then nigiri: the nigiri is tripled (Sun 3, Sunset 6, Moon 9)
39. Fire Paste with no nigiri after it scores 0
40. Nigiri played BEFORE the Fire Paste is not tripled
41. Only the first nigiri after one Fire Paste is tripled; the next is worth face value
42. Two Fire Pastes then two nigiri: both nigiri are tripled
43. With two unused Fire Pastes a nigiri goes onto the oldest one
44. A nigiri played after an already-used Fire Paste is not tripled again
45. Fire Paste and nigiri taken together with Twin Sticks (either order of the pair): the nigiri is tripled
46. An unused Fire Paste does not carry into the next round
47. A tripled nigiri adds to the same round total together with sets (Fire Paste + Moon + two Prawns = 9 + 5)

## Twin Sticks

48. Twin Sticks score 0 points
49. A double pick is refused when there are no Twin Sticks on the table
50. Twin Sticks cannot be used the turn they are taken (a double pick is still refused while they are only in the hand/just picked)
51. Twin Sticks on the table allow taking two cards from the hand in one turn
52. After a double pick the Twin Sticks leave the table and go back into the hand
53. The returned Twin Sticks are passed on with the hand to the next seat
54. After using Twin Sticks the hand size stays equal to the other diners' (n-1 cards, sticks included)
55. A double pick must be two different cards in the hand
56. Twin Sticks cannot be used with only one card in hand
57. A double pick on the second-to-last turn leaves the round ending on schedule (sticks become the last card passed)
58. Twin Sticks used for two nigiri in a turn each count (Sun + Moon = 4)

## Custard Cups

59. Custard Cups score nothing in the round they are played
60. Custard Cups played in rounds 1 and 2 are still counted at the end of the game
61. 3+ diners: most Custard Cups +6, fewest -6 (3, 2, 1 -> +6, 0, -6)
62. A tie for most splits +6 rounded down (3 diners 2,2,1 -> +3, +3, -6)
63. A three-way tie for most splits +6 (4 diners 3,3,3,0 -> +2 each, -6)
64. A four-way tie for most splits +6 rounded down (5 diners 2,2,2,2,0 -> +1 each, -6)
65. A tie for fewest splits -6 (4 diners 3,2,1,1 -> +6, 0, -3, -3)
66. A three-way tie for fewest splits -6 (5 diners 4,3,0,0,0 -> +6, 0, -2, -2, -2)
67. A tie for fewest that does not divide splits rounded down (5 diners 4,4,2,1,1 -> +3,+3,0,-3,-3)
68. A diner with zero Custard Cups is "fewest" and loses 6
69. 2 diners: the diner with fewest does NOT lose points (3 vs 1 -> +6, 0)
70. 2 diners: a tie for most splits +6 with no loss (2 vs 2 -> 0, 0 since everyone is equal)
71. Everyone equal (3 diners 2,2,2): nobody scores or loses
72. Nobody has any Custard Cups: nobody scores or loses
73. Custard points are applied only at game end (not in round totals) and added to the sum of the three rounds

## Round and game scoring

74. A round total adds rolls + prawns + fish + buns + nigiri + paste bonus (Fire Paste Moon 9, 2 Prawns 5, 3 Fish 10, 2 Buns 3, 3 icons -> 6 alone)
75. Sets do not carry between rounds (a lone Prawn in round 1 and another in round 2 score nothing)
76. Game total = sum of the three round scores + custard points
77. The winner is the diner with the highest total
78. Tie on points: the diner with more Custard Cups wins
79. Tie on points and on Custard Cups: the win is shared
80. Tie on points among three: only those tied are compared on Custard Cups

## Variants

81. The base game ships no variant menus: a game given extra variant options plays by the same rules (deck, hands, rounds)
