# Kaiten Kitchen UI report

Files: `src/ui1.js` (globals, helpers, table model, live scoring hints, "what would this plate score now"), `ui2.js` (render: table, belt, dock, group pop-up), `ui3.js` (flow: new game, AI driver, pick / serve, reveal-pass-score sequence, hot-seat, save/load), `ui4.js` (one-at-a-time step cards, guide tips, round score pad, final result), `ui5.js` (drawers, start screen, events, phone mode, boot), `ui6.js` (sound map), `net.js` (online, see ONLINE-REPORT.md), `head.html`, `body.html`, `build.py` -> `kaiten.html` (+ `ui.js`, `x.js` build by-products). Tests: `lay.js`, `lay-phone.js`, `click.js`, `net/p2p-kk.js`, `net/p2p-kk-phone.js`. `shoot.js` is a screenshot helper. Engine, AI, kit, audio and shell are inlined unchanged (no engine bug found).

## What the player sees
* **The table is the board** (`#bd`): one wooden counter per diner (kit chef, name, live score bubble, gold glow while still choosing), played plates grouped by type with live hints ("1 of 2", "= 5", "2 of 3", "= 6", "waiting" for Fire Paste, "1st +6" for the roll race with the icon count in the badge, Fire Paste + nigiri shown as one combined plate "= 6"), a dashed Custard shelf per seat that keeps its count across rounds, and a covered plate (cloche) at the right end of a seat once that diner has chosen. Tap a group for details (pop-up in the dock, never over the board); tap a chef for their whole table (Diners drawer).
* **The belt** (bottom of the board): your hand rides on the conveyor strip, labels at both ends say who you pass to and who you get a hand from (arrows point the way the belt moves). Tap a plate to lift it (dock shows the enlarged plate, rule, "scores +N for you now" and why), tap it again or press Serve. A green +N badge on every plate shows what it would score right now (Settings: off; on by default). Twin Sticks: with sticks on your table and 2+ plates a "Use Twin Sticks" toggle appears, you lift two plates (numbered 1, 2), Serve both shows the combined gain. Hint button: the normal computer's pick, marked and explained.
* **Simultaneous reveal**: your plate flies to your seat under a cover, all covers lift together (kit `revealAll`, cloche sound), plates land on the counters (clink), then a packet of face-down cards slides from every seat to the next one and your belt hand slides out to the left while the new hand slides in from the right (pass sound).
* **Round end**: score pad modal, category by category (rolls with icon counts, prawns, fish, buns, nigiri, Fire Paste bonus, total) counting up with tick + coin sounds, roll-race sentence (ties explained), kit order slip with running totals, Skip counting / Next round. **Game end**: custard resolved (count, points, rule sentence), all rounds + sweets + total, winner banner with win jingle and confetti, Play again / Look at the table / Menu.
* **Modes**: guided first game (2 players, tips queue one at a time: welcome, simultaneous pick, each plate the first time it is in your hand, paste ready, sticks ready, last plate), vs computer 2-5 (level chosen per chef: easy / normal / hard), hot-seat 2-5 (pass-the-device card, hands hidden as card backs between diners), watch computers, online (ONLINE-REPORT.md), rules drawer (every plate with art and rule, scoring, sticks, custard, credits), log, Diners drawer, save / continue (localStorage in try/catch; not online), sound / music toggles, computer speed, guide full/light/off, hint toggles, speed tool (PerfHUD buttons in the menu, `?fps=1`, F9).

## Layout
* Shell: shared `shell.css/js` (bar, dock, drawers). Desktop: board left, dock right (prompt, round track, who is choosing, selected plate, Serve / Twin Sticks / Hint, scoring cheat-sheet). Tablet (<1000 px): dock under the board.
* Phone (`html.ph`, short side <= 500 or coarse <= 600, `?phone=1|0` overrides; `ph-p` / `ph-l`): the board (table + belt) is the whole left column or the full width under a 44 px icon bar; the dock is a sheet under it (height 150-206 px by screen height) or a right rail in landscape. The prompt moves into the bar in portrait. Dock panels scroll, the primary button (Serve, Continue, Take) is pinned to the bottom. Table rows shrink on short screens (rows scroll sideways instead of wrapping, hint pills stay 13 px). Targets >= 44 px, text >= 13 px. Safe-area insets are applied once (bar top, dock bottom).
* Seat arrangement: 1 column of counters when rows are >= 86 px high, else 2 (3 on very wide boards); my seat spans the full width at the bottom, others are ordered in pass order starting with the diner to my left.

## Weaknesses (honest)
* No real phone, no real ears: sounds are untuned by ear (SND_MAP in ui6.js), animations only seen in headless Chromium (reveal frames caught by screenshots only sometimes).
* Landscape phone with 4-5 diners is tight: counters scroll sideways, plates are ~26 px; the Diners drawer has the full table.
* Tablet portrait (768x1024) gives the board 52% of the screen (the shared shell caps the dock at 43% height).
* Re-rendering is signature based (unchanged seats / plates are reused) but a full table still rebuilds once per turn; jsdom is slow (15-160 s per random game).
* The belt scrolls sideways when a hand does not fit at the minimum plate width (60 px, 54 px on short screens); plate names are in the kit's 13 px chits, not enlarged except in the dock.
* Hint uses the normal AI on the player's own view, also online.

## Painted table (PixiJS) and new screens
* `src/ui7.js`: the board is painted on a PixiJS 8 canvas (`#pxc`, under the DOM). The DOM stays the layout / hit / accessibility layer: belt plates, counter groups, seats and slots are still real buttons with their badges; with `html.kkpx` their own pictures are hidden and `pxSync()` (after every `render()`, belt / counter scroll, resize) moves sprites to their boxes. Re-flow eases toward the new boxes; served plates fly to the covered slot (face down halfway, squash, under the cloche); revealed plates fly from the slot to their group and burst `+N` when the group's points rise; hands slide out left along the scrolling belt and the next hand slides in from the right; packets of backs travel seat to seat; steam over buns, flames on waiting Fire Paste, glints on paste + nigiri, sparks when covers lift. Sprites only mirror what the DOM shows, so hidden hands stay hidden.
* Renderer: WebGL, else Pixi's canvas renderer, else the plain DOM view (`?px=0` forces DOM, `?px=canvas` forces the canvas renderer). The Pixi source is stored as `<script type="text/plain">` and only run when used (jsdom never runs it).
* Graphics (Menu): Auto / High / Medium / Low. High = pixel ratio up to 2, blur filter on the steam layer, full particles; Medium = 1.5, pre-blurred sprites, half the particles; Low = 1, no particles, no filters, no counter masks, still belt, drawing only on change. Registered with PerfHUD (levels, pixel-ratio cap, auto step-down, 10 fps idle saver).
* Art: `../art/*.webp` + `manifest.json`, made by `../paint/paint.js`; `build.py` embeds them as `KK_ART` (a `<name>.png` in `../art` wins, resized). The kit draws frames, names and badges around the painted plate (`KKKit.setArt`).
* Screens: painted title (Play / Online / Resume only with a save, How to play), setup "Who is at the counter?" with four diner story cards (invite / level), Start + guided / hot-seat / watch; on phones a one-line summary with Configure (sheet). Online screen holds the old host / join block.
* Tests: `px-test.js` (WebGL High / Medium / Low, canvas renderer, hot-seat backs, DOM fallback: animations finish, every card and plate sprite ends at its button with the engine's hand / table, no blur or particles on Low, canvas not blank). click.js, lay.js, lay-phone.js and net/p2p-kk.js go through the new title / setup / Configure / Online screens.
