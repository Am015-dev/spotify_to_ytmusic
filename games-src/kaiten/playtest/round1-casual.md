## Report
### 1. Goal and what a round is (my words)
Pick one plate (card) from your hand each turn. Everyone reveals at once, then the hands pass to the next diner. Plates score in sets (prawn pairs, fish triples, buns that grow, nigiri, which Fire Paste triples, a seaweed-roll majority race). A round ends when the hands run out, and there are 3 rounds. Custard cups are kept until the end, and whoever has the most gets a bonus. Highest total wins.

### 2. Top 10 confusion moments
1. The guided game is a secondary button; the big red one skips it (003).
2. The opponent's green "waiting" plate, then a sudden +6 for her (008, 012). Fire Paste is not explained until you hit it.
3. A dark "8" tag flying near my avatar during the reveal (012).
4. A second Seaweed Roll badge shows 0 while the first showed +6 (013).
5. My score dropped 16 -> 13 without my doing anything (042), from the live roll race.
6. A tip popup covers the hand on most turns (018-033); there are too many tips.
7. "Twin Sticks" and "Use Twin Sticks" were never explained, and they scored 0 (088, 096).
8. A tip about Moon Nigiri when I held none (055).
9. Cryptic plate tags on my tray: "5 +1/2", "1 of 3", "x2 = 4", "1st +6" (033, 096).
10. The hand row scrolls sideways (a 7th card off-screen at x=417), and when it shrinks it re-centres, so taps land in the gaps (010, 088).

### 3. "What just happened?" moments
- Odile +6 from one green plate in round 1 (012).
- My score 16 -> 13, and Odile's 55 -> 52 -> 55 (042, 133, 137).
- Custard +6 to Odile appearing only on the final screen (148).
- Round-1 total briefly showed 11 for me against a breakdown that added up to 13 (it was still counting up) (052).

### 4. Too much on screen
The top status line is squeezed beside 4 icon buttons. Tips stack over the hand. My tray strip packs tiny badges with x-counts, labels and the custard box. During the reveal, avatars, flying tags and both trays animate at the same time.

### 5. Final score
I lost, 35 to 61. I understood it mostly from the score tables, which are clear: Odile collected sets (prawn pairs, fish triples, Fire Paste combos, custard), while I took the biggest single +N badge each turn. During play I did not understand the live score changes; the tables at the end of each round explained them afterwards.

### 6. Fun verdict: 3/5
Charming art and the +N badges make it easy to play without thinking. But as a skimmer I lost badly without seeing why until each round ended, and the tips, Twin Sticks and moving scores felt noisy rather than guiding.
