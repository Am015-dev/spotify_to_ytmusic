## Final report

### 1. Goal and round, in my words
Have the most points after 3 rounds. A round = 10 turns: each turn you and the opponent each pick one plate from the hand on the belt, both reveal, then hands swap. You score plates by collecting sets (prawn pairs, fish triples, growing bun chains, nigiri, a roll "race" for most roll icons). Custard cups are kept until the end of the game: most +6, fewest -6.

### 2. Top 10 confusion moments
1. A "custard" plate with a dashed outline sits on my tray from turn 1, never explained until I happened to lift a Custard Cup in round 2. (004, 005)
2. Served +5 and only gained +2 (roll race lost 3, my plate label said "tied 1st", the log said "(now 1st)"). (027)
3. Served +2 and score went DOWN by 1. (059)
4. "New: X" tips arrive late or repeat: Steam Bun tip after I already had 2 buns (018), Seaweed Roll tip "New" in rounds 2 AND 3 (038, 074), Moon Nigiri "New" in round 3 (080).
5. The tray overflows: plates shrink, then run off the right edge; custard/nigiri cut off as "cu"/"custa". (012, 015, 030, 053, 089)
6. The setup screen says "You + Odile and Kofi, 3 diners", but the guided game is 1v1 with Odile only. (003)
7. Twin Sticks: Odile had "2 plates later", then they turned up back in my hand; never told what they do. (021, 024)
8. Picked the roll the tip said had 3 icons; my tray said 2. (038, 041)
9. Custard placeholder vanished, then came back, as plates were added. (044)
10. The live score during round 3 (Odile 49) did not match the banked result (52). (095, 105)

### 3. "What just happened?" moments
- 19 -> 21 after serving a +5 bun (027). 31 -> 30 after serving a +2 bun (059). Both were the roll race moving me down a place, which only the small log line explains.
- Odile's score jumped from 49 live to 52 at the end of round 3 (105).
- Seaweed Roll plates suddenly showed 0 instead of +6 once I was already 1st (009).

### 4. Too much on screen
- The bottom panel stacks the instruction line, two score pills, a "New:" tip box, the custard line and a two-line "Last turn" log. It is cramped and the log gets cut off at the bottom (012, 038, 053).
- Tray plates carry stacked labels ("1st now +6", "x3 next nigiri", "need 2 more", "x2 = 5"), which get tiny and overlap the bell on Odile's tray.

### 5. Final score
Lost 43 to 52 (R1 21-21, R2 9-17, R3 13-14, custard 3-3 = 0 each).
- My points came from the 5-bun chain (15) in R1 and from rolls and nigiri.
- I lost ground on Crispy Prawn pairs: Odile got 10/5/5 and I got 0. Odile also won the R2 roll race (+6 vs my +3) and got a Fire Paste x3 on a nigiri.
- The custard tie wiped out the custard points.
- Several leftover picks at round ends were worth 0. I went for the biggest green number, which tends to push you into the roll race and leaves you with no pairs.

### 6. Options or buttons I couldn't reach
- Menu: the "Show speed / Test speed" row is at y=777, below the 763px screen (073). I did not try scrolling.
- The driver's tap-by-text on the title "Play" button timed out (002), but tapping by coordinates worked. This is probably a driver/locator issue, not a game issue.
- The cut-off tray plates (custard, nigiri) can't be seen without scrolling.

### 7. Fun verdict: 3/5
The green "+N right now" badges and the Hint button make it very easy to just tap and play, and the bun chain and Fire Paste combos feel good. But scores dropping after a "+" serve, an unexplained custard slot, dead last turns and a cluttered, overflowing tray kept pulling me out of it.
