## Report

### (1) Goal and what a round is, in my own words
It's a sushi drafting game for 3 players. Each turn you secretly lift one plate from the belt in front of you and Serve it to your seat. Everyone reveals at once, then the remaining plates pass to the next player (my hand went to Odile, and I got Kofi's). A round ends when the belt is empty. Then sets score: rolls are a majority race (6/3), 3 Fish Slices = 10, buns score on a 1/3/6/10/15 curve, Crispy Prawns score per pair (5), nigiri are worth 1/2/3, and Fire Paste triples your next nigiri. There are 3 rounds. Custard Cups carry across rounds and score at the very end (most +6, fewest -6). Highest total wins.

### (3) "What just happened?" moments
- Score going down mid-round (10 to 7, 10 to 6, 24 to 22): roll majority swings. I worked it out only from the tiny "1st/2nd/no rank" chips.
- Kofi's live score was 14 at the end of round 1, but the round total gave him 12. I couldn't reconcile this (maybe a last-turn tie or split).
- The final banner said Odile 45. The order slip showed her at 48 before Sweets, and custard took -3 from her. That makes sense once you read the custard line, but the drop is not animated.

### (4) Too much on screen
The three belts are dense with tiny chips (x2, "2 of 3", "= 6", "1st +6", "waiting", custard) at about 10px. Opponents' rows get cut off at the right edge. The avatar strip ("... 6 / check 12") duplicates the scores shown next to the portraits. The bell icon takes space and tells me little. In the round-end table, the whole order slip plus the category table fills the screen. That's fine, but it's long.

### (5) Final score
I scored 44: Odile 45, me 44, Kofi 30. I lost by 1 point. I mostly understood why. Odile's 5 Steam Buns (15) in round 3 and her early Fish set beat me. I lost 6+ points by not defending the roll lead in round 1. The custard +6 nearly saved me. The round-end tables made every point traceable. Mid-round, the score swings were the only opaque part.

### (6) Fun verdict: 4/5
I picked it up from the per-plate +N badges and the rule panel when you lift a plate, without any tutorial. Hate-drafting, Twin Sticks and a last-round Fish set for +10 were genuinely fun. It loses a point for unexplained mid-round score drops and a cramped, chip-heavy table.
