# Rules checklist: The Thornbound Throne (published base game, own words)

Every numbered rule below has exactly one block in `logic-test.js` (same number). Generated by `node logic-test.js --md`; rule text is the published rulebook, never the engine's own data.

Sources: the publisher's rulebook (read via a text mirror), the notes in `rules-notes.md`, and an independent web check of the setup, phase, Council, Favour and end-of-game text. Solo mode and the card-by-card powers of all 51 Kingdom Cards / 16 Tactics / 19 Site cards are only partly covered: see section L and the "Not covered" list at the end.

## A. Setup and game length
_Setup A-P of the rulebook; one setup for every player count._

1. Game length: standard 5 Rounds, short 4, extended 6.
2. The base game plays with 2, 3 or 4 players.
3. Great Road: 4 face-up Kingdom Cards; the Kingdom Deck holds the other 47 of 51 distinct cards.
4. Kingdom's Favour starts on the Harvest Field (held by nobody), full 3 uses.
5. Each player starts with 0 Influence, 0 Lore, Herald off the map, 5 Supporters on the board, Hand Size 6, no Kingdom Cards.
6. Each player has 4 Tactic Tiles, all Unexhausted.
7. Opening Hand = the Heir plus 5 cards (6 total); Deck 8; Site of Power holds 5 cards (19-card faction deck).
8. The 14 Basic cards have Strengths 0,0,1,2,3,4,5,5,5,6,7,8,9,10 in every faction.
9. Every faction's Site of Power has exactly 5 cards, each with a Lore cost, at least one an HQ.
10. The Order Track lists every player once; the first player is the Active Player.
11. Round 1 skips the Start-of-the-Year draw: the game opens with the simultaneous Bid.

## B. Start of the Year
_Round 2 onward: draw, Attrition, Order Track._

12. Start of the year: every player draws up to Hand Size.
13. Attrition: must draw from an empty Deck -> Discard Pile shuffled into a new Deck and Hand Size -1, then draw to the new size.
14. Attrition never lowers Hand Size below 3.
15. No Attrition when no draw is needed (Hand already full, Deck empty).
16. Attrition shuffles only the Discard Pile: the Lost Pile is not reshuffled.
17. Order Track: sorted by Influence, most first.
18. Order Track ties reverse the previous relative order of the tied players (two tied).
19. Order Track ties reverse the previous relative order (all four tied).

## C. Spring: bids and Kingdom Cards
_Place Bids, Resolve Bids, Great Road repopulation._

20. Bids are placed simultaneously and secretly: each player with cards chooses exactly one card from Hand.
21. Bidding Strength is the printed Strength of the Bidding card; bids resolve highest first.
22. Bid ties are resolved by Order Track position (higher first).
23. Bid option I: take a face-up Great Road card and Occupy it; the Bidding card slides under it.
24. Bid option III: take the Bidding card back into Hand.
25. Bid option II: steal an opponent's Occupied Kingdom Card when your Bidding Strength is strictly higher than the occupier's Strength.
26. A steal needs STRICTLY higher: equal Strength cannot steal.
27. After a steal the occupier returns to its owner's Hand and the thief Occupies the card with the Bidding card.
28. A player holds at most two Kingdom Cards: a third must replace one (old card discarded, its occupier returns to Hand).
29. A new Kingdom Card may replace an occupied space even while the other space is empty.
30. Repopulating after a taken card: discard the rightmost remaining card, slide the rest right, refill to 4 face-up.
31. Repopulating when nothing was taken: discard the two rightmost cards.
32. When the Kingdom Deck runs out the Kingdom Discard is shuffled into a new Kingdom Deck.
33. Taking a Kingdom Card outside the Bid step refills the Great Road without a discard.
34. A card returning to a full Hand goes on top of its Deck instead.
35. A player with no cards in Hand makes no bid and the bid step still completes.

## D. Spring: Heralds, face-down cards, Supporters

36. Heralds are placed in turn order on any Location; several Heralds may share a Location.
37. Each player places exactly one face-down card next to each of the 3 Regions (simultaneous).
38. A player with fewer than 3 cards places first, one card per Region of their choice.
39. Face-down cards are hidden from opponents until the Clash reveal.
40. Spring Action Step: Supporters can be placed from the board onto Regions, any number, any Region.
41. Action Steps run in turn order and every Action is optional.

## E. Summer: Clashes

42. The last player on the Order Track places the Clash Markers (chooses the Region order).
43. Clashes are resolved Region by Region in the marker order.
44. Clash step A: face-down cards are revealed and become Active.
45. A side's Strength is the sum of its Active cards' Strength plus 1 per Supporter in the Region.
46. A player with no Active cards still competes with Supporters.
47. The highest total Strength wins the Clash.
48. The winner chooses ONE of the two Locations of that Region (offered: exactly those two).
49. Castle (Highlands) rewards 1 Influence and a Govern with an Active or Hand card.
50. Wilderness (Highlands) rewards 1 Influence and a Journey with an Active or Hand card.
51. Harvest Field (Plateau) rewards 1 Influence and the Kingdom's Favour disc.
52. Battlefield (Plateau) rewards 2 Influence and nothing else.
53. Shrine (Lowlands) rewards 1 Influence and lets you move up to three cards to the bottom of your Deck.
54. Necropolis (Lowlands) rewards 1 Influence; shuffle Discard Pile and draw up to three from it (hand limit applies).
55. Herald Reward: if your Herald is on the chosen Location you gain 1 Influence (in addition to the Location Reward).
56. Herald Reward: also steal 1 Influence from each opponent whose Herald is on that Location.
57. No Herald Reward when your Herald stands elsewhere.
58. Stealing takes only what the victim has: Influence never goes below 0.
59. A tied Clash: tied players in turn order may play a face-down card from Hand to start a new Clash, or pass.
60. If every tied player passes, the Region ends in a tie: nobody gains rewards.
61. In the new Clash only newly played cards count (earlier cards and Supporters are ignored); players who passed still take part.
62. Deadly: all opposing Active cards in the Clash are Eliminated to the Lost Pile (and do not count for Strength).
63. Deadly cards facing each other Eliminate each other.
64. Invulnerable cards are not Eliminated by Deadly (a Deadly Heir kills the captain but not the Invulnerable follower).
65. Resilient cards that are Eliminated go to the Discard Pile instead of the Lost Pile.
66. Ambush (Day): add a face-down card from Hand; it is revealed after the Day step and counts.
67. Retreat (Day): send Active cards back to Hand.
68. Retreat (Day): the Herald and Supporters in the Region may go back to their boards.
69. Flank (Day): move the card to a different unresolved Region, where it is Active.
70. Flank cannot target a Region whose Clash is already resolved.
71. A Command is used at most once per card per Clash.
72. Day Action Step: activations run in turn order.
73. Cards stay Active after their Clash until Winter.

## F. Kingdom's Favour

74. Harvest Field: claim the Favour disc from the board at full uses.
75. Harvest Field: the Favour may be taken from its current holder.
76. Harvest Field: re-claiming a Favour you hold resets it to 3 uses.
77. Using the Favour rotates it one step (3 -> 2 -> 1) and only its holder may use it.
78. After the third use the Favour returns to the Harvest Field.
79. Nobility Favour: gain 1 Influence (or give Heirs Invulnerable for the Round).
80. Clans Favour (Day): move your Herald to a Location in the current Region and up to two Supporters from other Regions to it.
81. Uprising Favour (Spring): look at a Region's face-down cards and swap one of your face-down cards with an Occupier or a Hand card.
82. Gathering Favour (Autumn): move one Active card in the Region where your Herald stands to your Hand.

## G. Autumn: Govern, Journey, Lore, Commands

83. Govern (once per Autumn): move a Hand card showing at least 1 Vote into a Council of your choice.
84. A card with no Votes cannot Govern.
85. Govern can be done only once per Autumn.
86. Any number of cards may sit in one Council over time.
87. Journey (once per Autumn): a Hand card with Lore goes to the Lost Pile and you gain Lore equal to its Lore icons.
88. A Pathfinder that Journeys goes to the Discard Pile instead of the Lost Pile.
89. A card with no Lore cannot Journey; Journey is once per Autumn.
90. Lore may be spent on Site of Power cards: an Advanced card goes to your Hand.
91. Lore may be spent on an HQ card: it goes to your Supply (face-up), not your Hand.
92. An Advanced card bought with a full Hand goes on top of the Deck.
93. Unspent Lore is kept in your Supply.
94. Rally (Autumn): return Active cards to your Hand (the Heir rallies itself).
95. Deploy (Autumn): put a Hand card face-up (Active) next to a Region with Influence tokens on it; tokens fall off one per Winter and the card stays Active meanwhile.
96. Autumn Action Step runs in turn order.

## H. Councils

97. Council of Coin: when you claim a Herald Reward you may remove your cards here and gain Influence equal to the Votes removed.
98. Council of Whispers (Autumn): place Faction Markers equal to your Votes on Locations.
99. Council of Whispers: a Location with 4 of your markers is claimed for its Reward text only (no Influence number, no Herald Reward); the markers are removed.
100. Council of Pledges (Autumn): move Supporters from the Map and/or Lost Pile to your board, up to your Votes.
101. Council of Pledges: +1 extra Supporter if you have strictly the most Votes there.
102. Council of Pledges: no extra Supporter when Votes are tied for most.
103. Council of Pledges needs at least 1 Vote there.
104. Cards leaving a Council go to their owner's Discard Pile.
105. Castle Reward: Govern discards every OTHER card in the chosen Council (including your own).
106. Cards in a Council are not Active (they cannot Clash).

## I. Winter

107. Winter cleanup: Heralds on the Map return to their boards.
108. Winter cleanup: Supporters on the Map go to the Lost Pile.
109. Winter cleanup: Active cards without Influence tokens go to their owner's Discard Pile.
110. Winter cleanup: an Active card with an Influence token loses 1 token and stays Active.
111. Winter ends the Round: the Round Marker advances and the next Round begins (Round 2 draws and sorts).
112. The game ends after the final Round (4 / 5 / 6 by length).
113. Hand Size can never exceed 8.
114. A Hand over its Hand Size must discard down.

## J. End of the game, scoring, ties

115. The player with the most Influence in their Supply wins.
116. Site of Power empty at the end: gain 1 Influence per 2 Lore in Supply before the tally.
117. No Lore bonus while any Site of Power card remains.
118. Tiebreaker 1: the tied player holding the Kingdom's Favour wins.
119. Tiebreaker 2: with no Favour holder among the tied, the higher Order Track position wins.
120. The Favour breaks a tie even against a higher Order Track position.
121. A whole game plays through to the end with no negative Influence and every card accounted for.
122. The game does not end early when someone reaches a high Influence total: play continues to the final Round.
123. Final standings put equal-Influence players in the Favour/Order Track order (ranking list is a full ordering).

## L. Kingdom Cards, Tactics and Site cards (sampled powers)
_Not every one of the 51 Kingdom Cards / 16 Tactics / 19 Site cards has its own rule here; the ones below are the powers that change core rules._

138. Sentinel Towers: +5 to the Bidding card, +1 Influence for resolving first, and you may not steal.
139. Monarch's Seal-Ring: +1 Hand Size on acquiring; -1 when you stop occupying it.
140. Veiled Patron: absorbs one Attrition (no Hand Size loss), then is discarded.
141. Statue Wrights (Spring Action): gain 1 Influence if you have a card in a Council.
142. Buried Hall: burning an Unexhausted Tactic gives 3 Lore (1 + 2 more).
143. The Lighthouse: acquire the Site of Power card with the lowest Lore cost, then discard the Lighthouse.
144. Frozen Bastion: all opponents' total Strength in that Region is halved (rounded up).
145. Blade That Dreamed: +5 Strength in the current Clash, then it and its occupier are discarded.
146. Dead King's Lance: an Active card gains Deadly for the Clash.
147. Crown's Edict (Nobility Tactic): every opponent's Bidding Strength becomes 0 (single use).
148. Doctrine of Masonry (Nobility Tactic): Supporters give 2 Strength each for the Round and stay on the Map in Winter.
149. Midnight Pressure (Uprising Tactic): each opponent discards one card per Kingdom Card you occupy.
150. Doctrine of Tempests (Clans Tactic): its user places the Clash Markers.
151. Martial Writ (Nobility Tactic): opponents may not add cards to the locked Region in its first Clash.
152. Grand Joust: players secretly play a card; equal Strengths are discarded, highest remaining wins a Location Reward.

## M. Variants the published game ships

153. Solo mode against the Sim opponent is available (1 player).
154. Optional Advanced Setup: each player is dealt 5 Kingdom Cards, keeps 1 under a Hand card, the other 4 are shuffled back into the Kingdom Deck, then everyone redraws to Hand Size.
155. Advanced Setup mulligan: all non-Heir Hand cards go back into the Deck, which is reshuffled; you redraw to Hand Size and still hold the Heir.

## N. Further edge rules

156. Herald Reward needs the Herald on the CHOSEN Location: a Herald on the other Location of the same Region earns nothing.
157. A Herald that Retreated (sent home) before the reward earns no Herald Reward.
158. Three players, two tied for the top: only the tied players take part in the new Clash.
159. A Flanked card fights in the new Region's Clash with its Strength.
160. Council of Pledges takes Supporters from the Map (not only from the Lost Pile).
161. A bold Kingdom Card (Veiled Patron) discards the Bidding card and goes to the Supply, not the board.
162. Winning a Clash gives the Location Reward only for the CHOSEN Location: the other Location of that Region gets nothing.
163. Region reward is per Clash: a player may win several Regions in one Summer and collect each reward.

## O. The round in table order

164. One round runs in the table order: secret bids, bids resolve, Heralds, face-down cards, Spring actions, Clash Markers, Clashes (claim a Location), Autumn actions, then Winter and the next Round.
165. Autumn: the same Action Step offers both Govern (a card with Votes) and Journey (a card with Lore); each is used once.
