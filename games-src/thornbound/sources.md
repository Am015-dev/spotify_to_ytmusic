# Sources (what each confirmed)

## Primary
1. Official rulebook PDF (47 MB on BGG, ~27 pages), read via a mirror: https://bghub.org/r/the-old-king-s-crown.pdf (BGG original: https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/306501/the-old-kings-crown-rulebook - 403 to automated fetch). Downloaded to the scratchpad `okc/private/` only (NOT in repo). Confirmed: components and counts (Influence 32x1 + 16x5, Lore 20, Kingdom Cards 51, Tactics 16, Faction Markers 40, Supporters 5, decks 19), setup A-P, Advanced Setup, game lengths 4/5/6, all phases and steps, Bidding and stealing, Great Road repopulation, Herald placement and rewards, Supporters, Clash/Day/Night/tally/tie, Location reward order, Govern/Journey, Councils (3), Site of Power rules, Lore->Influence end bonus, Favour disc, Hand Size 3..8, Attrition, Commands (6), Traits (3), Archetypes (9), glossary, winning and tiebreakers.
2. Publisher card library (Angular app; card images served from cards.eerieidolgames.com/cards/tokc/en-US/...): https://library.eerieidolgames.com/products . Images (306 files) downloaded to scratchpad private and read visually. Confirmed: the 6 Location rewards (Gain 1/1/1/2/1/1 + text), all 4 factions' 19-card decks incl. Strength/archetype/votes/lore/costs/commands/text, all 16 Tactics, 4 Favour actions, all 51 Kingdom Cards, 3 Council tiles (names), 24 Threat cards, 11 Simulacrum faction card faces. NOT confirmed there: Ambition/Scheme decks, Sim rules text, Forge card contents (faces exist in the library as TOKC_FORGECARDS1-60 but were not read), Wild Kingdom content (out of scope).
3. Publisher site: https://www.eerieidolgames.com/ - confirmed 1-4 players, 60-90 min, factions descriptions, Gamefound second printing 2026, downloadable rulebooks / strategy guides / Tabletop Simulator module exist.
