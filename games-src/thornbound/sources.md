# Sources (what each confirmed)

Retrieved 2026-10-02. "Private" = read for research only; no copy is in this repo.

## Primary
1. Official rulebook PDF (47 MB on the catalogue site, ~27 pages), read via a mirror (source kept in the private research repo) (the catalogue-site original returned 403 to automated fetch). Downloaded to a private scratchpad folder only (NOT in repo). Confirmed: components and counts (Influence 32x1 + 16x5, Lore 20, Kingdom Cards 51, Tactics 16, Faction Markers 40, Supporters 5, decks 19), setup A-P, Advanced Setup, game lengths 4/5/6, all phases and steps, Bidding and stealing, Great Road repopulation, Herald placement and rewards, Supporters, Clash/Day/Night/tally/tie, Location reward order, Govern/Journey, Councils (3), Site of Power rules, Lore->Influence end bonus, Favour disc, Hand Size 3..8, Attrition, Commands (6), Traits (3), Archetypes (9), glossary, winning and tiebreakers.
2. Publisher card library (Angular app; card images served from the publisher's card host) (source kept in the private research repo). Images (306 files) downloaded to scratchpad private and read visually. Confirmed: the 6 Location rewards (Gain 1/1/1/2/1/1 + text), all 4 factions' 19-card decks incl. Strength/archetype/votes/lore/costs/commands/text, all 16 Tactics, 4 Favour actions, all 51 Kingdom Cards, 3 Council tiles (names), 24 Threat cards, 11 Sim (solo opponent) faction card faces. NOT confirmed there: Ambition/Scheme decks, Sim rules text, Forge card contents (faces exist in the library as forge-card files 1-60 but were not read), first original expansion content (out of scope).
3. Publisher site (source kept in the private research repo) - confirmed 1-4 players, 60-90 min, factions descriptions, Gamefound second printing 2026, downloadable rulebooks / strategy guides / Tabletop Simulator module exist.

## Cross-check / secondary (numbers cross-checked against the rulebook)
4. A rules-mirror site (source kept in the private research repo) - AI-readable copy of the rulebook; cross-confirmed counts (51 Kingdom Cards, 16 tactics, 32/16 Influence, 20 Lore), setup, phases, tiebreakers, hand size 3-8.
5. A board-game archive page (source kept in the private research repo) - summary: 4 seasons, 3 Regions, win = most influence after final year, tiebreakers Favour then Order Track.

7. Two review blogs (source kept in the private research repo) - 5/4/6 rounds, 6 Locations, Kingdom Card max 2, phase list, 99 medium cards total (51 Kingdom + others), 5 Site of Power cards.
