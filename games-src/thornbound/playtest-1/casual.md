Played the full guided game: 4 rounds, about 90 taps and decisions, 150 screenshots. Result: lost 12-13 to the "easy" Gilded Court after leading 10-8 going into the last round.

## 1. Goal and round, in my own words
Goal: have the most Influence (points) after round 4.
A round as I experienced it: (a) everyone secretly bids one card, and the higher bid picks a "Kingdom Card" first (a lasting power that keeps your bid card stuck under it). (b) You place your Herald on one of 6 locations. (c) You hide one card face-down at each of the 3 regions. (d) "Spring": add Supporters (+1 each). (e) The regions fight one by one, highest total wins. Between fights there are "Day" actions. (f) The winner picks one of that region's two locations for points and a bonus, and gets +1 more if their Herald is there. (g) "Autumn": optional Journey (throw a card away for Lore, which buys stronger cards) and Govern (put a card in a Council). (h) "Winter": tidy up and score.
I can explain card-vs-card fights, Heralds and claiming. I CANNOT explain Councils, Favours, Tactics, Attrition, Lost Pile vs Discard Pile, Order Track, or why cards get "Eliminated".

## 5. Fun
Partly. The title, art and especially the big card-flip clash screens (/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-spotify-to-ytmusic/5a36d2af-8697-5203-aeea-a0f2a3329615/scratchpad/playtest/casual/019-wait.png, /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-spotify-to-ytmusic/5a36d2af-8697-5203-aeea-a0f2a3329615/scratchpad/playtest/casual/022-wait.png) were the best moments: clear, dramatic, "I won!". The secret bid and the bluff idea are fun, buying the 11 card felt good, and the last round was tense.
It got boring from round 2: Day and Autumn screens with nothing I understood, and tapping "End Day actions" 3 times a round. It got frustrating at the unexplained eliminations and when following the suggestion cost me points. I led the whole game and lost 12-13 at the end. The final breakdown doesn't add up, so it felt unfair, not "I'll do better".
What would make me keep playing: (1) every elimination says who did it and why; (2) a big live score and a strength total for each clash, so I don't have to read the tiny map; (3) far fewer optional powers in the first game (cards, bids, Heralds and Supporters only); (4) suggestions I can trust, or an honest "this is risky"; (5) a final score breakdown that sums correctly. Right now I would not press "Play again" today.
