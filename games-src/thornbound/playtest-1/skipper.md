## 1. In my own words: goal and round
Goal: have the most Influence (points) after 5 rounds. I only knew this for sure at the end; the game never said "most Influence wins" on any screen I saw.
A round: (1) Bids: everyone secretly bids a hand card; highest picks a Kingdom Card (an ongoing power) from the Great Road or steals one; your bid card gets stuck under it. (2) Heralds: put your Herald on one of 6 locations; bonus if you later claim it. (3) Cards: hide one card face-down by each of 3 regions (Uplands, Tablelands, Sinks). (4) Spring: optional actions, mainly putting Supporters (+1 each) into regions, plus faction "Tactics". (5) Clashes: regions resolve one by one; before each there are optional "Day actions"; highest Strength wins, and the winner claims one of that region's 2 locations for Influence plus a bonus. (6) Autumn: more optional stuff (Journey = trade a card for Lore, spend Lore on stronger cards, Rally cards back to hand, Govern = put a card in a Council). (7) Winter: cleanup.
I could NOT explain: what eliminates cards before a clash, what Councils do, why a card shows "9 (printed 4)", what Attrition is, or why my cards sometimes show as 0 flag cards.

## 4. Too much on screen
- The tip box ("Supporters add +1 Strength...") appears in every season and comes back after "Hide". Hide it for good once tapped.
- Every Spring/Day/Autumn screen: tip box + "these actions are optional" line + suggestion paragraph + "Other actions (N)" + section heading + 2 buttons. The suggestion paragraph alone is enough; drop the "optional" line and the repeated tip.
- Dotted-underlined glossary words everywhere (region, Clash, Strength, bid shown underlined 3-4 times per screen). Underline only the first use per screen, and do not make them tap targets right next to buttons (I hit one by accident, 046).
- Card detail (009, 057): too many keywords per card.
- The Kingdom panel (049): Councils rules text does not belong there.
- The long log lines at the top ("Rooftop Knife leaves the council (to Lantern Rising's...") get cut off and mean nothing to a new player; show only events that affect me.
- Meanwhile the map is too small in most phases (003, 014) to read the numbers on it, which is the one thing I wanted to see.

## 5. Fun
- Was it fun? In bursts. The Clash reveal screens (023, 068, 071, 080) are genuinely good: big cards, numbers, a crown, a clear winner. Bidding (004, 052) is clear and a bit tense. The end screen (253) with "where the Influence came from" is great. Everything between those moments is homework.
