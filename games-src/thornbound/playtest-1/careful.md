## 1. In my own words
- **Goal:** have the most Influence (points) after the last round (4 rounds in the guided game).
- **A round:** (1) everyone secretly bids one hand card; highest bid picks/steals a Kingdom Card (a lasting power) and that bid card gets tucked under it. (2) Put your Herald on one of six locations (bonus if you win there). (3) Hide one card face-down at each of the three regions, in a fixed order Uplands -> Tablelands -> Sinks. (4) Spring: send Supporters (+1 each; they are LOST afterwards unless a Council brings them back) and use tactics. (5) Clashes: one region at a time, cards flip, "Day" abilities (Flank = move, Retreat, Deadly = kill) can change things, higher total wins and claims one of that region's two locations (Influence + a bonus like Journey/Govern/draw). (6) Autumn: Journey (throw a card away for Lore; Lore buys stronger "Site of Power" cards) and Govern (put a card's votes in a Council for a power). (7) Winter: clean up.
- **Winning a fight:** card Strength + Supporters, highest wins; but hidden abilities (Poison/Eliminate, Deadly, Retreat, Doctrine of Masonry) can swing it in ways I often could not predict.

## 6. Fun
Yes, moderately (about 3.5/5), and more fun than I expected after a rough first 10 minutes. Best decisions: where to put my strongest card relative to my Herald; whether to Flank a losing card to save another region (098); the Riverbank Raiders + Cutthroat Crew steal combo (074/085); Brine-Hardened pulling my best cards back (118-123); using Ossuary to refill for the last round; Deadly wiping out a 16-strength stack (201). I won 16-7 but honestly I can't tell how much was my play vs. an easy opponent.
