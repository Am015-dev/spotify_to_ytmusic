# Thornbound Throne: story (all text lives in game/src/story.js and campaign.json)

## Cast
- **Old Ysolde the Rune-Reader** (guide, teal): reads the heath's omens, speaks in warm, short lines. Teaches the tutorial and every Story intro.
- **Tamsin of the Gate** (rival, blue; the Gilded Court): cheerful, careless, keeps the king's gate and wants its tolls. Your first rival, in the tutorial and chapter 1.
- Later rivals (see campaign.json): Brother Fennick, Seneschal Halvard Crane, Mother Quill, Captain Rook Ashby, Sable the Masked Heir, Warden-General Ida Marsh.

## Arc in three acts
1. **Spring on the Heath** (chapters 1-4): the old king is dead; you, a Heathbound heir, learn the road: clashes, Heralds, the Great Road, Lore. Boss: the Seneschal's Toll.
2. **Summer of Knives** (chapters 5-7): three rivals at the table; Councils, the Favour, Deadly agents. Boss: Sable, the Masked Heir.
3. **The Long Winter** (chapters 8-10): the sharpest rivals, a boss rule in every crowning fight, and the Throne.

## Tutorial = the prologue (chapters of one real round)
1 The Gate Road (bid, Kingdom Card) - 2 Banners and Secrets (Herald, hidden cards) - 3 Muster the Heath (Supporters, order) - 4 The Reckoning (Clash, claim, scoring, end).
Each chapter opens with a one-line beat from Ysolde, each action gets a reply from her, and the last step is the climax: the result against Tamsin.
Quick game: one-line intro from Ysolde and a result line (win / lose / tie) on the result card.
