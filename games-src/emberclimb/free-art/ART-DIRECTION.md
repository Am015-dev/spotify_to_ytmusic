# Art direction: emberclimb (free kit, round 2)

**Style:** one family only: the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup 32x32 tile set (CC0, ~6000 hand-pixelled sprites). Classic
dark-fantasy pixel art, every sprite scaled by a whole number with nearest-neighbour so pixels stay square and crisp
(cards x8-x10, enemies and heroes fill their canvas, icons x6-x8). No smoothing, no mixed families.

**Cards (512x384):** each card = the set's own wall tiles at x4 as the backdrop (warrior: dark brick, rogue: vine brick,
automaton: metal wall, monk: marble, neutral: stone, status: catacombs), a soft glow in the hero tone (ember / venom green /
arc blue / saffron), one focal subject from the set (spell icon keyed out of its frame, weapon, item or creature), optional
two small flanking sprites, vignette. Subjects follow each card's brief (lightning, frost orbs, daggers, venom, flames...).

**Enemies / heroes:** the set's monster sprites, full-canvas, ground shadow; bosses get an ember aura.
Heroes: warrior = hell knight, rogue = hooded assassin, automaton = brass electric golem, monk = robed winged ascetic.

**Icons:** relics, potions, intents, statuses, orbs, stances are item/spell sprites from the same set; map nodes sit in a
round pixel medallion with a type-coloured rim. Frames / gems / dice / card back are pixel art drawn at 125x175 and scaled
x4 to match the sprites (frame = 5-step bevel in type colour, gold corner rivets, type motif at the top).

**Backgrounds (1080x1920):** wall and floor tiles from the set at x6 with a horizon line, props (statues, altars, trees,
fountains, campfire) and a warm/cold light pool, heavy vignette, empty centre for combatants.
