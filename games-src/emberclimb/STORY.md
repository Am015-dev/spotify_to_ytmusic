# Emberclimb story

All text lives in `src/story.js` (`window.EC_STORY`); the tutorial, Story mode and Quick game read it from there. Edit lines there only.

## Cast
- **Warden Ashgrove** (guide, portrait slot `cast-guide`): the last keeper of the Cinder Tower. Warm, dry, proud of every climber.
- **The Gatekeeper** (rival, portrait slot `cast-rival`): an iron lord who wants the summit gate sealed and the ember snuffed. He sends a
  guardian down for every chapter; climbing is a race to the top before his gate locks.
- **Climbers**: Garrick the Ember Warrior, Sable the poison Rogue, the clockwork Automaton, Brother Quill the stance Monk.

## Arc (3 acts)
1. **The Mire Road** (chapters 1-3): the ember at the summit is fading. Ashgrove trains Garrick (prologue = tutorial), beats the Gatekeeper's
   Warden Captain, and Sable joins from the reeds. The Thorn Gate tests her poison.
2. **The Ash Stair** (chapters 4-6): the Automaton wakes, Brother Quill leaves a century of meditation, and the Behemoth that drowns the
   lower mire falls. The Gatekeeper is angry now.
3. **The Cinder Gate** (chapters 7-9): the Wraith burns decks, the Hall of Gears is a long climb, and at the Cinder Gate the Gatekeeper's
   Colossus stands in the door. Win and the gate stays open forever.

## Where the story shows
- **Prologue (tutorial)**: 4 chapters, each opens with a story beat (intro step) and ends with a payoff line from Ashgrove. Every step says
  why (story) and what (action) in at most 2 lines; after each action Ashgrove reacts in a speech card (portrait + line). The ending is a
  climax: floors and gold count up, then the payoff line, then the end card "The ember lives".
- **Story mode**: every chapter has an intro scene (2-3 lines: who climbs, what is at stake, the twist rule), a win outro that leads into
  the next chapter and a loss outro with a tip; the boss speaks before the fight and after it falls.
- **Quick game**: one flavour line on the map at the start, one result line in the game's voice.
