# Onyx Atelier: published rules vs ours (W645)

Source: the publisher's English rulebook (read only, nothing copied) and a rules summary site. Our names only.
Verdict: the engine already matched every published rule. Nothing needed fixing in the engine. Six rules had no direct test, so tests
were added for them (marked TEST+). The owner's "rules are wrong" came from the old guide teaching rules out of order and with gaps;
the guide is rebuilt (see tutorial-proof.js).
Brief note: the brief said the decks show "4/3/2 face up". The rulebook says 5 Rings, 4 Brooches and 3 Tiaras, and ours matches the rulebook.
