# Onyx Atelier: screen layout to copy (structure only; our own art and names)
Reference: the best digital edition of the real gem-engine game (official PC screenshots, studied by the orchestrator).
1. A TABLE, not a form: the card market is three rows of face-up cards, each card is a real card: big cost-bonus corner top-left with the points, gem colour top-right, the painting in the middle, and the cost as coloured round chips along the bottom-left. Tier decks are shown as thick stacks at the left of each row (tap = reserve from deck).
2. Gem tokens are a stack of coloured round tokens on the right with the count beside each; tap to take; picked tokens lift. Patrons (nobles) stand as small portrait tiles at the far right.
3. Player portraits with a round score badge sit at the left edge (rival top, you bottom); tapping a portrait opens their panel (reserved cards, tokens, bonuses) with a close X >= 44 px.
4. Bottom: a curved bar showing YOUR tokens and bonuses as coloured chips with counts, and a token-limit counter (8/10).
5. Affordable cards get a bright green outline, unaffordable are dimmed; the tap on a card opens a small buy / reserve popup.
6. No text walls: the rules live in a help panel (always reachable), card details in the popup, log in a panel.
Phone: market 3 rows x 4 cards at readable size (scroll rows horizontally if needed), token stack as a horizontal strip, panels as sheets with a X.
