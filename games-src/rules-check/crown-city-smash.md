# crown-city-smash: rules check

Adapts: a 2-6 player dice-rolling monster brawl for a central contested city (6 dice, 3 rolls, first to 20 stars or last monster standing), 2016 edition, plus several of its expansions (monster evolution decks, costumes, curses with an omen die, menace tiles, cultists, a tower, a berserk die, and a keyword-card pack). The shelf's rules-notes.md says the reference for each card was an online implementation, so card numbers follow that implementation and the 2016 rulebook.

Sources checked: the English rulebook PDF from a retailer CDN (text extracted: turn order, dice faces, enter/yield text, bay rules, end of game); rules summaries on rules.dized.com and officialgamerules.org (win condition and survive-your-turn rule); a Power Up expansion review set (evolution draw rules). Engine: games-src/kot/engine.js (newGame 86-110, resolveCore 279, heartUse 326, yieldStep 373, enterStep 388, onTakeCity 463, checkWin 501-507, refill 67, sweep 615) and data.js (CARDS 3-100, BASEDECK 101). Expansion cards were NOT re-checked card by card.

WRONG 0, MISSING 1, SIMPLIFIED 6

The base game is very faithful. I compared all 64 distinct base-deck cards (66 with the 2 duplicates: 47 keep + 17 discard) by name, cost and effect against the published list from memory (confidence med-high); every cost and effect matched. Checked and matching in the engine: 6 dice and 2 rerolls (engine.js:210), set of three scores its number plus 1 per extra die (engine.js:283), energy 1 per die, hearts do nothing in the city and cap at 10 hearts (engine.js:229, 35), claws hit the other zone (engine.js:352), yield only after losing hearts and still lose them (engine.js:373), attacker takes the vacated place in the enter step (engine.js:395), +1 star on entering and +2 at start of turn in the city (engine.js:144, 463), 5-6 players open the second city space (engine.js:100) and it closes at 4 or fewer with the occupant moving to the main city only if it is empty (engine.js:497-498; matches the rulebook text), 3 face-up cards and sweep for 2 energy (engine.js:67, 615), elimination at 0 hearts, last-standing win, all-eliminated = nobody wins.

## Not verified
- All expansion content (evolution cards, 12 costumes, 24 curses and the omen die faces, menace tiles by level, cultist costs, tower levels, berserk die faces, the 24 keyword cards): the shelf follows an online implementation and I could not open the physical rules or that implementation. Treat as unverified.
- Which of the 64 base cards belong to the 2016 printing versus the earlier printing (a couple, e.g. the extra-turn-on-three-ones card, may come from an expansion); costs and effects match published cards but the origin box was not confirmed.
- Whether moving up from the second city space to the main one gives the +1 entering star (shelf: no). The rulebook text does not say.
- The exact moment the 20-star win is checked (see above), and whether a monster that is eliminated in the same turn it reaches 20 can still win via the shelf's `w_last` card (that card is original).
