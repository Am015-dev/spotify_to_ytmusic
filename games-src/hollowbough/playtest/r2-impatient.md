About 85 actions total, one full game (4 seasons).

## 1. Goal / turn
Goal: have the most points when everyone has passed. You build a city of up to 15 cards (critters + constructions); cards give printed points, some give end bonuses, plus event flags and point tokens.
A turn = one action: put a worker on a place (get resources/cards), or play a card (pay twigs/resin/pebbles/berries, or play free via a paired construction), or "Prepare" for the next season when all workers are out (workers return, +workers, green production cards pay out). After the last season you Pass.

## 2. Result
I won 46-27 vs Bramble (normal). My 46 = 26 printed card points + 6 point tokens (copied Bramble's Poultice Badger twice via Echo Mole, spending berries) + 11 prosperity bonuses (Acorn Academy +6 for common critters, Goodwife Vole +3, Gildenfern Court) + 3 event (Harvest Gathering, 4 green cards). Bramble had only 9 printed points; it got 12 from the Long Road (spots 5,4,3) and passed very early (end of my Summer) with a small city, then lost 2 more to my Mudpie Jester. Bramble led most of the middle game (13 vs 11, 23 vs 14) mostly from Long Road + tokens, then stopped scoring.

## 6. Fun verdict
3.5 / 5. Yes, I'd press Play again. Skipping the guide was fine: tapping things explains them and the glow + star suggestions carry you. Free plays, copying the rival's Badger, and dropping a Jester on Bramble were the fun moments. It loses points for the opponent's turns being a blur, the unreachable card/spot, and the normal computer giving up early so the end felt like a lap of honour.
