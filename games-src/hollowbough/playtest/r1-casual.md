## 1. Goal / turn
Build a city of up to 15 woodland cards; most points wins. A turn = one action: put a worker on a place to gather twigs/resin/pebbles/berries or cards, play a card from hand or the shared "meadow", or Prepare for the next season (workers come home, +workers, green Production cards pay out). Each player moves through Spring -> Summer -> Autumn on their own clock; when you can't/won't do more you Pass and you're "out".

## 2. Result
I won 52 - 13 vs Bramble (guided/gentle AI). My points: printed card points 22, prosperity bonuses 10, events 12, Long Road 4, tokens 4. I won mostly because Hint played for me: it filled all 15 city slots and grabbed events (score jumped 29 -> 46 at one event). Bramble went "out" in Autumn with only 1 card's worth of printed points and coasted at 13 - a very gentle opponent; the contest was never close after Summer.

## 6. Fun verdict
3.3 / 5. It's gorgeous and the guided mode + Hint + star suggestions let a skimmer finish and win, and "free card" moments felt good. But I mostly pressed Hint and watched; I couldn't tell why points happened, the opponent vanished halfway ("out"), and the win was a 52-13 blowout so there was no tension. I'd press Play again once to try without Hint, but only if the places got names and the Pass button stopped looking like the main action.
