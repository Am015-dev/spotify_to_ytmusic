## 1. Goal / turn
Build a city of up to 15 cards (critters + constructions) worth the most points by the end of Autumn. A turn = one action: either drop a worker on a place to gain resources/cards, or play one card from hand or the meadow by paying twigs/resin/pebbles/berries (or free via a linked construction). When out of workers you "Prepare" for the next season (workers come back, +workers, production cards pay out). After Autumn you pass, and the game ends when everyone has passed.

## 2. Result
I won 48-36 vs Bramble (normal). Me: 32 printed card points, 3 point tokens (Poultice Badger berries), 8 prosperity bonuses (Glowworm Playhouse +2, Elderheart Oak, Goodwife Vole...), 3 event (Harvest Gathering), 2 Long Road. Bramble: 16 printed, 12 Long Road, 4 prosperity, 3 events, 1 token. I won by stacking free critters on constructions and cheap production cards early; Bramble spent workers on the Long Road (12 pts) which made its score jump in ways I couldn't see.
