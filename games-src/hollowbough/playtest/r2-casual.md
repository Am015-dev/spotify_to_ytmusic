## 1. Goal / what a turn is
Build a woodland city of up to 15 cards worth the most points when everyone has passed. On a turn you do ONE thing: put a worker on a place to get resources/cards, play a card (pay twigs/resin/pebbles/berries or get it free), or Prepare for the next season (workers come back, you get more, green cards produce). Game runs Winter -> Spring -> Summer -> Autumn; in Autumn you can also send workers to the Long Road for points.

## 2. Result
I won 56 to 36 (Bramble, gentle computer). My 56 = 24 printed card points + 8 point tokens + 7 prosperity bonuses + 6 events + 11 Long Road. I won mostly by (a) claiming two 3-pt events early, (b) cheap free plays via Goodman/Goodwife Vole + Thistle Regent, (c) three Long Road placements (5+4+2) in Autumn. Bramble got 0 from events and only 3 from the road. Honestly, I followed the stars/red outlines almost every time - the suggestions played the game for me.

## 5. Too much on screen / hidden
- Top 1/3 of the screen is a dense icon grid; meadow 8 cards; then resources, buttons, hand. Very busy for a phone.
- Hand row clips the 7th/8th card (often the suggested one).
- Choice sheets (free-play list 6 options, Long Road spots, worker-relocate list) extend below the screen fold with no visible scroll cue.
- My city (15/15) is behind a tab; I never looked at it and didn't need to - but I also didn't realise how close to the cap I was until 14/15.
- "Pass" is a full-size button beside Hint from turn 1 - risky for a tap-happy player.

## 6. Fun verdict
3.4 / 5. The guided game is genuinely friendly: plain-English "You gain..." sheets, "suggested" stars with reasons, itemised +points toasts, and a great end-of-game breakdown. Claiming events and the twig-for-points beaver felt good. But I rarely made a real decision - stars did the thinking, the repeated trigger-order prompt is busywork, and a few score changes are unexplained. Would I press Play again? Probably yes, once, vs computer without guidance to see if I actually understand it - the 15-minute length is right.
