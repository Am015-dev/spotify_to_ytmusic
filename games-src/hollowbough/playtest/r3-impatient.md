## 1. Goal / turn
Goal: have the most points when everyone has passed, after the final season. Points come from cards you build into your city (max 15), point tokens, flag events (meet a requirement like "4 green cards"), end-game bonuses on purple cards, and the Long Road (discard cards to park a worker for points in Autumn).
A turn = one action: put a worker on a place (get resources/cards), or play one card from the meadow or hand by paying resources (or free when it "occupies" a matching building), or Prepare for the next season once all workers are out (workers come back, you get more, green cards pay out). Seasons: start -> Spring -> Summer -> Autumn (last).

## 2. Result
I won 48-43 vs Bramble (normal). Mine: 27 printed card points, 3 tokens, 5 prosperity, 6 events (two flag events, 3 each), 7 Long Road (5+2). Bramble beat me on tokens (7 vs 3) and prosperity; I won on cards and events. The free "occupy" plays (Goodwife/Goodman Vole on Bramble Allotment, Tallyshop Mouse on Cranny Market, Footsore Drifter on Spyglass Perch, Crook Shrew on Candlewick Shrine) were my biggest edge - lots of points for zero resources. ~85 actions total.
