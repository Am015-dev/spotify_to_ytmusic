## 1. Goal / turn
Goal: build a woodland city (up to 15 cards) worth the most points when everyone has passed after the last season (Autumn). Points come from card values, point tokens, end bonuses, events and the Long Road. A turn = do ONE thing: put a worker on a place to get resources/cards, pay for a card, or Prepare (bring workers home, get more workers, advance a season). Seasons: Winter -> Spring -> Summer -> Autumn, each player advances on their own.

## 2. Result
Tie, 38-38 vs Bramble (guided easy opponent). Mine: printed cards 17, tokens 8, prosperity 6, events 0, Long Road 7. Bramble: 15/12/4/0/7. I scored from Lantern Rest (play meadow cards 3 cheaper), the Goodwife+Goodman Vole pair bonus (+5), Acorn Academy bonus, Long Road twice (discarding dead cards), and stacking 3 workers on the shared Gossip Glade. I lost ground because I ran out of resources mid-game, never did events (flags/stars), and Bramble collected many point tokens and visit points.
