# Assets (Hollowbough kit)
Board pieces, icons, tokens and the drawn card art (the fallback) are generated procedurally (SVG strings, feTurbulence/feDisplacementMap filters) in kit.js, original to this project.

**Painted art (2026-10):** the card paintings (`../art/`, 47 of 48 cards, embedded by `game/build.py` as `HB_ART`), the campaign portraits, card backs, tables, title and end-screen art (`games/hollowbough/media/`) were made with Google Flow (Nano Banana) from our own prompts, with no reference to any other game, its art or its publisher. Credit line in the game's About text and in `../../audio/hollowbough/credits.html`.
Fonts: none embedded. System serif stack: Palatino Linotype, Book Antiqua, Palatino, Iowan Old Style, Hoefler Text, Georgia, DejaVu Serif, serif. No Google Fonts, no OFL file needed.
Names/art are original; no art from the original game, layout or card frame is imitated.
