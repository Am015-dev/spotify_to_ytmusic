# Meadowfold: screen layout to copy (structure only; our own art and names)
Reference: the real 2024 stacked-landscape-token + animal-card game (rulebook layout of the table, published photos of the
box contents, and reviews of its online/digital implementations). NOT the hex-tile + animal-token habitat game (that is Wildmark):
there are no tile+token pairs, no nature tokens, no per-animal scoring ladders for tile patterns.

What a player sees at the table, which is what the screen copies:
1. Your valley board fills the screen: a fixed grid of hex spaces (side A 23 hexes with the river, side B 25 hexes, islands).
   Tokens are round discs that STACK: a hex shows its whole pile with visible height (trunks under leaves, 1-3 grey, red on top of
   one token). Settled animals sit as a cube on the habitat's home hex. Legal hexes glow for the selected token; others are inert.
2. The central board: 5 pockets of 3 tokens (solo 3 pockets), plus the pouch count. Phone: along the bottom; PC: below the board.
   Taking a pocket moves its 3 tokens into your hand; place them in any order; then End turn. Undo works inside your own turn.
3. The face-up animal cards (5, solo 3) are ALWAYS visible on the table, beside the central board: each card shows the animal,
   its habitat pattern (2-4 hexes with the stacked tokens it needs and a marked home hex) and its cube track (the point values
   under the cubes; the card scores the highest uncovered value). Tap = card detail with Take.
4. Your cards in play (at most 4 unfinished, a kept spirit counts) sit in their own row next to the face-up cards, each with its
   track and a "home ready" mark when the habitat is on your board. Tap = select, then the glowing home hex.
5. Spirit card (variant): kept on turn one, shown with your cards; its bonus text replaces the track.
6. Score pills for each player at the top (tap = view that valley). Help: Rules, Hint, Menu. Panels (card list, how each
   landscape scores, totals, rules) are closable (X >= 44 px, tap outside).
7. End of game: a score table per landscape (trees, mountains, fields, buildings, river or islands, animals, spirit, total).
Phone 402x874: score pills, card strip (your cards then the 5 face-up cards, scrolls sideways), board, pocket tray.
PC 1366x768: card column on the left (face-up cards, then your cards), board in the middle, panel buttons on the right, tray below.
