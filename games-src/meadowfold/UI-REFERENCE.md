# Meadowfold: screen layout to copy (structure only; our own art and names)
Reference: the best digital edition of the real landscape-and-animals tile game (official PC screenshots, studied by the orchestrator).
1. The landscape fills the screen: your tiles form a growing hex habitat in the middle of a soft pale grid of empty hex slots; each animal slot on a tile is a round marker; matching animals on a tile show their icon.
2. Top-left: slim player strips with name, colour and a single point/leaf badge. Tap = their habitat.
3. Bottom-left: a tray with YOUR current pair: the offered tile + token (4 offers), your nature-token count, tiles left (14 ...). Tapping an offer picks tile and token; rotate by tapping the placed tile.
4. Right edge: a vertical rail of round animal icons (one per animal). Tapping one opens a card panel on the right: name, painting, the scoring ladder (2 / 5 / 9 / 13 points with the animal icons needed) and one line of rule text. Panel stays until closed (X >= 44 px).
5. Bottom-right: a big END TURN button and an undo arrow. Legal slots highlight; illegal are not tappable.
6. Scores are not shown as a spreadsheet during play; final scoring is a full panel with a table.
Phone: tray along the bottom, animal rail collapses to a row of icons that open the card panel as a sheet.
