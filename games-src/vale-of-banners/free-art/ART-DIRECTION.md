# Art direction: vale-of-banners (free kit, round 2)

**Style:** one family only: Kenney's flat isometric *Hexagon Pack*. Every estate tile is a real hex tile from that
pack (crisp rim, flat colour, small trees), so the board reads like a printed tile set, not icons on gradients.

**Tile language (readable at 120 px):**
- Buildings, keep, mine: the pack's finished medieval tiles (castle, church, lumber yard, smithy, tower, windmill, mine, house);
  Fair Green and Drovers' Inn are composed from pack objects (stalls, tavern, trees) on the pack's grass hex.
- Barge: pack hex tinted river-blue with a Kenney Pirate Pack ship.
- Pastures: plain grass hex with 2/3/4 animals (Quaternius low-poly farm animals rendered from 3/4 above to match the
  isometric angle). Goat = llama model (brown/white), goose = white chick model: the only goat/goose models available CC0.
- Lore (knowledge): all on the cream sand hex so the category is obvious at a glance, one clear pack object each;
  "Roll of the X" lore tiles show that building's own tile as an inset hex.
- Tile back: cream sand hex with a crimson pennant.

**Boards and scenes:** wooden table (Poly Haven planks, darkened), cream mats with sunken hex slots drawn in the pack's
palette (rim #966939, mat #F2E4C4). Title / map / result screens are hex islands built from the pack's own tiles on a
flat sea, with a soft vignette. Defeat = same island in ruins, desaturated.

**Palette:** pack green #27AE60, sand #F2E4C4, dirt #C0844A, river #4696D2, seat colours crimson/azure/saffron/violet.

**Not changed this round (kept from earlier kit):** portraits and boss portraits, goods tokens, worker/silver/points/die
tokens, 96 px icons, fx sprites. No CC0 medieval character family at portrait size was reachable; goods need a
food/craft pack (next round: Kenney Food Kit renders).
