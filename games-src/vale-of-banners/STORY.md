# Vale of Banners - cast and story (W379)

All names are our own. Text lives in data: `src/story.js` (tutorial prologue, Quick game lines, Story loss outros) and `campaign.json`

## Premise
The old lord of the Vale has died without heirs. By old custom, the steward who raises the finest estate by the harvest moot flies the
banner of the Vale and speaks for the valley. You arrive with a bare patch of land, a home keep and a few coins.

## Arc (3 acts)
- Prologue (tutorial, one spring): Odo teaches you the trades of the Vale while Tobin Reed races you for the spring charter. Payoff: the
  harvest moot counts both estates; Odo reacts to the win or the loss.
- Act 1 Spring (c1-c3): learn to build, sell and fill regions; Cass Ferrow wants every river landing. Boss: Cass starts with 2 extra workers.
- Act 2 Summer (c4-c6): keeps, moot halls and chains; Marla's flocks close the back lane; Lord Haskel goes first and chains his keeps.
- Act 3 Autumn (c7-c10): the full year; Haskel's rematch with extra workers, frost closes the back lane, and Ivo Grath arrives with 6 extra
  silver to buy the valley. Beat him and the banner is yours.

## Where the story shows
- Tutorial: every step has a story reason (title + 1-2 lines), the glow shows where; after the action Old Odo reacts in a portrait bubble.
  Each chapter opens with a story beat (Prologue, Market day, The keep, The moot, Harvest eve) and ends with a payoff reaction; the final
  count is the climax (totals count up, Odo's win/loss line).
- Story mode: chapter intro scene (portrait + lines, the twist rule spoken by the boss or Odo), win outro (campaign.json), loss outro
  (story.js, in the result card), boss taunt/praise.
- Quick game: one flavour line from Odo at the start; one result line in the Vale's voice (solo has its own pair).
