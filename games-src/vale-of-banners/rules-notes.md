# Vale of Banners: rules in our own words

## Confirmed (core of the base rules, kept exactly)
- 5 seasons x 5 rounds. Each round every player rolls 2 dice; turn order follows the banner track.
- A die = one action: take a tile from the market stall with that number into storage (3 spaces); build a stored tile on an
  empty space of its colour with that number, touching your land; sell all goods of that number (1 silver + points per tile);
  or take 2 workers (any die).
- Each worker changes a die by 1; 6 and 1 are neighbours. Once per turn buy one back-lane tile for 2 silver (no die).
- Tile kinds: Keep (extra action with any number), Mine (1 silver per season end), Lore (26 rule-benders), Buildings
  (8 kinds, instant effects, never two of a kind in one quarter), Pasture (score same animals in that meadow), Barge
  (take all goods from one stall + move up the turn track).
- Buildings: Moot Hall = build again at once with any number (the chain); Joiner's Yard = take a Building; Bell Chapel =
  take a Keep/Mine/Lore; Fair Green = take a Barge/Pasture; Drovers' Inn = 4 workers; Counting House = 2 silver;
  Beacon Tower = 4 points; Granary = sell one kind of goods.
- Region complete: 1/3/6/10/15/21/28/36 points for 1-8 spaces + 10/8/6/4/2 for seasons 1-5.
- Goods: 5 per season laid out, one per round goes to the stall shown by the white die. Max 3 kinds of goods held.
- End: +1 per silver, +1 per unsold goods tile, +1 per 2 workers, Lore end scrolls. Tie: more empty estate spaces, then later in turn order (W505).
- Start: 1 silver, 1-4 workers by starting order, 3 random goods, home keep on the centre space.

## Guessed / simplified (say so if the owner wants them changed)
- Stall size: 2/3/4 tiles per stall for 2/3/4 players, drawn at random from the 124 normal tiles; the back lane (4/6/8) is drawn only from the 40 black-backed tiles.
- Colour bonus: 2p 6/2, 3p 7/4/1, 4p 8/5/2.
- Sale points: (players + 1) per tile.
- Estate layout: our own map in the standard 37-space hexagon (keep 3, mine 3, lore 6, buildings 11, pasture 8, water 6).
- Lore names and effects are our own set of 26 (16 rule-benders, 10 end-game scrolls).
- A fourth goods kind that does not fit stays on the stall it came from (published rule; fixed in W437).

## Cross-check against the published rulebook (7 Nov 2026)
Checked against a rules summary of the official rulebook (rulespal.com rulebook page, officialgamerules.org) and fixed:
- Selling: 2 / 3 / 4 points per goods tile with 2 / 3 / 4 players (we had players + 1). Fixed.
- Colour bonus: first 5 / 6 / 7 (2 / 3 / 4 players), second 2 / 3 / 4, later players nothing. Fixed (we had guessed 6/2, 7/4/1, 8/5/2).
- Back lane size: 4 / 6 / 8 tiles for 2 / 3 / 4 players (we had 4 always). Stalls 2 / 3 / 4 tiles per number: matches. Fixed.
- Tie-break: fewer empty estate spaces, then the player further back on the turn track (we had the higher one). Fixed.
- Tile pool raised to 164 so 4 players never run the bag dry.
- Confirmed unchanged: 1 silver, 1-4 workers by turn order, 3 random goods at start; 2 dice + white die for goods; 2 silver back lane;
  region 1-36 by size + 10..2 by season; mines 1 silver per season end; 1 point per silver, per unsold goods, per 2 workers;
  turn order by track with the top of a stack first; the 8 building effects; max 3 kinds of goods.
- Lore tiles stay our own 26 (the published set is copyrighted text; ours bend similar kinds of rules).
- No open-source implementation of this game with a usable licence was found (search of GitHub and the web: only a closed digital
  edition, a hosted browser version and a solo module for the card game), so no third-party code is used.

## W437 follow-up: components of the original (8 Nov 2026)
- Tile mix is now the published one: 164 tiles = 26 barges, 12 mines, 16 keeps, 26 lore, 28 pastures (7 per animal kind), 56 buildings (7 per kind). Before: 22 barges, 12 keeps, 36 pastures.
- 40 tiles are black-backed (6 barges, 2 mines, 2 keeps, 6 lore, 8 pastures, 16 buildings) and feed only the back lane; the six stalls never get one.
- Animals per tile (2/2/3/3/3/4/4 per kind) is our guess; the published split was not found.
- Not confirmed, so not changed: whether leftover tiles stay on the stalls at season end; whether a meadow may hold two animal kinds.

## W505 gameplay gate (8 Nov 2026)
Read the full rulebook text again (setup, phase setup, actions, every tile, end) plus two rules pages that list a correction.
- Fixed: goods crates still on the stalls at a season's end now stay there; only the hexagon tiles are cleared and refilled.
- Fixed: tie-break is now MORE empty estate spaces, then the player later in turn order. The English rulebook says "fewest"; two rules pages
  note this is a translation error, so we follow the correction.
- Confirmed unchanged: sale 2/3/4 per crate, colour bonus 5/6/7 first and 2/3/4 second (nothing for later players), region 1-36 + 10..2,
  back lane 2 silver once per turn, barge picks any stall, a full storage forces a discard, the 8 buildings, keep = one free action.
- Still different: the original's stall spaces are colour-coded (each space shows the colour of tile it takes; in 3-player games stall 6 holds a
  keep in seasons 1, 3, 5 and a mine in seasons 2, 4). The exact colour layout was not found, so our stalls still draw at random.
