# Tidewake: rules notes (the original game + its original expansion)

Suggested original title: "Tidewake: Leviathan Reach" (theme: lantern-ship captains charting a haunted archipelago; monsters = "Leviathans"; wake tiles = "currents"). Expansion: "Tidewake: Deepwater Perils". Real names appear only in `ref`.
Our names: current tile (wake tile), leviathan (monster), deck cannon, rift gate, rogue wave, maelstrom.

## Components (base) [CONFIRMED, official rulebook text via ultraboardgames + retailer listings]
- 1 board; 56 wake tiles; 10 leviathan tiles; 8 ships (one colour each); 2 dice (1 gold, 1 blue). 2-8 players, 20-40 min.
- Wake tile: 4 wake lines linking 8 edge points (2 per side); lines may cross, movement follows a single line.
- Board: gold numbers 1-6 along top, blue numbers 1-6 along left side (dice coordinates). Short white start marks on the outside edge.
- Board grid size: CONFLICT. One source says 7x7 (49 squares), another 8x8 (6x6 sub-board usable for the classic base game), dice only reach 1-6 ("some spaces monsters cannot appear"). UNRESOLVED; see guessed section.

## Setup [CONFIRMED]
- Each player picks a ship; shuffle wake tiles; deal 3 face down to each (hand 3); rest = draw pile.
- Leviathan count at start: 2-4 players = 6; 5-6 = 5; 7-8 = 4.
- Place each: roll gold+blue; gold = column (top numbers), blue = row (left numbers); put tile face down there; if square already taken, re-roll; then rotate randomly and flip face up.
- Oldest player goes first, play to the left (clockwise). First player puts ship on any start mark; clockwise others choose; ALL ships placed before any tile is played.

## Turn (4 parts, mandatory) [CONFIRMED]
1. Roll both dice. Total 6, 7 or 8 -> monsters move (see below). (Total 6-8 of 2d6: 16/36 = 44%.)
2. Place a wake tile from hand on the open square next to your ship, any of 4 rotations. Never moved afterwards unless destroyed.
3. Move your ship along its wake to the open end. If the new tile extends any other ship's wake, that ship also moves to the end of its wake. Ships pass through each other, no collisions.
4. Draw to 3 tiles. Wake tiles destroyed by monsters go to BOTTOM of draw pile.

Placement prohibitions (unless no other legal move): connect own wake to board edge; endless loop; connect to a leviathan tile; make two ships travel the same wake in the same direction.

## Leviathan movement [CONFIRMED]
- Activated by active player's roll of 6/7/8. Process all monsters in ascending order of the rotation number in the tile's corner (next to curved arrow); tie -> the one with the gold arrow first.
- For each monster roll ONE die: 1-5 -> do what that tile's arrow for that number shows (move one square N/E/S/W, or rotate one turn in the rotation arrow's direction). 6 -> that monster does not move; instead a new leviathan is placed per setup rules (roll to place).
- Each tile has five arrows: four directions plus one rotate.
- Off the board: monster removed from play.
- Moves onto wake tile: tile removed (to bottom of draw pile), monster takes square, any ship on that tile eliminated.
- Moves adjacent to ACTIVE player's ship (square in front of the ship): ship destroyed, player eliminated (monster blocks placement). Exception: a second ship on the same side of the same tile facing same way is not auto-destroyed (monster may move before its turn).
- Moves onto another monster: stationary one destroyed (removed from board; leviathan tiles not mentioned going back).
- Minimum 3 leviathan on board: if movement leaves fewer, the NEXT player rolls to place up to 3 (setup rules), and does NOT roll for movement that turn. A monster placed on a wake tile removes it and eliminates ships on it.
- Blocked start: if a monster occupies the first playable square or leaves the board where a ship still sits at its start, that player may relocate to any other start mark on the same edge with no tile there.

## Elimination / end [CONFIRMED]
- Eliminated: open wake end reaches board edge or a leviathan tile; destroyed by monster; ship on a removed tile.
- Elimination bonus: when a placed tile eliminates opponents, active player may swap any of own hand tiles for the same number from the eliminated players' hands; the rest go to bottom of draw pile; still draws up to 3.
- Last ship wins. If two or more exit on placement of the last available square, they all win.
- Optional: No Leviathan variant (all else same).
- Official variants (original publisher's variants page, via search snippets only): SOLO: set up as 2 players (6 leviathan), keep min 3; goal survive longer than monsters, i.e. get through all 10 leviathan (removed monsters stay out); easy solo: survive through all wake tiles, destroyed tiles are discarded not recycled; a solo variant adds cannon + portal tiles. TEAMS: play tile in front of own or a teammate's ship; teammates may not show tiles; last team standing wins. 2-player is just the normal game (setup as 2-4 players).

## the original expansion [CONFIRMED, official expansion rules text via ultraboardgames]
Requires the base game; all rules optional by agreement; each piece can be used separately. Contents: 1 Rift Gate, 2 Rogue Wave (gold, blue), 1 Maelstrom, 5 Deck Cannon = 9 tiles. (Not captains/rocks/other promo pieces.)
- Rift Gate (1): shuffled into wake deck. Holder may play it instead of a wake tile, or whenever facing elimination, even on another's turn (then holds only 2 tiles until end of own turn; hand refills only at end of own turn). Placed like a wake tile, does not replace existing tiles. Ships on it are moved: each separately in play order, active player last, then leviathan (active player rolls). Each affected player rolls 2 dice for coordinates (setup rule). If no wake tile at target, owner places a tile from hand and chooses which wake and direction; if a tile exists, chooses which wake and direction; ship must then move to end of wake; may not share a wake in the same direction as another ship (opposite OK). Portal stays all game, transports ships and monsters only, works even when covered; can share a square with tsunami/whirlpool and rescue ships before they meet them.
- Rogue Wave (2): gold tile shuffled into leviathan deck (can be drawn at setup); blue is set aside. When drawn: placed with standard monster placement, spun, flipped; its "2" arrow shows travel direction. Its "row" = every square to the left and right of the tile relative to its facing (the line across the board), plus its own. Blue tile put on the board edge the gold 2 faces, its blue 2 pointing at the gold 2, as marker. Any time the active player's ship is in the tsunami row (by own move or because the wave moved) roll one die; must equal or beat the strength on the active arrow or the ship capsizes (eliminated). Starts at 2. Also when a ship is set moving by another's tile into/through the row, roll immediately. Wave moves once per game round: before the placer's NEXT turn (round = everyone has gone once): move gold one square toward blue marker, rotate gold to show "3" and rotate blue to match. Rounds 2-3 on board strength 3; from the fourth round strength 4, stays until it leaves. Moves over tiles without destroying them; only ships affected; ships may end on its square. When gold moves off the edge, both tiles are removed from the game (not back to monster deck).
- Maelstrom (1, whirlpool): shuffled into leviathan deck, placed like a monster. Moves only on turns when the monsters do NOT move (the 6/7/8 roll failed): roll one die, compare to its arrows; move one square; 5 or 6 = no move. Anything in the destination (wake tile + ships on it, monsters) is removed; a monster moving into its square is removed too. Off board -> removed from game. Maelstrom and tsunami do NOT count towards the minimum 3 leviathan.
- Deck Cannon (5, cannon): shuffled into wake deck. On draw: keep, or show everyone and discard (removed from game) and draw a replacement. Hand limit 2 cannons. Play any time a ship is about to be eliminated by a leviathan (even another's turn, same 2-tile-hand caveat) to destroy that monster; or instead of a wake tile to remove a monster adjacent to own wake. Cannon removed from game; the monster goes to bottom of leviathan deck.
