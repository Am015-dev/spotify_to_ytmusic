## Final report

### 1. Goal and one round, in my own words
You're a little ship (a junk) on a 6x6 sea. Last ship still floating wins. On your turn you pick one of 3 current tiles, rotate it, and lay it in the square in front of your ship. Your ship then follows the line on the tile until it reaches an empty square. Sailing off the edge, into a serpent, or into another ship sinks you. Each turn starts with two dice. A total of 6, 7 or 8 makes every serpent move (a 6 on its own die summons a new one). Serpents smash tiles they land on, and a serpent in front of a ship can trap it.

### 5. Fun verdict: 2.5/5
The tile choice with green-check/red-X preview and the golden path line is satisfying, and rotating a doomed hand into a safe one was a real "aha". But I only made 2 decisions in the whole game, most of the time was spent watching serpents and reading text, and I won without understanding why. Would I press Play again? Yes, once, because the game was short and I want to understand what killed Cobalt. If the next game felt as passive, I'd stop.
