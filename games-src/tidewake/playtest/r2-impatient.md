## Final report

### 1. Goal and one round, in my words
Be the last junk (ship) still afloat. On each captain's turn: they roll two dice; on some totals (6, 7, 8) the leviathans "stir" and each one rolls to move a square, turn, rise as a new monster, smash tiles or eat other monsters. Then the captain picks one of three current tiles, can rotate it, and lays it in front of their ship; the ship follows the current line until it stops. You sink if you sail off the edge, if a leviathan moves onto your square, or if a leviathan sits on the square in front of you when your turn starts.

### 2. Why I lost, and why each other ship sank
- Game 1: I sank on turn 6 because the leviathan Stormcoil ended up on the square directly in front of my ship; at the start of my turn that counts as being "blocked in" and you sink automatically. I'd only laid one tile. Cobalt sank the same way (Dredgeback in front of it). Jade won by surviving.
- Game 2: Jade was crushed when Saltshade swam onto its square during Cobalt's turn. I was crushed by Saltshade on my own dice roll (it moved south onto me) before I could lay a tile. My last tile choice had forced me next to monsters (every safe orientation led to col5 row2/row3). Cobalt won by surviving.
Both losses felt like dice, not decisions.
