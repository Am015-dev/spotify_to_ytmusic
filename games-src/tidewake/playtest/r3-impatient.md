## 1. Goal and one round, in my own words
Goal: be the last junk (ship) still afloat. A ship sinks if its path runs off the 6x6 edge or into a leviathan.
On my turn I roll 2 dice. The total only matters for the monsters: on 6, 7 or 8 "the leviathans stir" and some of them move, turn, swim off, smash tiles, or a new one rises. Then I pick 1 of 3 current tiles, can turn it, and place it in front of my ship. My ship (and anyone else whose path touches it) sails along the currents until it hits an empty square. The ✓ / ! / X badges tell me whether the route is safe, could be hit by a monster next roll, or sinks me.

## 2. Why I won or lost, and why each other ship sank
- Game 1 LOSS (turn 20): I took "✓ safe" tiles that walked me along the right edge into the bottom-right corner, pointing outward. Then two new leviathans rose and a monster tore up the tile next to me, so every tile and rotation sent me off the edge or into Brinemaw.
- Game 1 Jade sank on turn 16 by sailing off the edge on its own turn. Cobalt won.
- Game 2 WIN (turn 23): I deliberately picked tiles whose gold line looped back into the middle.
- Game 2 Cobalt sank on Jade's turn (turn 6). I think Jade's tile extended Cobalt's path off the left edge after a leviathan smashed the tile at column 1, row 3.
- Game 2 Jade sailed off the top edge at column 3 on its own turn 23, so I was last afloat. Honestly I won by the AI's mistake, not by my own strategy: my ship was also parked against the edge at column 6, row 2.
