# Final report

**Games played:** 2. Game 1 = Guided first game vs 1 easy computer (Cobalt): **won** on turn 22. Game 2 = "Play now" with default settings (again just me vs Cobalt): **lost** on turn 6. About 60 driver actions total.

## 1. Goal and what one turn is like
The goal is to be the last ship (junk) still afloat. You sail one junk on a 6x6 sea. On your turn the game rolls two dice for you. If the total is 6, 7 or 8, every sea serpent (leviathan) moves on its own die roll: it moves, turns, eats the current tile it lands on, swims off the board, or stays put while a new serpent appears. After that you get 3 current tiles. Each one has a ✓ (safe) or ✗ (sinks). You can rotate them with Turn, then Place one on the square in front of your junk. Your junk follows the line on the tile until it stops. You sink if you sail off the edge, sail into a serpent, a serpent swims onto your square or tile, or you collide with another junk. Then the computer takes its turn the same way.

## 2. Why I won or lost, and why the other ship sank
- **Game 1 (win):** Cobalt's own route ran off the edge of the board, so it sank ("its wake ran off the edge of the chart"). I didn't do anything clever. On every turn I picked a ✓ tile, and when all three were ✗ I pressed Turn until one became ✓. I won because Cobalt made a mistake.
- **Game 2 (loss):** I started on the left edge with serpents all around me. On turn 4 I had only one ✓ tile, and it left me right next to two serpents. On the next roll of 6-8, Stormcoil rolled "moves east", smashed the tile I was on at column 2, row 5, and I sank. A ✓ only means the route is safe right now. It says nothing about the serpents' next move, so a newcomer can't see this risk coming.

4. **"Cobalt (2 afloat)" made me think Cobalt had 2 ships.** It's actually the total number of ships afloat, tacked onto the last name. r2-9333/004-Best_start.png, r2-9333/084-wait.png
5. **"Your turn" with only a Skip button and no tiles**, and the dice rolled by themselves. I didn't know what I was supposed to do. r2-9333/004-Best_start.png
6. **Turn rotates all three tiles, not just the selected one.** One press turned all of them from ✗ to ✓. r2-9333/058-93_533.png

## 6. Fun verdict: 2.5 / 5
The look and mood are great: the parchment sea, serpents, a clear "Sunk!" card with the reason, and a nice Victory/Defeat summary. But as a casual player I never really made a choice. I tapped whichever tile had the ✓, and sometimes there was only one. I won because the computer sailed off the edge, and I lost to a serpent die roll I couldn't see coming or avoid. Most of my time went to tapping Continue and waiting for serpent animations. Would I press Play again? Maybe once, using "Rewind to my last move" to see whether I could have survived. I wouldn't keep playing unless I could see which squares serpents might move to next.
