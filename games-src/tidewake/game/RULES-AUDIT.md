# Tidewake rules audit

Sources: SRC-B = research/tsuro-of-the-seas.php.txt (base rulebook text), SRC-V = research/veterans-of-the-seas.php.txt (expansion), NOTES = rules-notes.md. "(memory)" = recalled, not in the sources. Probes: scratchpad/audit-test.js (uses the rules-test rig; engine untouched).
Totals: 1 wrong, 1 missing, 9 ambiguous (2 of them high-impact design calls). No other rule contradicts the sources.

## Findings

1. **Maelstrom-destroyed tiles are recycled** (WRONG, medium). SRC-V: a wake tile the whirlpool enters is removed from the game, with its ships (leviathans return tiles to the bottom of the pile; the whirlpool does not). Engine: `AG.arrFinal` calls `destroyTile` for every mover, so the Maelstrom's tile goes to the bottom of the draw pile. Confirmed by probe: board tile gone, `deck` +1, tile is last card, `gone` empty. Fix: in `arrFinal`, if `a.k==='M'` clear `bd` and push the card to `G.gone` instead of `tileToDeck` (the invariant counts `gone`, so it stays valid). Same path covers a Maelstrom that spawns on a tile. Drawer text (texts.js) already says only "destroys whatever it enters"; add "tiles it destroys leave the game".
   **Status: FIXED. `destroyTile(x,y,gone)`; `arrFinal` passes `a.k==='M'` so Maelstrom-destroyed tiles go to `G.gone`. The expansion text lists only tile, ships on it, and a leviathan in the square (leviathan -> `mgone`, already so) as removed; nothing else. Test: FIX1.**

2. **Board size 6x6** (AMBIGUOUS, high design impact). NOTES: 7x7 vs 8x8 unresolved; dice reach only 1-6. (Memory, low confidence: the real board is larger than the 6x6 dice grid, with dice hitting an inner area.) Consequences already visible in ENGINE-REPORT: 56 currents on 36 squares, "last available square" shared win and easy-solo "play every tile" are unreachable, solo win rate 3-6%. Fix options: keep 6x6 (documented in the drawer's guess list) or set `BW=8` and offset dice by +1 in `rollSq`/`gateShip` (`x=d6-1` is hard-coded in both, so `BW` alone is not enough).
   **Status: DECIDED (planner): keep 6x6 (rulebook: gold die on top numbers, blue die on the left numbers). Listed as our guess in the drawer.**

3. **Easy solo parameters** (AMBIGUOUS, medium). NOTES (snippet-only source): solo = set up as 2 players (6 leviathans); easy solo = survive through all wake tiles, destroyed tiles discarded; no start count given. Engine: easy solo starts with 4 leviathans (`soloLev` default) and ends at 24 turns or pile exhausted. Both are inventions, the 24 forced by finding 2. Drawer states the 24 but not the 4. Fix: say "4 leviathans" in the drawer, or use 6 like solo.
   **Status: DECIDED (planner): keep 4 starting leviathans and the 24-turn goal; stated as OUR variant (TEXT-PATCH.md). Test: FIX3.**

4. **Setup count with Wave/Maelstrom in the deck** (AMBIGUOUS, medium). SRC-B: place 6/5/4 daikaiju tiles drawn from the shuffled pile; SRC-V: Wave and Whirlpool are shuffled into that pile and "can be drawn during set-up". Engine draws until N true leviathans are down, so the extras come on top: probe, 4 players, all expansions: 63/200 games start with Wave+Maelstrom plus 6 leviathans, 46 with only the Maelstrom, 45 with only the Wave. A literal reading counts every drawn tile toward N (then a game can start with fewer than 3 leviathans, which min-3 only fixes after a move). Decision for planner; either way document it.
   **Status: FIXED (literal rule). `setupMon` counts every drawn tile, Rogue Wave and Maelstrom included, toward 6/5/4. A start with fewer than 3 leviathans is repaired by the min-3 refill after the first move. Test: FIX4.**

5. **Forced same-wake collision** (AMBIGUOUS, low). SRC-B: ships "pass each other and do not collide"; the only rule is the placement prohibition (same wake, same direction). The sources never say what happens when forced. Engine: both sink (classic-Tsuro behaviour, memory). Documented in the drawer. Head-on links: ships follow each other's wake back to the start edge and die by edge, which is the straight reading of SRC-B. Fine as is.
   **Status: KEPT (best reading): the rulebook only forbids placing onto an occupied wake/direction; forced collision stays 'both sink'. Documented in the drawer.**

6. **Which squares are "adjacent to the active ship"** (AMBIGUOUS, low). SRC-B: a leviathan moving adjacent to the active ship kills it because it blocks tile placement. Engine: only the front square counts (kill at turn start after the roll, or on arrival if the ship stands on that tile); a leviathan beside the tile but not in front is harmless (probe confirmed). Reasonable, but the literal text could also mean any side. Same for the own-turn Deck Cannon: SRC-V says a leviathan "adjacent to his wake"; engine allows only neighbours of the front square or of the ship's current tile, not the whole wake.
   **Status: KEPT (best reading): the rulebook's reason for the kill is that the leviathan blocks tile placement, i.e. the front square; cannon 'adjacent to his wake' kept as neighbours of the front square / current tile.**

7. **Rift Gate interrupt placement and spawn on a gate square** (AMBIGUOUS, low). SRC-V gives no square for an out-of-turn gate; engine uses a free orthogonal neighbour of the doomed ship (documented). SRC-V also says the gate transports leviathans that land in it and may share a square with the wave/whirlpool. Engine: a moving leviathan or Maelstrom entering a gate is thrown (correct for leviathans; Maelstrom is not a "daikaiju" in SRC-V, so arguable), but dice placements of leviathans, Wave and Maelstrom re-roll gate squares instead (probe: 0/300 land on the gate). Harmless approximation; MISSING only in the literal sense (no "newly placed leviathan is carried through the gate", no wave/whirlpool sharing the gate square).
   **Status: KEPT (best reading): the text names no square for an out-of-turn gate, so a free neighbour is used; dice placements skip gate squares (harmless approximation). Noted in ENGINE-REPORT.**

8. **Rogue Wave roll timing edge cases** (AMBIGUOUS, low). SRC-V: the active player's ship in the row rolls "any time", by own move or wave movement; ships put in motion by others' tiles roll at once. Engine rolls (a) when the wave moves, for the placer only, (b) when any ship's path touches the row. Not rolled: a wave that spawns onto the active ship's row if the ship then leaves the row; other ships already in the new row when it moves (SRC-V says "players in or entering the new row", which could mean all). If the wave's owner is dead its slot still moves the wave but the next alive player rolls nothing even if now in the row (`turnStart` resets `waved`). Strength 2 -> 3 -> 3 -> 4, row perpendicular to travel, off-edge removal all verified.
   **Status: KEPT (best reading): the text speaks of 'the active player's ship' rolling, which the engine does (wave move, path touching the row); other ships rely on the 'put in motion by another player's tile' clause.**

9. **Maelstrom on a no-roll turn** (AMBIGUOUS, low). SRC-V: it moves when the 6/7/8 check fails. On a min-3 refill turn nobody rolls, so engine does not move it. Defensible; note it.
   **Status: KEPT (best reading): the Maelstrom moves 'when the 6/7/8 check fails'; a refill turn has no roll, so it does not move. Noted.**

10. **Teams with an odd number of players** (AMBIGUOUS, low). NOTES (snippet-only): teams = play a tile in front of own or teammate's ship, last team standing. Team sizes unknown (memory: 4/6/8, two teams). Engine accepts 5 and 7 (3v2, 4v3), which is lopsided. Fix: restrict teams to even counts, or leave and say so.
   **Status: FIXED (planner: 4, 6 or 8 only). `newGame` coerces teams to 4/6/8 (2,3->4; 5->4; 7->6). UI wording in TEXT-PATCH.md. Test: FIX5.**

11. **Cannon over the limit** (AMBIGUOUS, trivial). SRC-V: hand limit 2 cannons; says nothing about a third draw. Engine silently shows and discards a third cannon and draws on (probe: hand 57,58 + draw 59 -> 59 gone, replacement drawn). Sensible.
   **Status: KEPT: third cannon is shown and discarded, replacement drawn (the rulebook is silent on a third draw).**

## Verified correct (engine + probes/rules-test)
- Setup: leviathans 6/6/6/5/5/4/4 for 2-8 players (probe, all seven counts); hand of 3 each (2-8); deck sizes consistent; ships on edge marks before any tile; placing by gold=column, blue=row with re-roll on occupied square; random facing.
- Turn order (roll, place, move, draw), roll skipped on refill turn; 6/7/8 trigger; leviathans move in corner-number order, gold arrow first on ties, one die each, 1-5 arrow, 6 = stays and a new one is placed; spawn onto a current removes it and sinks ships.
- Off-board removed for good; moving onto a tile destroys it (to the bottom of the pile) and sinks ships on it; moving onto a stationary leviathan destroys that one; second ship on same side is not auto-killed (only the active ship is checked); blocked start may relocate along the same edge.
- Min-3: counts leviathans only (Wave/Maelstrom excluded); next player skips the roll and places up to 3.
- Path following through chained tiles; every ship whose front is the new square moves; edge or leviathan end = elimination; own-edge/loop/leviathan/same-wake placements offered only when nothing else is legal.
- Elimination bonus: swap offered only when a placement eliminates opponents; unswapped tiles to the bottom of the pile; still draws to 3; hands of ships killed by leviathans go to the pile bottom without a bonus (probe).
- End: last junk wins; ships lost in the same step share the win (probes: 2p both sink -> both win; 3p active+one sink -> third wins).
- Solo: 6 leviathans, win when every leviathan is gone; easy solo discards destroyed tiles; teams place in front of teammates, no bonus from teammates' hands.
- Rift Gate: shuffled in, play instead of a tile or when facing elimination (out of turn, no refill until own turn end), does not replace tiles, stays all game, ships move in play order with the active player last, 2-dice target, place tile or choose wake and direction, no shared wake in the same direction.
- Rogue Wave: placed as a leviathan, 2 arrow = travel, strength 2/3/3/4 by round (probe), moves before the placer's next turn, harmless to tiles, leaves play off the edge (not back to deck), not counted in min-3.
- Maelstrom: moves only on failed 6/7/8, 5-6 stay, removes tile+ships+leviathan in destination, a leviathan entering it is removed, off-board removed for good, not counted in min-3 (except finding 1).
- Deck Cannon: shuffled in, keep or show-and-discard on draw (removed from game, replacement drawn), limit 2, interrupt on any leviathan kill even on others' turns, own-turn instead of a tile, cannon leaves the game, leviathan goes to the bottom of its pile; cannot hit Wave/Maelstrom.
