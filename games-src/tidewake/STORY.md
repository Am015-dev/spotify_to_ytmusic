# Tidewake: cast and story arc

All story text is data: Story mode in `campaign.json` (cast, acts, 10 chapters with intro and win/loss outro, three bosses); the tutorial prologue in `game/texts.js` (`TUT_TEXT`).
Original names only.

Cast: Grandmother Osk (lantern keeper, guide), Wick (cheerful, careless rival), Gull Harrow (boss, act 1 toll-taker), Brann Ashkeel (cannon-smith), Saltshade, Sabel Riftwright (boss, act 2),
Corsair Vey, The Abyssal Crown, Admiral Ysolde (boss, act 3). Tutorial rival: Cobalt, the cocky newcomer who claims the Lantern Reach.

Arc: Act 1 Calm Shallows (chapters 1-3): learn to lay currents; Harrow's Toll closes the act. Act 2 Open Water (4-6): Deck Cannons, Rift Gates, the long watch; the Riftwright closes it.
Act 3 The Deep Reach (7-10): Rogue Wave, Maelstrom, the hunt for the Crown, the Drowned Admiral. Prologue: "The Lantern Race" (the tutorial): Cobalt vs you under Osk's eye; you win the lantern, Story begins.
Quick game: the table banner "Your turn" and the result card in the game's voice.
