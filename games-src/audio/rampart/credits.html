Audio

Music was made for this game with Treblo (our own prompts). Sound effects are public-domain (CC0):

  • Music: ten instrumental tracks generated with Treblo (treblo.com)
  • Sound effects: Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Music Jingles, RPG Audio, UI Audio by Kenney (CC0)

All sounds were trimmed, loudness-normalised and converted to MP3 for this game.
