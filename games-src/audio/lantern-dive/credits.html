Audio

Music was made for this game with Treblo (our own prompts). Ambience and sound effects are public-domain (CC0):

  • Ambience: “Underwater Ambient Pad” by isaiah658 (OpenGameArt, CC0)
  • Sound effects: Casino Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Music Jingles, UI Audio by Kenney (CC0)

All sounds were trimmed, loudness-normalised and converted to MP3 for this game. Names, card text and art are original.
