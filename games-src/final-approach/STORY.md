## Prologue (the tutorial)
Check ride at Port Alder. Voss has flown five rounds; Marlow takes the last two with Ravi. Pell listens on the radio. Ch 1 Your first turn (axis), ch 2 Clear the way
(radio, engines, coffee), ch 3 Landing day (reroll, brakes, gear, level, flaps, last thrust). Climax: the landing checklist goes green and Pell says "...Passed."

## Three acts (Story mode, 15 chapters)
1. **Flight school** (ch 1-6): one control per lesson, Port Alder, the check ride with Voss as boss.
2. **Green Wings** (ch 7-9): routine landings with traffic, fuel and corridor tabs; the Foxmere night queue; the long left turn with Vance.
3. **The List** (ch 10-15): crew cards, fog and a trainee, the mountain bowl with Sted, storms between towers, a silent tower in warm rain, and Cloudspire: eight names on
   Kade's list, and the player may become the ninth.

Each chapter has an intro scene (guide, Ravi and the tower, who is at stake and the twist rule), an outro (win), and on the result card the tower's praise (win) or
taunt (loss). Quick game: the flight card gives a one-line flavour intro; the banner and captions use the crew's voice ("Ravi used Flaps 1: Extends a flap.").
