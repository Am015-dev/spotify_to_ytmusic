## Summary

1. Goal / round: You and a computer co-pilot (Ravi) fly an airliner down 7 altitude rows to land. Each round you can "say" one canned phrase (no numbers), both roll 4 hidden dice, then alternate placing them on cockpit controls: Axis (both must, tilt = difference, 3 = spin/crash), Engines (both must, sum vs. markers = move 0/1/2 spaces), Radio (clears traffic planes N-1 spaces ahead), Gear/Flaps (raise the speed markers), Brakes (2,4,6 in order), Coffee (earn +/-1 tokens). To win you must be at the airport with clear track, gear+flaps down, level, slow enough.
2. Result: LOST in game 1. The plane was still 1 space short of the airport when round 7 (the last altitude) started. I thought "Land in round 7" meant I had until round 7 to get there and moved too slowly (two rounds of 0 movement because Ravi kept putting 1s on Engine and I followed the Coffee hints). Flaps were also only 2/4. Game 2 started the same way (identical dice) and I stopped after round 1.
3. Top 10 confusion moments:
   1. Loss rule only explained at the end: "must be on the airport space when the last altitude starts" while the banner said "Land in round 7" all game (t9343/104.png, t9343/097-211_429.png).
   2. How to play says "By the END of the last round the plane must be on the airport", which contradicts the loss screen (t9343/110-314_22.png).
   3. "Airport N spaces ahead · M more moves" numbers don't match and I never understood them: 5/4, 2/1, 1/0, 4/5 (t9343/085-112_213.png, t9343/119-112_213.png).
   4. Guided tip boxes hide the Landing checklist, which is the most useful thing on screen. I only saw it in game 2 with tips off (t9343/115.png).
   5. Tip 2 says "tap a phrase", but the phrases are hidden behind the tip box (t9343/006-Got_it.png).
   6. The tutorial told me to put a die on the Axis, and I put a 6. No warning that the difference with Ravi's die matters, and I was instantly at tilt 2 of 3 (t9343/022-123_263.png).
   7. Tips come out of order and too late: Engines tip in round 2, Coffee/Concentration in round 5, numbering skips Control 3 (t9343/033-28_263.png, t9343/076.png).
   8. Once a die covers Axis C/Engine C you can't tell which slot it's on. I misread Ravi's engine die as an axis die (t9343/063-36_429.png).
   9. Dice pips are small: I misread a 2 as a 3 and my radio did nothing. The round summary omitted my wasted radio (t9343/040-76_263.png, t9343/044-80_259.png).
   10. Placement order matters (the engine moves the plane instantly, so radio range changes), and nothing says so. My last radio die became useless (t9343/096-112_213.png).
4. "What just happened?": my radio die cleared nothing and the summary didn't mention it (t9343/040). The plane token stayed put while an empty orange box appeared ahead (slow animation) (t9343/055). The engine threshold kept rising (<=4 to 5 to 6 to 7 to 9 to 10) as gear went down, with no explanation of why gear makes moving harder (t9343/020, t9343/089). A sudden red "ends the flight" warning appeared on my last die of round 6 (t9343/097). After a reroll the game said "You spent a reroll token", which I hadn't realised the button did (t9343/098).
5. Too much on screen: the main board (about 30 slots with tiny P/C badges and labels like "Flap C 2-3"), plus the status chips, plus the tip box, plus the phrase list, plus the sticky footer, all at once on a 390px phone. The "one control at a time" guided mode still shows every control from the first second (t9343/005-Guided.png). The die info panel covers the dice tray (t9343/016).
6. Unreachable / did nothing: the "Level the axis" phrase button was stuck under the sticky Roll bar and tapping timed out (t9343/024). "More" on the first tip did nothing visible except outline the status box (t9343/106). The icon-only top bar buttons (briefing/log/help) have no labels, so I didn't open help until after losing. Tapping a Log round header by its text timed out, though tapping the coordinates worked (t9343/042).
7. Fun verdict: 2.8/5. There's a satisfying co-op dice puzzle in here, and the round summaries and die info panel are good, but the guided mode explained the controls and not how to win, so I lost to a rule I first read on the loss screen. I'd press Play again only because I now know the real rule. A truly casual player would probably quit after the first loss.
