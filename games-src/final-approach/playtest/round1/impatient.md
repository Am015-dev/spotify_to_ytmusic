## Summary

1. **Goal and rounds.** You and a computer co-pilot land a plane. Each round you both roll 4 dice in secret and take turns placing them on the cockpit panel. Axis compares your two dice and tilts the plane (3 tilt = spin). Engines add both dice to move 0/1/2 spaces along the approach track. Radio removes the planes in your way. Gear and brakes are yours, flaps are his, and coffee gives tokens that bump a die by 1. A round is one altitude step (6000 ft down to 0 ft, 7 rounds). In the last round you must be at the Airport with no planes left, all gear and all flaps out, the plane level and speed no higher than brakes.
2. **Result.** Game 1: LOST. The only red item at the end was "All flaps out" (flaps 0/4 until the final round, then 2/4). Everything I controlled was green: no planes left, gear 3/3, level, speed under brakes. Flaps are only on the co-pilot's side. The computer put a die on Coffee almost every round (Coffee earned: 11) and only started on flaps in round 7, when it was too late. I never knew flaps were a loss condition until the end screen. The checklist showed "Flaps 0/4" but never warned me it was falling behind. Game 2: stopped at round 3 (around 105 actions), already heading the same way (flaps 1/4, Ravi back on coffee).
