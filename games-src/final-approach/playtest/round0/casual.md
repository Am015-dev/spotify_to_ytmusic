## Summary
1. GOAL / ROUND: Co-op dice game. I'm the pilot (blue), a computer co-pilot is orange. 7 rounds, the plane drops one altitude row each round. By the end you must be at the Airport with no planes left on the track, all gear and flaps down, a level axis, and final speed no higher than the brakes. Each round: tap a canned "strategy" phrase, both roll 4 hidden dice, take turns putting dice on slots (axis + engines mandatory; radio clears planes, gear/flaps/brakes are switches, coffee gives +/-1 tokens). Engines sum vs two markers decides 0/1/2 spaces of movement.
2. RESULT: LOST on round 7. "Too fast: speed 6 above brakes (4)", and axis 2 right. Gear, flaps and planes were all fine. Why: I didn't know until round 5 that brakes and a level axis mattered at the end. The checklist sat below the fold. In round 6 I followed the Hint ("a 5 on the axis, easy for crewmate to match"), Ravi put a 6, and the tilt went to 2 right. Axis never resets, so I couldn't fix it in one round with 3 low dice. I only learned "never resets" from How to play after the game.
