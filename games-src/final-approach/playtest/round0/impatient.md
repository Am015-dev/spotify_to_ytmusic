## Summary

1. Goal / round: Land a plane co-op with a computer co-pilot in 7 rounds (6000 ft down to 0). Each round both of you roll 4 hidden dice and take turns placing them on the cockpit panel. Axis (the two dice must be close or the plane tilts; a tilt of 3 means a spin) and Engines (sum vs two markers: stay, 1 space or 2 spaces) must be filled every round. Radio clears traffic planes ahead on the track. Gear (yours) and Flaps (co-pilot's) must all be down. Brakes cap your speed on the last round. Coffee lets you bump a die by 1. To win you need an empty track, all gear and flaps down, a level axis and a slow enough final speed by the airport.

2. Result: lost both games. Game 1: spin at 1000 ft (round 6). Two reroll tokens got burned in a row, I don't know how, and left me with 1,1 and no coffee against Ravi's axis 5. A 4-point tilt, so a spin. Flaps were at 1/4 anyway, because the AI co-pilot ignored flaps for 5 rounds while hoarding coffee. Game 2: collision at 3000 ft (round 4). Round 3's speed 11 parked me on space 4, which still had a plane. Next round, the moment the engine dice were in, I "left" the space and crashed before I could place a radio die.
