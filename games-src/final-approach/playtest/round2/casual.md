## Summary

1. GOAL / ROUND: Fly a plane down a 7-space approach track and land it in round 7 with a computer co-pilot (Ravi). Each round we both roll 4 hidden dice and take turns placing them on cockpit controls. Axis and Engines must get a die from each of us every round. Engines sum = how far the plane moves, Axis difference = tilt. You also have to radio away the planes in your path, lower 3 gears, set 4 flaps and set brakes. In round 7 you win if the axis is level, there are no planes left, all gear and flaps are out, and speed is no more than the brakes.

2. RESULT: LOST on the very last die. Everything had a tick except "Axis level" (my 3 against Ravi's 2 = 1 left). Moments that mattered:
 - R1 plane +1, 2 planes radioed: understood (round summary popup is excellent).
 - R2 "Speed 3: advances 0": did NOT understand at the time. I thought I put a 4 on engines. The log showed it was a 2, so I misread small pips.
 - R2/R3 gear moving the blue marker up (<=4 to 5 to 6 to 7): understood only after the fact. The tip for gear came after I had used it.
 - R3 nearly spun: understood, because the warning in the die panel ("tap twice if you mean it") saved me.
 - R4 "Speed 6 -> 0 spaces": did NOT understand at the time. I thought Ravi's engine die was a 4; it was a 2. I also misread which slot his die was on.
 - R5 speed 12 -> +2 and R6 speed 11 -> airport: understood, and those were the fun moments.
 - Flaps raising the orange marker so 2-space moves get harder: understood from the summary, but it felt like a trap.
 - Final loss: understood only from "Look at the panel". I had spent my last coffee token on Brake 6, which I didn't need (speed 4 <= brakes 4 was already enough). Keeping it would have let me bend 3->2 and land. Nothing told me that before I spent it.

3. TOP 10 CONFUSIONS
 1. In the guided flight the whole panel (about 30 slots) is on screen from the start, even though it promised "one control at a time". t9345/004-Guided.png
 2. The briefing tip says "tap a phrase", but the phrases are below the fold, under the tip. t9345/005-Got_it.png
 3. Small die pips: I misread my own 2 as a 4 and lost a whole round of movement. t9345/024-Roll_my_dice.png, then t9345/040-100_258.png (log)
 4. The highlight ring on Ravi's newest die covers the slot label, so I thought his 2 on Engine was a 4 on Axis. t9345/057-Roll_my_dice.png
 5. Phrases do nothing. I said "Move two spaces" three times and Ravi put a 2 on engines, then a 6 on axis. The game itself says Ravi "goes by the panel, not by phrases", so why are the phrases there? t9345/067-195_361.png, t9345/098.png
 6. The tips come out of order and too late: "Control 2: Engines" appears in round 3, after I'd already used Engines, Gear and Radio. t9345/045-112_213.png
 7. The Radio tip says "your space is 1" while the plane is drawn on space 2. t9345/029-76_263.png
 8. The coffee +/- stepper moved between screens, so my tap on + did nothing. Tapping it by its label worked. t9345/082-232_536.png
 9. The landing win conditions (axis must be exactly level, speed no more than brakes) weren't spelled out before my last die. No red "this will fail the landing" warning like the one for spins. t9345/121.png
 10. Hint twice told me to put dice on Coffee while the axis was still empty. That led straight into the "last die must go on Axis" panic. The hint also silently applied a coffee token once. t9345/015-Hint.png, t9345/019-195_361.png, t9345/107-Hint.png

Yes, once, because I now see how close I was. If the second loss also felt unexplained, I'd quit.
