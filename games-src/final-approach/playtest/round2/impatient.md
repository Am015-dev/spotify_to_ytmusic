## SUMMARY

1. Goal / round: Co-op dice landing. You (Pilot, blue) and a computer Co-pilot each roll 4 hidden dice per round and take turns placing them on a cockpit panel. Every round you MUST fill Axis and Engine; the engine sum moves the plane 0/1/2 spaces along a 6-space track to the airport (thresholds shift as gear/flaps go down), the axis dice difference tilts the plane (3 = spin). Radio dice clear traffic planes off the track, gear/flaps/brakes are checklists filled with specific values, coffee dice earn shared +/-1 tokens. A round = one altitude step (6000ft -> 0ft); 7 rounds, round 7 is the landing check: no planes, gear 3/3, flaps 4/4, axis exactly level, speed <= brakes.

2. Result: LOST both games (Port Alder, computer normal).
 - Game 1: lost only on 'Axis level' (finished 1 right). I believed the only axis danger was the red '3 = spin' chip; the 'Axis level' landing requirement lived in a Landing checklist below the fold I never saw. Understood the loss only on the result card.
 - Game 2: lost only on 'All flaps out' (3/4). Ravi's last die was a 4 and the flap needed 5-6; I had just spent the last shared coffee token. Understood it only after 'Look at the panel'.
 - State changes: Plane jumping 2 spaces (round 1 G2, round 6 G2): understood (big engine sum). Plane NOT moving at speed 7 (G2 R5): understood after the fact - forgot gear pushes the blue 'stay' marker up. Tilt swings (2 right -> 2 left -> 2 right in G1): understood the arithmetic but NOT why Ravi chose dice that made it worse. 'Equal dice, tilt stays': understood. Reroll token 'comes aboard': did NOT understand why. Coffee tokens draining: partly - Ravi spent them without me noticing.
