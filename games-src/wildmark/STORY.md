# Wildmark story

All story text lives in one data file, `campaign.json`, and can be edited there without touching code:
`cast`, `acts`, the `chapters` (intro, outro, opponent praise/taunt), `prologue` (the tutorial lines) and `quick` (the
Quick-game intro and result lines). Original names only.

## Cast
- **Ward Elder Rowan** (guide): an old ranger who remembers the valley before the long frost. Warm, dry, proud of you. Speaks in
  the tutorial and every chapter intro. Code-drawn crest (green disc, gold ring) until a painted portrait exists.
- **You**: a new ranger given one small valley to bring back to life.
- **Bram Hollowell** (rival): a timber-cutter who wants to fence the thawing valley for wood. He is not cruel, only practical:
  "a wild valley pays no one". Whoever's valley is wilder at the thaw keeps the claim. Boss of act 1.
- **Pip**: an eager apprentice ranger, all heart and beginner's luck.
- **Sorrel** (meadows), **Linnet** (birds), **Oake** (old growth): rangers of the Mistwood who test you in friendly contests.
- **Sable Quill**: a surveyor mapping the Mistwood for the council; boss of act 2.
- **The Frost**, **The Snowline**: the season itself as an opponent (solo chapters).
- **The Ash Warden**: keeper of the High Cascades after a fire; boss of act 3.

## Arc (3 acts)
1. **The Thawing Valley.** The frost breaks. Rowan teaches you (the tutorial is the prologue). Bram's saws are already at the
   edge of the woods. You grow your first valley, then beat Pip, then out-grow Bram so the valley stays wild.
2. **The Mistwood.** Bigger habitats, sharper rivals. The Mistwood rangers measure you against their own valleys, and Sable Quill
   tries to map the wild into neat lots for the council.
3. **The High Cascades.** Short summers and harsh rules. A fire left the peaks bare; the Ash Warden doubts anything can return.
   Your final valley proves it can.

## Prologue (the tutorial)
The tutorial is the first three days of the thaw, told by Rowan. Bram wants the valley for timber; make it wild in 3 days and it
stays wild. Every step gives a story reason before the action ("A bear is waking in the old forest: take that forest tile").
The next step opens with Rowan's reaction ("A den of two! +4"). The final score is the climax, and the end card is the payoff:
"Bram packed up his saws, for now."

## Quick game
A one-line flavour intro when the game starts, plus a result line in the game's voice on the result screen (win, lose, tie, solo).

## Story mode
Every chapter has an intro scene (portrait + 2 lines: who, what is at stake, the twist rule) and a win outro that leads on. Every
opponent now has a praise line (you won) and a taunt line (you lost), shown on the result screen. Bosses already had these.
Not done: separate loss outro scenes. The shared campaign kit plays the outro only after a win; a loss shows the taunt.
