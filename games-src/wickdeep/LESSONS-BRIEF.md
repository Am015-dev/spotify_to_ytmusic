Lessons (each: id,title<=3 words,rule,goal<=12 words an outcome,setup,success,fail returning a one-sentence cause->effect message,par,moves(),hints [nudge, {say,target selector}],debrief). Reuse the wording of the old lessons in old-ui5-floating-tutor.txt (the LS array) for rule/debrief where it fits.

Goal: Act before the rat this round. Rule: you act at the lower of your two initiatives; lowest first. success: after the cards are readied, G.order[0].id is the hero. fail: G.order[0] is the monster (message names both numbers: your lowest and the rat's 29).

4 retal 'Hits back': a ghoul (type ghoul, retaliate 1) hp 4 at distance 2, hero hp 1, hand [ww07 Hot Wax Hurl (a2 r3 ranged), ww01, ww04, ww03]. Goal: Hurt the ghoul and stay alive. A melee hit takes retaliate and kills the hero; the range-3 shot does not. success: ghoul hp < 4 and the hero alive when the turn is over. fail: hero dead/exhausted.
5 door 'Open the door': use the real scenario-1 map (keep the map, door D). Hero 4 hexes from the door, no monsters in the room, hand [ww04 (m4 bottom), ww03, ww05, ww06]. Goal: Open the door and see the next room. success: next room revealed (G.rev). fail: turn over and still closed.

## Facts (so you do not need to explore)
- save() in ui3.js skips saving when UI.tut is true: set UI.tut=true in stack() (and false when GXST closes: listen to the 'gxst' window event, detail.type 'close'). nextLesson is overridden to return null so no dock lesson shows.
- Hex keys: K(q,r); neighbours WD.nb(key) (WD = window engine, same object scn.js uses); G.hex[k] = {t:'f'|'o'|'trap'|'haz'|'diff'|'door', coin, room, ex, open}; G.rev[room] revealed rooms; E.reveal(G, room) reveals + spawns placed monsters (so first delete all monster figs AFTER revealing, and clear G.place).
- Coin: G.hex[k].coin=1; the hero picks it up when ending a turn / moving on it (engine.js:299,605) into f.coins.
- G.done set by checkEnd (engine.js:812): scenario 1 is kill-all; set G.place=[] and remove monsters only AFTER placing your own so the game does not end at once (a kill-goal ends when no foes remain, so keep at least one foe alive until the lesson wants it to end; for lessons 3-6 give the monster a later target or just accept G.done and judge success/fail BEFORE it: judge on events: G.ev / hero.coins / hp / rev).
- Judge only when settled: !UI.busy and G.ask is the next hero pick or G.done.
- Test taps (see scn.js wd-board): card '#tray .cd', ready '#readybtn', half '#tray .half.can', play '#playbtn', target '#hot .glow.atk', hex '#hot .glow'.
