# Via Tessera: screen layout to copy (structure only; our own art and names)
Reference: the best digital edition of the real game (official PC screenshots, studied by the orchestrator).
1. The MAP fills the screen. Cities sit on the map as round badges with the good they produce on top; city name on a small ribbon; below it the coloured house slots (one per colour) and the cost chips. Roads are dashed lines. Your colonists/ships are standing figures on the map. No text walls.
2. Top-left: slim stacked player strips (rival first): name, colour, score, and tiny counts for houses/colonists/ships/coins. Tap a strip opens that player's panel (close X >= 44 px).
3. Top-right: a row of icon buttons only: tiles/market, players, menu. A '?' help button opens a rules panel.
4. Bottom: a fanned hand of cards (the cards you can play), tap to inspect (stays open until closed) then PLAY. A thin storehouse bar along the bottom with one slot per good you hold.
5. Bottom-right: one big round check button to confirm/end turn, and an undo arrow next to it. Prompt line ('End turn.') top right of the map.
6. Hover/tap a city or card gives a short tooltip. Valid targets are highlighted on the map; everything else is dimmed.
Phone: strips collapse to score chips; hand fans along the bottom; panels open as sheets with a X.
