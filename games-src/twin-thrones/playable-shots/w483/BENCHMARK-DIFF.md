# Twin Thrones vs the official phone app (store screenshots, kept local, not committed)

Side-by-side image: `vs-benchmark.png` (local only, as it contains store art). Ours: `tt-phone-first-turn.png`.

## New-player touch run, Play to the end of Age I (390x844): where I got lost
1. I tapped a card and did not know what I would pay or why (no price on the card in hand).
2. Build / Sell / Wonder were small spots on the table; "Sell" and "Wonder" did not say what they give.
3. The wonder slots were tiny and showed no cost, so I could not plan a wonder.
4. The sell value was hidden until I lifted a card.
5. War and science goals were not visible: which end of the track wins, how many symbols I have.
6. Covered cards were too dark to read their names.
7. "Who starts" and token picks used the old navy panel with grey buttons.

## Fixed (copied from the benchmark)
1. Tap a card: it lifts big with a coin bubble for what you pay (green Free, red when you cannot afford), one plain line on what it does and why that price.
2. Three big buttons under it: Build (pay N / free / too costly), Sell (+N coins), Wonder (name / too costly). With 2+ wonders you can afford, those wonders glow on the table too.
3. Your wonder slots show their coin cost in a gold bubble (red when too costly), like the benchmark's wonder row.
4. The bank always shows "Sell +N", like the benchmark's discard pile.
5. Track ends say "Rival wins" / "You win"; your plate shows science "n/6". Covered cards are brighter.
6. The choice panel is a wooden tray with gold and parchment buttons.

## Still different from the benchmark
- The benchmark is one big wooden board: both cities are columns of compact cards that show their effect icon (not art), with the pyramid below the board. Ours keeps picture cards and a stacked layout, so the cities are smaller.
- The benchmark's war track runs down the left edge and the progress tokens run down the right. Ours are horizontal rows above the pyramid.
- The benchmark shows coins as a purse in the corner and the sell value on the discard pile. Ours uses the plate and the bank pill.
- There is no long-press legend for icons yet.
