# REFERENCE-UI: how top official phone adaptations play

Generic words only. No original names. Read before any phone layout work.
Evidence: store pages, reviews and developer write-ups (2013 to 2026). Strong themes:
- Players tap once to look, again to act. Long-press zooms.
- The board is never covered. Side info slides in as tabs.
- Rival moves are replayed or can be re-read. Complaints: unclear whose turn, too-fast rival moves.
- Reviews of phone ports: tiny buttons and tiny text are the top complaint.
- Skippable animation. Short phase banners.

Genre status: the six strongest lessons are sourced. Per-genre rules mix sourced facts with common practice. Treat rules as defaults to test.

## 1 Worker placement
1. At rest: board with action spots is big. Own workers and resources sit in a thin top strip. Rival boards hide in a drawer.
2. Turn: tap a worker, tap a spot, preview of gain shows, tap Confirm. Undo until confirm.
3. Rivals: a banner names who is playing. Their placed worker slides to the spot. A log chip replays the last move.
4. Teach: tap a spot for a plain-words card. First game, show a label under each spot.
5. Rules:
   - Spots form one scrollable column, not a wide map.
   - Free spots glow. Taken spots show the owner colour.
   - Cost and gain on the spot, in icons plus a number.
   - Bottom bar holds Confirm and Undo only.
   - Round end shows a short score recap with reasons.

## 2 Card drafting / tableau building
1. At rest: your tableau fills the screen. The draft row is a slim strip. Hand sits at the bottom tray.
2. Turn: tap a card to zoom, tap the Play button on the zoom. Drag is optional.
3. Rivals: a small tableau chip per rival. Tap it to open that tableau full screen.
4. Teach: long-press zooms any card. Icons have a legend sheet.
5. Rules:
   - Cards in hand fan and overlap. Selected card lifts up.
   - Tableau scrolls sideways in rows by type.
   - Legal targets glow. Illegal ones dim.
   - Cost payment is auto-filled, with a change link.
   - Score total is always visible.

## 3 Tile laying
1. At rest: the map fills the screen. The tile to place sits bottom centre, big.
2. Turn: drag or tap-then-tap. Legal spots glow. Rotate with a tap on the tile. Confirm to lock.
3. Rivals: their tile flies in and rests highlighted for a beat. Last-placed tile keeps a thin outline.
4. Teach: scoring pops float over the map with the reason. First game, show a one-line hint per phase.
5. Rules:
   - Pinch zoom and pan the map. Double-tap re-centres.
   - Rotate button is thumb-size and near the tile.
   - Only legal spots are tappable.
   - Followers or markers use a second step with large targets.
   - Live score strip at top.

## 4 Deck building
1. At rest: hand fan at bottom. Play area middle. Supply is behind a tab or sheet.
2. Turn: tap a card to play it. Buy by opening supply, tap card, tap Buy. End Turn is one big button.
3. Rivals: a strip shows the last cards they played and bought. A log is one tap away.
4. Teach: long-press shows a big card with keyword help. Keywords underline.
5. Rules:
   - Hand cards never smaller than a thumb pad wide.
   - Supply piles show count and cost on top.
   - Pile of draw and discard show a number. Tap to browse.
   - Auto-play of simple cards is an option.
   - Show coins left as a live pill.

## 5 Dice / push your luck
1. At rest: dice are huge in the middle. Score at stake is a big number. Bank and Roll are two big buttons.
2. Turn: tap dice to keep, tap Roll or Bank. Shake is optional only.
3. Rivals: their dice roll in the same space. Their keeps are shown. Result line says what they got.
4. Teach: a tiny odds hint under the Roll button. Faces explained on first long-press.
5. Rules:
   - Roll and Bank are at the bottom thumb reach, far apart.
   - Kept dice are visibly locked.
   - Risk meter shows what you could lose.
   - Rolls are fast, under one second.
   - Fair and honest roll display. No fake near-misses.

## 6 Racing
1. At rest: track view with your car large. Position list at one side. Speed and fuel as a slim dial.
2. Turn: pick a move card or speed step, preview the path, then Confirm. Real-time games use big steer zones.
3. Rivals: ghost previews or a replay of their path after they commit.
4. Teach: path preview shows hazards with icons. First race shows a coach line.
5. Rules:
   - Camera follows you but keeps leaders visible.
   - Preview before commit. One tap to commit.
   - Hazards use shape plus colour.
   - Lap and place always at top.
   - Result screen shows why you gained or lost places.

## 7 Co-op survival
1. At rest: shared board. A threat meter and resource pills across the top. Each hero is a card in a bottom row.
2. Turn: tap a hero, tap an action, tap a target. Action points show as pips.
3. Rivals (allies and the threat): the threat phase plays out step by step with a caption each step. Allies' plans show as ghost icons.
4. Teach: the danger preview ("next round will...") is always visible. Long-press any token to explain it.
5. Rules:
   - The next threat is shown before you act.
   - Action pips are large and clear.
   - Undo any move that reveals nothing new.
   - Lose conditions are bars, always on screen.
   - End-of-round summary names what went wrong.

## 8 Set collection / pattern building
1. At rest: your pattern board is central. Source pool is above it. Collected items sit in a bottom row.
2. Turn: tap a source, tap a row or slot. A ghost shows the result. Confirm.
3. Rivals: their boards are small thumbnails. Tap to expand. Their pick is highlighted in the pool.
4. Teach: bonus lines show progress counters. First game, label each row.
5. Rules:
   - Valid slots glow. Others dim.
   - Penalty slots show their cost in red.
   - Progress toward bonuses is visible on the board.
   - Colour plus symbol for every item.
   - End-of-round scoring plays slowly once, then can be skipped.

## 9 Trick-taking
1. At rest: your hand is a wide fan at bottom. The trick area in the middle. Rivals are name plates around it with a card count.
2. Turn: tap a card to lift, tap again or swipe up to play. Illegal cards are dimmed.
3. Rivals: each rival card flies to the trick area. The led suit and current winner are marked.
4. Teach: tricks won shown as a counter. Trump suit marked with a badge.
5. Rules:
   - Hand sorted by suit by default.
   - Only legal cards can be played.
   - Last trick is reviewable by tap.
   - Bid and score in a top strip.
   - Pass-the-phone mode hides the hand.

## 10 Hand management / bluffing
1. At rest: your hand large at bottom. Table state in middle. Rival plates with coins or influence around the top.
2. Turn: pick an action chip, pick a target, confirm. Challenge window has a clear countdown or button.
3. Rivals: claims are spoken in plain words in a banner ("Ana claims ..."). Challenge and block buttons appear for you.
4. Teach: tap a role card for what it can do. Claim helper lists legal claims.
5. Rules:
   - The claim banner stays until you answer.
   - Hidden info is clear: face-down cards are labelled.
   - Challenge button is thumb-size.
   - Pass-and-play has a cover screen between players.
   - Log of claims and results is one tap away.

## 11 Area control
1. At rest: map is big, with pinch zoom. Region strength shown as number badges. Your reserve at a bottom bar.
2. Turn: tap source region, tap target, choose count with a slider or plus/minus, Confirm. Drag is optional.
3. Rivals: arrows draw between regions. Battles resolve with a short, skippable beat.
4. Teach: tap a region for owner, units and bonus. Long-press unit types.
5. Rules:
   - Region borders are thick, owner colour is fill.
   - Region numbers are readable at default zoom.
   - Legal moves highlight from the picked region.
   - Count controls use big buttons.
   - Collapsible tabs for cards and scores, so the map stays visible.

## 12 Dungeon crawl
1. At rest: room or map view, hero card at the bottom, enemy cards above. Health as bars with numbers.
2. Turn: tap hero, tap move or attack option, tap target. Dice or cards resolve in a small panel.
3. Rivals (monsters): they act one at a time, each highlighted. A caption says the effect.
4. Teach: tap any enemy for stats. Long-press an item for its text.
5. Rules:
   - Threat range shown on tap.
   - Hit chance or damage preview before commit.
   - Loot choices appear as big cards.
   - Fog of war is soft. Seen rooms stay visible.
   - Fast mode skips monster animations.

## 10 rules for every game on a phone
1. Target size: touch targets at least 44 px. Text at least 14 px.
2. One primary action per screen. Bottom of screen, thumb reach.
3. The board or table is never covered. Info lives in tabs and sheets.
4. Tap to look, long-press to zoom, tap button to act.
5. Preview, then Confirm. Undo until a secret is revealed.
6. Always say whose turn it is, in words and colour.
7. Rival moves play visibly, then can be replayed from a log.
8. Icons: shape plus colour plus a number. A legend one tap away.
9. Animation is short and skippable. Never slower than reading.
10. Portrait first. No horizontal page scroll. Pinch zoom only on maps.
