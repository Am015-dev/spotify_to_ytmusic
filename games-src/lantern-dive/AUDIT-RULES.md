# Lantern Dive: independent rules audit

Scope: `game/src/engine.js`, `game/src/data.js`, `rules-notes.md`, checked against the publisher rulebook (research folder, `rules.txt`; page numbers below are the printed rulebook pages), the retailer logbook text (`64oz.txt`) and the two per-card tables (`classic.json`, `tasks-ref.json`). Nothing from the private research folder is copied here: rulebook content is paraphrased and cited by page. Words in the table use the game's own names (Lantern = trump card, Commander = captain, job = task card).

No game files were edited. Reproduction scripts live in the session scratchpad (`.../scratchpad/audit/oracle.js`, `repro.js`, `r3.js`, `fuzz.js`); the essential snippets are inlined below. Existing `node rules-test.js` still passes (87/87); none of the bugs below is covered by it.

## Method in one paragraph

1. Read the whole engine and data file against the rulebook. 2. Wrote an independent evaluator for all 96 jobs (`oracle.js`, written from the card wording, not from the engine) and ran 4,500 random complete deals (3, 4 and 5 divers, all 96 jobs on random owners, random legal play) comparing (a) the engine's final job status and (b) every early "done/failed" status against the eventual outcome. 3. Fuzzed all 32 dives x 2-5 divers x 24 seeds through the full flow (assign, flare, pass, predict, signal, play) with `checkInvariants` after every move: 3,072 dives, 0 stuck states, 0 invariant breaks. 4. Built concrete positions for every claim below.

Severity: **WRONG** (engine contradicts the rulebook), **MISSING** (rule not implemented), **UNCLEAR** (rulebook silent or ambiguous; engine choice noted), **FINE** (checked, correct).

## Job cards: difficulty values and condition logic

**Difficulty values.** All 96 triples (3, 4, 5 divers) in `data.js` are identical to `classic.json` and `tasks-ref.json` (script compare: 0 mismatches), and the wording order matches entry by entry. The rulebook itself only fixes the mechanism (p.8: add up the value for your crew size, hit the dive total exactly, skip cards that overshoot). Both tables are community sources, so the values are as trustworthy as those tables; the rulebook confirms thresholds of the "less than 8/12/16" job (p.18) and the first-trick / first-two-tricks examples. The draw (`drawTasks`) hits the total exactly in all 7,552 dives x seats x seeds I tried (no empty lists).

**Condition logic.** I did not just sample 30 cards: `oracle.js` evaluates all 96 jobs independently. Result over 4,500 deals x 96 jobs on random owners: final status identical to the engine for 93 of 96 jobs; the three exceptions are the bugs in rows 1 and 2 (jobs 49, 51, 52). Early statuses (done / failed before the last trick) were never wrong either, apart from the same three. A hand-checked sample against the wording (id: source wording, 3/4/5 values, verdict):

How "done" and "failed" are decided: `jobStatus` returns 1 / -1 / 0 from public information; a dive is lost the moment any job returns -1 (or a dive limit breaks), won when all jobs are 1 (and, for hold dives, after the last trick, see row 7), or evaluated at the end of the last trick. This matches p.10 for failure; the only deviation is row 7.

## Summary

The engine is in good shape. Deck, dealing (including the 3-diver extra card and the 2-diver drone), following suit, trumps, trick winner, leader, signalling in all its variants, the flare, hand-out procedures and the dive limits all match the rulebook, and the 96 job conditions agree with an independent evaluator in 4,500 random deals apart from three jobs. All 96 difficulty triples equal the two community tables; 23 of 32 dive difficulties are confirmed by the retailer logbook text, 8 remain guesses and cannot be settled from the material here.

Bugs to fix before release:
1. **Job 49** wrongly lets Lanterns count (row 1; p.18 forbids trumps in total-value jobs). One-line fix.
2. **Jobs 51/52** are scored failed at the end of a 3-diver dive when the unplayed card is a Lantern (row 2). One-line fix.
3. **Dive 19** can hand the Commander a Commander-comparison job, which is unwinnable (row 3).
4. **Missing re-deal** for jobs 57/58 (row 4).
5. **Missing infeasibility checks**: conflicting job pairs are not detected for most/count jobs and are never rejected when there are fewer jobs than divers (row 5); jobs the dive's own limit makes impossible (dive 8, job 21/23/25) are dealt (row 6).

Rulings that should change in `rules-notes.md`: guess 10 (22-or-23 job is not allowed Lanterns), guess 2 (confirmed, not a guess), guess 11 (probably wrong, rulebook p.10 ends the dive at the last completed job). The other guesses are reasonable; rows 8-14 list the alternatives.

Not rules, but worth knowing: `engine.js` line 571 adds an enumerable-false `__ld` property to `Object.prototype` (harmless today, remove it); `condBroken` contains a dead `&& true`.
