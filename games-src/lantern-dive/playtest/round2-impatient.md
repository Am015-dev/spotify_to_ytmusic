## 1. Goal and one round
Co-op trick-taking (feels like a well-known co-op trick-taker). Four divers, 10 cards each, colours Coral/Tide/Kelp/Sunstar 1-9 plus Lantern trumps (1-4). Each dive hands out "jobs" (win a specific card, win no 9s, win none of the first three tricks, win three 5s...). All jobs must be done; if a job becomes impossible the dive fails. A round: pick/pass jobs -> optional distress flare (pass a card, costs an attempt) -> optional signal (show your highest/lowest/only card of a colour, once per dive) -> play tricks: follow the led colour, highest of led colour wins, a Lantern beats everything. The dive ends as soon as all jobs are done (or one fails).
