## 1. Goal and one round, in my own words
It's a co-op trick-taking game. Everyone is on the same team. Each dive deals out "jobs" (win a specific card, win more tricks than the Commander, win 5 Corals, never win Sunstar/Kelp, win an all-odd trick...). You play 10 tricks: you must follow the colour that was led, the highest card of that colour wins, and Lanterns beat every colour. Once per dive you can "signal" one card (your highest, lowest or only card of a colour), with no talking. The dive is won when every job is done and lost as soon as one job becomes impossible. Each trick, everyone plays one card and the winner leads the next.

## 2. Win or lose
I won dives 1-4 on the first attempt. Dive 1 was trivial: one trick, and it was Sumi's job. I lost dive 5. My job was "win the Coral 3", and Sumi (who held it) had to lead it in trick 8. Bram trumped it with Lantern 1, and the only Lantern I had left was a 2 while I was forced to follow Coral. I probably should not have taken a job for a card I couldn't win late in the dive.

## 7. Fun verdict: 3.5/5
Once I got the follow-the-colour rule (which every trick-taker knows), I could play without the rules screen. The tips mostly got in the way, but "Careful: every card you can play breaks a job" and the Last trick panel were genuinely helpful. The teamwork moments were satisfying: throwing Sunstar 1 into Nerea's trick, or keeping a Lantern back so Bram couldn't be forced to win. Dive 1 was over in one trick, which felt like nothing. The AI turns resolve so fast that I lose track of tricks, and the job and signal icons are tiny and cryptic. Failing dive 5 felt a bit unfair because I didn't understand that taking a job means guessing about other players' hands. I'd play again.
