## 1. Goal and one round
A co-op trick-taking game. Each dive deals jobs (e.g. "win the Lantern 3", "win fewer tricks than the Commander", "win a trick with a 6"); the team wins if every job is done. Each trick: leader plays, you must follow colour, highest of the led colour wins, Lanterns are trumps. Once per dive you can show one card (signal). An optional "flare" lets everyone pass a card.

## 2. Win or lose
Dive 1 (guided) won in 2 tricks - I held both top Lanterns, could not lose. Dive 2 won: I ducked every trick (0 tricks vs Commander 8). Dive 3 lost: Bram needed to win with a 6 but his Kelp 6 got trumped by Nerea's Lantern 4 on the last trick. Partly my fault: I followed the Hint button which made me win 3 tricks that Nerea (most tricks) needed.
