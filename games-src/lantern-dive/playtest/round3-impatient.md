## 1. Goal and one round
Co-op trick-taking (like a well-known co-op trick-taker). Each dive: jobs (e.g. "No 9s", "2 tricks in a row", "win Tide 5 + Coral 8") are handed to divers; all 4 play 10 tricks following colour, Lanterns are trumps, highest wins. Do every job = dive cleared; one job becoming impossible = dive failed and an oxygen tank lost. The Descent: 4 zones x (3 dives + boss). Optional once-per-dive signal (show highest/lowest/only card of a colour) and an optional "distress flare" card pass.

## 2. Win or lose
Won all 3 Sunlit Reef dives first try and beat the boss Snapjaw the Eel first try. Then failed Kelp Forest dive 1 (2 oxygen left): Bram's "win a trick where every card < 7" never happened because AI Nerea led and won 7 tricks in a row; I had no job and little leverage.

## 7. Fun verdict: 4/5
Fast, clear, and the boss was the highlight: Undertow flips every other trick so a high card becomes safe and a low card dangerous, and planning to dump my Tide 2 before the cursed last trick felt clever. Job feedback (green ticks, a precise failure panel naming the trick) is excellent. Minus: tip/popup spam for an impatient player, repeated full-screen curse popups, and in Kelp Forest the AI took every job and hogged tricks, leaving me a spectator who lost an oxygen tank.

## 8. Boss and curses
Understandable and exciting. Intro popup with character voice, banner with "Curse on trick N" countdown that flips to a red "Lowest wins" tag - very readable. Undertow genuinely changes decisions. Weak spots: the full-screen "Brace!" popup repeats 5 times per fight; HP bar tiny and static; Lantern ranking under Undertow unclear; boss kill gets the same generic "Dive complete" panel (only a one-line eel quote) and no mark on the map.

## 9. Progression
Made me want to continue: 3 quick dives (2 were over by trick 3-7), stars on the map, a boss, a new zone with a new twist (murky signals) and the oxygen-tank stakes. It turned slightly chore-like only when I had no job and the AI decided everything; the difficulty jump 2 -> 5 between zones is steep.
