## 1. Goal and one round
Co-op trick-taking. At the start each "job" card is given to one diver (e.g. win the Lantern 3, win no Coral, win the first and last trick). Then you play tricks: follow the led colour if you can, highest card of that colour wins, Lanterns are trumps. The team wins the dive when every job is done; it fails as soon as any job becomes impossible. Optional once-per-dive signal: show one card (highest/lowest/only of a colour).

## 2. Win or lose
Won dives 1 (guided), 2 and 3. Lost dive 4 ("Win no 5s"): I kept Kelp 9 too long, was later forced to win a Kelp trick and Nerea had to follow with Kelp 5. I understand why: the result screen said "Trick 8: You won it with Kelp 9" and Look at the table showed the cards.
