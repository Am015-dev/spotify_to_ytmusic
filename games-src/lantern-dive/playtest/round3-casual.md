## 1. Goal and one round
Co-op trick-taking. Each dive has a few "job" cards (win the Lantern 3, win the first two tricks, win fewer tricks than everyone, win two 7s...). Each job belongs to one diver. Everyone plays one card per trick, must follow the led colour, highest card of the led colour wins, Lanterns are trumps. The team wins the dive the moment every job is done; one impossible job fails the dive and costs an oxygen tank. The Descent = 4 zones x (3 dives + a boss). Bosses add curses (Snapjaw: every 2nd trick lowest wins).

## 2. Win or lose
Training: won. Sunlit Reef dives 1-3: won all first try (3 stars). Boss Snapjaw: lost attempt 1 (Sumi got stuck with Tide cards and Nerea never won an "all above 5" trick, both broke on trick 10), won attempt 2 in 3 tricks (Bram needed three 5s and got them fast). Reached Kelp Forest dive 1, stopped there.

## 7. Fun verdict: 3.5/5
It looks lovely, and the map with zone bosses is exactly the hook I like. When I had a job, or could slip a 7 into Sumi's trick to finish hers, it felt clever and great. But too often I was benched: no job, just following suit or tapping Hint. The boss fight was decided by the AI teammates' jobs, and the win in 3 tricks felt like nothing. The red-X fake failure scared me.

## 8. Did the training teach me?
Mostly yes. It was short (3 tricks) and taught follow-suit, highest wins, trumps, jobs and the "dive ends when jobs are done" idea well, and the recap screen was good. It skipped the flare and signals ("press No"), never explained the difficulty dots or the Commander, and didn't teach the most useful skill: dropping a card into a teammate's trick to help their job. I learned that by myself in dive 3.

## 9. Progression and boss: exciting or chore?
The map, stars, oxygen tanks and boss portraits with a taunt are exciting, and I wanted to see the next zone. The boss itself was a letdown. Its curse was cool, but I had no job, the first attempt failed for reasons outside my control, and the second ended after 3 tricks with no hit animation, HP drain or celebration. To keep me playing: always give me a job (or let me pick before the AIs), show the boss taking damage when a job finishes with a big defeat moment, mark beaten bosses on the map, add rewards/unlocks per zone (new diver, card back, lantern skin), and add a clear note when the jobs that break the dive are someone else's.
