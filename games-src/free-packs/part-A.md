# Free art packs, part A (6 games)

Checked 7 Nov 2026 (UTC) by download through the proxy. "Verified" means the files were downloaded, a licence file or page was read, and real images were rendered or extracted into the sheet. Packs were NOT copied into the repo (they sit in `/tmp/scratch/s/work/`, outside it).

Style note: the free CC0 packs that are consistent and have a known author are mostly 3D low-poly (KayKit, Quaternius). They become 2D art by rendering them (a three.js script in `/tmp/scratch/s/work/my/rend/`, orthographic camera, transparent background, one light rig). That gives one consistent look, but it is "toy" look, not painted. The painted/classic look comes from public-domain museum and book plates.

## Rejected on the way (do not use)
- gaspardani87 "Ultimate Fantasy RPG Icon Pack" (itch): says CC0, but the images look AI-generated (1024 px, odd details, dated 2026, no provenance). Excluded under the no-AI rule.
- Antahonist "FREE 48 VTT Monster Tokens" (itch): licence is a free-commercial one ("use these tokens in unlimited personal and commercial projects ... Ship them inside a compiled game"), but it also forbids redistributing the files, and the faces look AI-generated. Excluded.
- Brosure "Potion Icons" (itch): the licence text says "The images were created with AI assistance." Excluded.
- CleanAssets "2D Vector Aircraft" (itch): no licence file in the zip, crude flat art, fighters and WWII crosses. Excluded.
- KayKit Halloween Bits: no cauldron or potions, so not a fit for Cauldron Fair.
- KayKit Dungeon (glb): rendered with washed-out white textures in my script, so its look is unverified; not used.
- Wikimedia API was returning HTTP 429 from about 22:25 UTC, which is why the Commons-based picks have few verified files.

## Known gaps
- No free source found for: Final Approach cockpit panel and approach-track art; Crown City Smash power-card art and a full set of 6 kaiju; Doorkick Dungeon door-deck monsters beyond 4 skeletons; Kaiten Kitchen bun, custard and belt art.
- Rendered-3D picks are "toy" style. If the owner wants painted style, the public-domain plate route (Wikimedia, Met, BHL, NYPL) is the only free one found, and it needs a human art-direction pass (cropping, framing).
