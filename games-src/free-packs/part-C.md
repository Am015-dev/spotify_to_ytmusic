# Free art packs, part C (shipwreck-isle, short-fuse, sunglaze, thornbound, tidewake, twin-thrones)

Checked 7 Nov 2026 (UTC). Every pick below was downloaded through the proxy, unzipped, its licence file read, and its images extracted or rendered for the contact sheets. Nothing from the packs is copied into the repo.

## Method and honesty notes

- **Style of the picks.** The KayKit and Quaternius packs are CC0 3D models, not flat sprites. Their art is consistent, finished toy-like 3D (soft light, bevels, real materials). The sheets show those models rendered to transparent PNG with a throw-away three.js script in headless chromium (isometric camera, one light set). So "use" means a render step first. Quaternius 3D Card Kit also ships finished 750x1050 card-face PNGs, which need no render.
- **Coverage %** = share of six slot types the pick covers in one consistent style: card faces, tokens/pieces, board/map tiles, character portraits, backgrounds/table, icons/item art. Full = 1, partial = 0.5. The 6 slots are my generic split (the per-game slot lists in the repo are larger, 124 to 183 slots).
- **Direct download.** itch.io files need the page's own "Download Now" step (name-your-own-price, $0 is accepted). The itch page URL is the link. The Internet Archive file has a plain URL.
- **No fan-made or publisher art is used.** The Quaternius card kit and KayKit models are original works by their authors.
- **Rejected after checking:** "Free paintings with fantasy game characters" (EnchantedTranquility, itch): page says "AI Assisted" and states no licence. Pixel Frog "Tiny Swords" (current pack): custom licence ("Feel free to use this asset pack in both personal and commercial projects ... You may not redistribute, resell, or repackage the assets") and a pixel look that would not read as a polished commercial board game; its older CC0 build is in the download but is pixel art too. Pixel Frog "Treasure Hunters" is CC0 but pixel UI only.
- **Gaps that no pick fills:** short-fuse has no wire or bomb art anywhere in these packs; tidewake has no ships or leviathans in the free KayKit hex pack (the paid one has them, not used); shipwreck-isle has no survivor portraits.
- **Wikimedia Commons** returned "too many requests" through the proxy during this run, so no Commons file is used. The Internet Archive scan was used instead.

## Reproduction notes (nothing of this is in the repo)

- Packs downloaded with `itch2.sh` to `/tmp/claude-0/s/work/<pack>/`, rendered with `/tmp/claude-0/s/lib/render.js` (three.js 0.160 GLTFLoader, 320 px, transparent background).
- Character renders show the rig's T-pose; use the packs' animation files to pose them before using them as portraits.
- Quaternius pages list only the free "Standard" portion of each kit; full versions are paid and were not used.
