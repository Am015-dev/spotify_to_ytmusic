# W205 asset use audit, 24 Oct 2026

Measured by `games-src/nightrun/qa/assetuse.js` (hooks the Image src setter and every `drawImage`, resolved to asset ids; anim frame = sx/sw). One seeded run on a 390x763 phone: flight stage 1 (god mode, stepped), boss called at 30 s, played to its defeat, then the street level (zeil, god mode) through its boss to the end. No game code changed. Phases: s1, boss, street, sboss (street boss).

## Owner's words vs what the run shows

- Inventory: **287 anim sheets** (35 ship-*: 8 player, 27 enemy; 10 boss-*, each idle/attack/hit/defeat; 16 fx-*, 16 wpn-*, 9 prop-*, 3 pk-*, 16 street actors) and **1038 webp files** in media/. The page requested 329 picture files in total; only **35 anim sheets were drawn** in stage 1 + street 1.
- **Stage 1 only fields 4 enemy ship types: echarger, edrone, egunship, eturret.** Another 23 enemy sheets (27 x 3 states) are never met in the first stage. edrone also draws a tinted copy of the same sheet (`ship-edrone~180_115_154`): one image, recoloured, not a different ship.
- **Stage 1 boss = zentral-ice. It DOES play all four states** (idle 722, attack 1454, hit 408, defeat 75 draws; 4/4/3/4 frames). The boss is not stuck on idle. The other 9 boss sheet sets (36 sheets, ~20 MB) were not reached (stages 2+), so they are unverified here, not proven broken. Only sek-adler's sheets were even loaded.
- **Player ship: `ship-std` flame (4 frames) only**; its death sheet was loaded but never drawn (god mode, no death). The other 7 player ships were not flown.
- **Player shots draw `fx-w-pulse-bullet` (22.4k draws), `fx-shot-std` (7k), `fx-shot-perfect` (4.7k)**, plus anim `wpn-pulse/muzzle` and `impact`. One bullet picture for every shot: same image reused. The stills `fx-w-pulse-hit` / `-muzzle` exist but are drawn once (the anim sheets replaced them).
- **Enemy shots: the same image for everything.** `fx-bullet-enemy` 43.2k draws (33k in s1, 10k in the boss fight), `fx-bullet-needle` 8k (s1 only), `fx-bullet-shell` 1.5k (boss only). `fx-bullet-rocket` never drawn. **All 10 `fx-eb-*` pictures (aim line, bomb, heal ring, laser sweep, mine, plasma, rocket trail, shield bubble, shockwave ring, sniper beam) are never drawn** in stage 1 or its boss.
- **Explosions:** only `fx-boom-small-fire` (s1 and boss), `fx-boom-large-fire` and `fx-boom-boss-a` (boss) were drawn. The other 10 `fx-boom-*` (electric, plasma variants, debris, ground-dust, boss-b) and `fx-explosion-*` stills never drew. Hit sparks: `fx-spark`, `fx-hitconfirm` (4.4k, boss only).
- Weapons: only `wpn-pulse` (the starting weapon) was used; 15 wpn-* sheets and 45 of 48 `fx-w-*` stills never draw because no other weapon was equipped. All 43 `fx-fuse-*` and 22 `fx-ft-*` (on-foot fx) are never drawn in these two levels.
- Street (see limit below): Kai drew idle/run/runshoot/shoot/hurt/land/aimdiag/victory (8 states); props car, police, holo, steam looped; `foot/bg-*` layers and sc-* props drawn. Kai's `jump`/`crouch`-type sheets and `foot/*-crouch/fall/hit` stills were only touched at build time (1 draw), i.e. not shown to a player in this run.

## Limits of this measure (honest)

- **217,360 street draws could not be attributed to an id** (cached/tinted canvases of the street enemies and boss). So I cannot say which grunt/gunman/boss states played on the street: **the street enemy and street boss rows below say "not drawn" only where nothing resolved. Needs a second pass that hooks `FOOT` actor draw calls.**
- One seed, god mode, a bot that sweeps toward targets: a rare enemy or state may exist that this run never met. "Never drawn" = never drawn in stage 1 + its boss + street level 1.
- The probe's "busy moment" screenshot is not busy (3 foes, 0 enemy shots at 30 s; the bot kills fast). The boss appeared about 90 s of game time after `bossNow()` (the hook only works between waves): the pair is real play but not a hand-picked moment.
- 1 draw = build-time cache blit, not a player-visible draw.

## Art that is missing, to ask the owner for (Google Flow), not to draw in code

1. A **player-shot set per weapon in a distinct colour**, bullet + muzzle + hit, for each weapon (this run only saw the pulse weapon, where `fx-w-pulse-bullet` does all the work; other weapons were not tested).
2. **Enemy bullet variants with a colour no other object uses**: round orb, needle, shell, rocket, plasma bolt, each 4-frame loop, one colour per enemy type.
3. **Stage-1 backdrop is one still**: separate near/far parallax layers with a depth cue (the busy shot reads as a pasted picture behind small ships).
4. **Street boss telegraph frames** (windup pose) if attack sheets lack a readable warning (needs the second pass first).
5. A distinct silhouette per enemy type (art question; draw size is a code decision and was not measured here).
