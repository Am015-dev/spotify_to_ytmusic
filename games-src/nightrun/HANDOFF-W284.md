## 2. Why phone < PC (RC, measured)
a. Latency and speed (qa/phone-flight-probe.js, real time, CDP touch vs keyboard): input seen by the page 74 ms after dispatch, ship moves 4 ms after that (touch) vs 4 ms (keyboard). Top speed with a finger drag: 569 world px/s across, 976 along (402 / 690 screen px/s); keyboard 337 across, 614 along (506 / 920 screen px/s). Touch is NOT slower or laggier. (Game cap: touch 520 px/s of ship pull, core/b.js:329; keyboard 300.) The keyboard 'along' figure is above its own 300 cap and is unexplained, probably dash or boost; not chased.
b. Overlap: the touch strip (DASH 54, EMP 54, PAUSE 44 px) is its own 84 px band at y 679-763, below the 679 px play frame; the centre column hits the game canvas at 12 of 12 heights. The ship's box is within the frame. A drag that STARTS on the strip does not steer (stage touchstart ignores #touch), by design; ship travel box 29-347 x 42-633 screen px. No overlay eats touches. DASH/EMP are 54 px (>= 44).
c. Boss: only 16 of 109 life losses happened with a boss up. Counted new boss bullets 0.9 per boss second (pooled objects may under-count). Free lateral corridor ahead of the ship (bullets within 320 world px, field 40..500 = 460 units = 390 screen px at 0.707): median per run 278-460 units (60-100 % of the field), narrowest sample per run 82-128 units (18-28 %, 58-90 screen px). Ship size not measured. The boss is not what kills the phone runs.
d. Lives lost by cause (RC, 109 hp drops in 8 runs): 93 (85 %) in the wave phase before the boss, 16 at the boss. Wave: bullet of an unnamed kind (b.t absent) 68, enemy body 21, nothing within 90 px 4. Boss: 10 with nothing near (boss far, 450-500 px: a beam or area attack, not a tracked bullet) and 6 with a bullet. Two failing runs (dodger s3, shooter s1) never reached the boss. Bullet kind names are not set on enemy bullets, so no per-type split is possible without a game change.
e. Decision rate: NOT measured (no time for a STEPS=3 vs 12 run). Reasoning only: STEPS=12 is one decision per 0.2 s, which is the same on PC where the dodger clears 4/4 (and PC ship speed is lower), so under-sampling does not by itself explain phone < PC.
## Smallest game-side factor / ONE change
No game defect found (no overlay, dead zone or speed clamp). Phone-specific difference that remains: the dodger bot's finger is lifted whenever it reaches 30 px from the frame edge (gauntlet-flight.js I.move) and re-grabbed one decision (0.2 s) later; the game stops the ship when the touch ends. That costs about 0.2 s of steering per edge visit, which exists only on touch. This is a bot artefact, UNPROVEN: not tested. Proposed ONE change (bot, not game): anchor the finger at the frame centre and re-centre it with a short separate move instead of lifting. No game code changed, no difficulty or art change.
