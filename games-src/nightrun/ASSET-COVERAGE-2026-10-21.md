- **fetched**: the browser requested the URL. **drawn**: it reached a visible canvas through `drawImage` (also via off-screen copies and ImageBitmaps). **shown**: it was visible in the DOM (<img> or CSS background). **covered** = drawn or shown.

The other files are generated SHAPE PLACEHOLDERS (`placeholders.json`): by ASSET-POLICY.md and `placeholder_guard.py` they are never listed in ready.json and never drawn, so they cannot count as wired until the owner replaces them with art.
