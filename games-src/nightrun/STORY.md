# Nightrun Story, the map, difficulty, strategy and the song switch (how it works)

Source parts: `parts/story.js` (stages, stage select, result, Hard toggle), hooks in `a.js` (song switch), `b.js` (bosses, difficulty), `c.js` (HUD, flow).
Build: `python3 games-src/nightrun/parts/build.py`.

- **Song switch** (`AU.switchTo` / `AU.xfade` in `a.js`): wait for a bar line of the playing song, start the new song one bar early (mid-file, so its first beat lands on that bar line), equal-power crossfade over that bar. `NR.sw` logs old/new beat position at every switch; both must be whole bars. Mini-bosses and SEK-ADLER keep the stage song and get a drum layer (`AU.intense`); district bosses 2 and 3 switch to `boss`, the final boss to `boss2`; after a boss the next stage song returns on the next bar.
- **Story**: 40 stages in 5 districts, eight each (`CITYS` in `story.js`): run-through, hunt, gimmick, a new twist, the mini-boss, a convoy of leaders, a pre-boss test and the boss. Five optional **challenge** stages (`CHALL`, numbers 41 to 45) branch off each district's mini-boss: `nohit` (the first hull hit ends it), `speed` (the kills before a bar deadline), `perfect` (a PERFECT target, 1.4x for the second star). Stars: clear, PERFECT count (`perf`), no hull lost (challenges: a score). Progress and per-stage upgrade snapshots live in `localStorage` key `mnr_story`; older numberings (16 and 30 stages) move to the new numbers by stage name once (`v40`). `?all=1` opens every stage, a 1 s long press on the title does it permanently.
- **The map** (`map.js`): a painted route. Portrait phones climb it upwards, wide screens run it sideways; nodes are the painted medallions (open, done with stars, boss / mini-boss bigger, locked), the next stage glows, a tap shows the plate (goal, three stars, best, the boss's pattern and the counters, PLAY). One debounced relayout feeds on resize, rotation, visualViewport and ResizeObserver.
- **Difficulty**: one table, `LV` / `HULL` / `TUNE.dm` at the top of `story.js`. `LV[n-1]` is the stage level (gentle start 1.6, +0.2 a stage rising to +0.3, bosses +0.2 above their neighbours, ends at 11.5); it drives bullet speed, enemy HP, extra waves, elites, heal drops and boss HP. `TUNE.dm` adds a level and an HP factor per difficulty on top of the `DIFFS` speed/density multipliers (`b.js`).
- **Balance**: `story-sim.js` plays stages headless in the real page (`?sim=1`: no audio, page stepped by hand; the bot keeps moving, as the anti-idle beam would otherwise hit it). Re-tune `LV` after any change: `RUNS=12 STAGES=$(seq -s, 1 45) node games-src/nightrun/story-sim.js`.
- **Strategy** (`paths.js`, `strategy.js`, `strategy-test.js`): build paths (Glass Cannon, Tank, Beat Combo), pair synergies and 2/4-piece set bonuses (the 4-piece bonus has a price), a heat budget (damage upgrades heat every shot, Coolant and Heat Sink cool), 6 to 8 upgrade slots per run, a stock that rotates per run and per pit stop, selling at 50%, boss picks read from the boss's real pattern list, trade-off perks in the garage, the daily market with a sale, a cargo hold and sell-back. `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node games-src/nightrun/strategy-test.js` checks the numbers.
- **Sweep**: `ONLY=story,switch` runs the map / goal / result / boss-music checks (it taps a node, then PLAY); the full sweep includes them.

## Adventure layer (parts/tale.js, flag `RET.tale`, `?tale=0` = off)
Six cast members, 32 story beats (`TALE_BEATS`) and 5 boss taunt/defeat lines, all in one data table at the top of `tale.js` (edit the text there). `before` beats play as a card the first time PLAY is tapped on the stage plate (one tap or SKIP; the plate shows a one-line preview; the title's PLAY / CONTINUE, NEXT STAGE and RETRY start at once); `after` beats play on the clear screen after the first clear. Every beat unlocks in the STORY LOG (MENU) when its stage is first cleared; locked ones read "???" with the stage that unlocks them. Save: `mnr_tale` (in the export list). Portraits: `media/char-<id>.webp` 256x256 (manifest group "Story cast"); a coloured hex with the initial until the file is real art. `?sim=1` pages never show cards (`&tale=2` forces them). Test: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node games-src/nightrun/tale-test.js`.

## Campaign through-line (parts/storydata.js + parts/saga.js, flag `RET.story`, default ON, `?story=0` = off)
One data file holds every line (`storydata.js`: id, chapter, trigger, speaker, line 1, line 2; max 2 lines of 140 characters a card). Ten chapters over the 40 stages
(`STORY_CH`): a courier carries the stolen Kronos ledger from Frankfurt to an open server in Athens; Wren (the clerk who sent it), ODA (dispatcher, betrays you in
chapter 6, redeems herself in 9), Lumen (mechanic, takes over comms), Vex (bounty racer, turns ally), Direktorin Halde (Kronos chair), ten bosses with an intro and a defeat line.
Where it shows: the opening (first story start, ~25 s, auto-advance, tap / SKIP), a one-card briefing before each stage (first time), comms lines in the stage (start,
mid-point, boss in, boss down; 3 s, no input, they move away from the ship and the touch buttons), the chapter's end scene and a 'story so far' line on the result screen,
the epilogue + credits after stage 40, the street run's before/after (through foot.js / TALE.show). HOME: STORY tile (`hub-story-slot` when present); MENU: STORY LOG
(chapter list, last line per chapter, replays). With RET.story on, tale.js's beats, plate preview and STORY LOG button stand down. Pages under test automation never
meet a scene unless `?story=2`. Save: `mnr_saga` (exported). Missing portrait: ASSETS-NEEDED-STORY.md. Test: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node games-src/nightrun/story-test.js`.
