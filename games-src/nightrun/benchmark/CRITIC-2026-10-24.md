What every reference does that we must match (seen in colour and grayscale):
1. **Value ladder.** Backdrop sits in the low-middle values with low local contrast; actors and bullets are the brightest marks on
   screen. In F2 grayscale the backdrop is dull grey and every orb and ship pops; nothing in the backdrop is as bright as a bullet.
2. **The player has a light.** F2: a white-hot engine flame and a bright muzzle; F1/F3: a white or red hull with a long bright shot
   stream. The player is found by the light, not by searching.
3. **Player shots are streams**, 40-80 px long, bright yellow / white, fired continuously; you always see where you are shooting.
4. **Enemy shots have a halo + white core** in one or two reserved colours (F2 orange and blue, F1 green), drawn in the art itself.
5. **Brawlers:** actors are 1/4 to 1/3 of screen height, thick dark outline, saturated costume colours on a desaturated stage; hit
   sparks are big and drawn (S1, S2), the floor is a clear walkable band.
