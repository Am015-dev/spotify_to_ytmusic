What every reference does that we must match (seen in colour and grayscale):
1. **Value ladder.** Backdrop sits in the low-middle values with low local contrast; actors and bullets are the brightest marks on
   screen. In F2 grayscale the backdrop is dull grey and every orb and ship pops; nothing in the backdrop is as bright as a bullet.
2. **The player has a light.** F2: a white-hot engine flame and a bright muzzle; F1/F3: a white or red hull with a long bright shot
   stream. The player is found by the light, not by searching.
3. **Player shots are streams**, 40-80 px long, bright yellow / white, fired continuously; you always see where you are shooting.
4. **Enemy shots have a halo + white core** in one or two reserved colours (F2 orange and blue, F1 green), drawn in the art itself.
5. **Brawlers:** actors are 1/4 to 1/3 of screen height, thick dark outline, saturated costume colours on a desaturated stage; hit
   sparks are big and drawn (S1, S2), the floor is a clear walkable band.

Lowest three and the exact fix:
1. **Find-the-player 1.** `parts/render/pure.js:31` draws the player at `K*1.1`. Make it `K*1.6` on phone (W<600) and `K*1.4` on PC,

   length, transparent background, same palette as current ship".
2. **Bullets 3 (player shots invisible).** `pure.js:37`: shot width `(b.len||20)*1.2` -> `*3`, and draw each shot picture twice with
   'lighter' (art drawn on black loses half its brightness that way). Keep them under enemy bullets (they are: `shots()` runs before
   the enemy bullets). Target: a visible stream ahead of the ship in every frame, as F1/F3.
3. **Grayscale 3 (backdrop brighter than the actors).** This is the owner's backdrop competing, not an overlay of ours, so no veil.
   Two honest options: (a) owner art: ask for a "far" variant of the district backdrop in Google Flow: "same skyline, distant, most
   windows dark, low contrast, values kept below 50% brightness, no bright spots"; (b) a depth step on the far layer only (not over
   ships or bullets), e.g. `pure.js` far `tile()` with `ctx.filter='brightness(.62) contrast(.85)'`, behind a flag, and show the owner
   one pair before keeping it. Prefer (a); (b) is the rejected "grade" family and must not touch sprites.

Verdict: **REDO.** Every row except owner-art and clutter is below 7. Score per rule 17 rows (cap 25): SHIPS 12, BULLETS 10, ENVIRONMENT 20.
