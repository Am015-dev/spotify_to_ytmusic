# Nightrun independent review (18 Oct 2026)

Reviewer session only: no game code was changed. Screenshots come from `review-tour.js`:
`NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) OUT=<dir> node games-src/nightrun/review-tour.js`. It produces 35 states x 2 sizes (390x763 touch, 1280x720), plus `measure.json`.
Screenshots stay out of git.

## Verdict
**Weighted score: 59.9 / 100.** By reviewer: veteran 55.2, casual phone player 60.8, UI/accessibility designer 63.8.
That sits in the band of a shipped hobby game moving toward good indie, below every benchmark (63-80).

What is good:
- the meta layer: weapons with levels, the garage, the pit stop, medals, chests, checkpoints;
- technical hygiene: 59 fps, 0 errors, PWA, save export;
- the painted title, map and boss art.

What costs points:
- what a player sees in the first five minutes. Bullets and hints blend into a busy backdrop, and debug text and placeholder art show in live play.
- one broken desktop screen (weapon cards).
- a cramped on-foot phone layout.
All reviewers say these are cheap to fix compared with what they cost.

Would they keep playing after 5 minutes?
- veteran: no (bullet readability, flat feedback);
- casual: maybe (art yes, but the tiny text and the 12-button menu);
- designer: yes, but doubts the polish.

### Practices to copy (our words, no assets)
1. **Feel:**
   - forgive input: a short grace window on dash and dodge, and buffered inputs (E);
   - kill bursts and hit-stop on every enemy death, not only the boss (D).
2. **Music:**
   - the reward for hitting the beat must be audible, with more stems joining as the on-beat multiplier rises (B);
   - make every verb rhythmic: dash, bomb and reload, not only fire (B).
3. **Difficulty:**
   - an optional harder version of each boss, unlocked by clearing it, plus a letter grade (D);
   - a soft clock that guarantees a run ends (C).
4. **Depth:** an "evolved" weapon that needs a max-level weapon plus a matching passive, so players hunt for the pair. The 120 fusions already fit this; make the recipe visible (C).
5. **Enemy/boss:**
   - every boss phase changes the body and the verb, not only a banner (D);
   - about three new enemy behaviours per chapter (B's reviews warn about too few).
6. **Readability:**
   - one colour means one thing: only enemy bullets use the danger hue (D);
   - the player's silhouette and hitbox stay visible at all times (E).
7. **Audio:**
   - the gameplay state drives the music stems (B);
   - an adaptive score per area (E).
8. **Onboarding:**
   - teach each verb with a safe moment that forces it, with no text screens (E);
   - one input to learn in the first 30 s (C).
9. **Progression:**
   - every run pays toward a visible unlock;
   - challenges that unlock content (C).
10. **Content:** cheap content first, meaning new weapons, ships and modifiers (C).
11. **On-foot:**
    - unlimited retries with no penalty;
    - one risky action that refills the super meter (D).
12. **Touch:**
    - one thumb, a floating stick, auto-fire, no FIRE button (C, A).
    - Our on-foot pad has five buttons.
13. **Accessibility:** an assist menu with game speed, invincibility and extra dashes, framed without judgement (E). We have assist modes; add a game-speed slider.
14. **Performance:** keep 60 fps on mid phones and measure on a real device, not only headless.
15. **Trust:** nothing unfinished on screen, consistent language, no forced waits (A's free-to-play grind is its weak point).

## Risks to reviews
- **First minute:** a phone player meets the 12-button menu, then grey hints on a busy backdrop. A placeholder box can appear in stage 3+.
- **Phones:** headless shows 59 fps with no GPU, but no real mid-range Android or iOS device has been measured. The 4 MB first load and the 923 KB single file are fine on Wi-Fi, but heavy on 3G. The music adds more.
- **Difficulty extremes:** the bot clears 0% at stages 18, 23, 39 and 40 with the default kit (RETENTION.md). The economy takes about 360 h to max.
- **Black squares and placeholders:** 329 placeholder files are listed. The 12 fake-ready ones show in play now. Any new `ready.json` entry without a guard can repeat this.
- **Audio** was not reviewed by anyone (stills only), but it is the game's identity. A listening pass is the next review.
