1. STREET CLEAR false resume line: parts/street/foot.js:581 shows the line when `S.bcp` is set; on the clear screen it must test `S.bossCp`
   (YOU WENT DOWN keeps `S.bcp`, that is the offer). Proof after the fix: one late-bot clear with resumes=0 shows no resume line; one with resumes=1 shows it.
2. Store page "Unlock nine ships in the garage": the build has 8 ships (7 to unlock). Fix the number in release/STORE-PAGE.md (or count in the built garage first).
3. Flight STAGE 1 CLEAR card: one score only (hide the HUD score under the card or make them equal), and the "Ready in Garage" line only when a GARAGE button is on the card (the rule W321 applied to game over, profile/ret.js:34). Drop or explain "Grades".
