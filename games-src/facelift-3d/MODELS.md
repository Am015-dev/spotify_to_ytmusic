Pipeline: flatten/join/weld, ground at y=0 and centre x/z (all sources were already upright), simplify to budget (position+UV, permissive), base colour texture to 256-512 px WebP (1024 for hero pieces), metallic 0 / roughness 0.75 (metal-rough texture kept at 256 only for metal items), meshopt. Thumbnails: 512x512 transparent WebP, 3/4 view, three.js headless. 2D games use the `.webp` as sprites.
