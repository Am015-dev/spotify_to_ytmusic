## 1. Goal / one turn
Build a shared map from square tiles; most points wins. Each turn you get one random tile, tap a glowing spot, turn it if allowed, Place, then optionally drop one of your 7 followers onto a town, road, priory or farm on that tile. Finished towns, roads and priories score at once and the follower comes back. Farms and unfinished stuff only score at the end. The game opens with a river laid first (only 1 legal spot per turn).
