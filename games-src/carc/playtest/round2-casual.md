## 1. Goal and a turn
Tile-laying in a valley (classic tile-laying). Each turn you draw a tile, place it so edges match (glowing squares show legal spots), then optionally put one of 7 followers on a road, town, priory or field on that tile. Finished roads/towns/priories pay out and return the follower; at game end unfinished ones pay a little, and farmers pay 3 per finished town next to their field. Game starts with a forced river phase. Most points wins.
