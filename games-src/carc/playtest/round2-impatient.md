## 1. Goal and a turn
Lay one square tile per turn so its edges match (towns, roads, fields, river), building a shared valley. After laying you can drop one of 7 followers on a town, road, priory or field of that tile. Finished towns (2/tile + 2/banner), roads (1/tile) and priories (9 when surrounded) pay out at once and return the follower. Unfinished stuff pays a little at the end, and fields pay 3 per finished town next to them at the end. River tiles come first, one spot only. Most points wins.
