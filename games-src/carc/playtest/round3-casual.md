## 1. Goal / one turn
Build a shared valley with square tiles; most points wins. Each turn: draw a tile, tap a glowing square (preview tells you what it extends/finishes), turn it, Place, then optionally drop one of your few followers on a road, town, priory or farm on that tile. Finished roads/towns/priories pay at once and the follower comes back; farms and unfinished stuff pay at the end. Computer then plays and a summary card shows its points.
