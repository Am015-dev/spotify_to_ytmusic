## 1. Goal and a turn
Build a shared valley of square tiles (classic tile-laying). Each turn you get one tile, tap a glowing square where its edges match (town/road/field/river), optionally turn it, tap Place, then optionally put ONE of your 7 followers on a road, town, farm (or priory) on that tile. Finished roads/towns/priories score and give the follower back; farms only score at the end (3 per finished town touching). Most points wins. Game starts with a river phase.
