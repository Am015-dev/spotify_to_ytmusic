## Town buildings (8 kinds x 7)
| original role | ours | effect |
|---|---|---|

## Lore tiles 1-26
1 Free Charter: same building twice in a town. 2 Deep Seams: +1 worker per quarry at chapter end. 3 Silver Tongue: selling gives
2 silver. 4 Hired Porters: selling gives +1 worker. 5 River Charts: a barge loads 2 neighbouring stalls. 6 Open Purse: buy from any
stall. 7 Shepherd's Psalter: +1 point per animal tile scored. 8 Strong Backs: a worker turns a die by up to 2. 9 Mason's Square:
free ±1 to place a town tile. 10 Boatman's Knot: free ±1 to place a barge/animal. 11 Lord's Seal: free ±1 to place a keep/quarry/lore.
12 Trader's Eye: free ±1 to take a tile. 13 Paid Day: taking workers also gives 1 silver. 14 Full Muster: taking workers gives 4.
15 Wide Trade: 3 points per goods kind sold. 16-23 Rolls: 4 points per Granary / Bell Tower / Joinery / Chapel / Fair / Hostel /
Counting House / Guildhall. 24 Bestiary: 4 points per animal kind. 25 Ledger: 1 point per goods tile sold. 26 Seal Roll: 2 points per seal.
