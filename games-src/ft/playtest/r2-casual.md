## 1. Goal and a round, in my own words
Most points at the end wins; coins count as points. Each round: bid coins for turn order (2 players = 2 markers each, so I play twice), then on each turn pick up all the people on one tile and drop them one per tile along a path (like mancala). The tribe where the last one lands gets "worked": Advisors/Sages = keep for points, Traders = take goods/mystic cards, Masons = coins, Shadows = kill one person (or a rival's Advisor/Sage). If the tile empties you put a camel there (land points), then a forced tile action (palm on oasis, palace on hamlet, djinn on shrine, buy goods at bazaar). At end of turn you may sell a set of different goods. Ends when camels run out - or, as happened to me, when no legal move is left.

## 2. Why I won/lost points (3 concrete examples) and why I won
- R2 plan "2 Advisors": camel on Hamlet C5 +5, palace +5, Advisors 1 each + 10 for having more than Teal -> about +22. Advisor majority was my biggest swing.
- R3: I lost 10 during Teal's turn (130 -> 120) when Teal caught up to 2 Advisors and my "+10 for more Advisors" bonus went away. Same around R4 (Teal's Shadow took one of my Advisors: 4 -> 3) and R5 (152 -> 142).
- R5: Traders gave me 5 goods -> 6 different kinds = a 30-coin set; R3/R4 Masons earned 15-20 coins. Final table: 97 of my 283 points were coins.
- I won 283 to 206 because I always pressed "Best plan" and the suggested option; Teal mostly kept Sages/djinns and lost its Advisors to my Shadows. I made almost no decisions myself.
