## 1. Goal & what a round is (my words)
Most points wins. Points = coins + kept Advisors/Sages + djinns + claimed tiles (camels) + palms + palaces + sets of goods. Each round: bid coins for turn order (each player places 2 markers in 2p; higher slot = earlier, costs coins = points). On your turn you pick up ALL people from one tile and sow them one per tile along a path; the last one must land on a tile holding its colour; you take all of that colour there and use them (Advisors=keep, Sages=keep/pay for djinn, Masons=coins per blue tile around, Traders=take goods, Shadows=kill a person). If the tile is now empty you put a camel on it. Then the tile's own action (bazaar buy, shrine djinn, oasis palm, hamlet palace). Game ends when a player runs out of camels or nobody has a legal move.
