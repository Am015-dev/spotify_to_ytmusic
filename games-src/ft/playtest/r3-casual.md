## 1. Goal and a round (own words)
Most points (coins count as points) after the round in which someone places their last camel. Each round: bid coins for turn-order spots (2 markers each in 2p, so 2 moves each), then on your move you pick up all people on a tile and drop one per tile along a path; the last one must land on a tile holding its own colour, you take all of that colour (Sages, Advisors, Masons, Traders, Shadows) and use them, then maybe claim the tile with a camel and do the tile's action (summon djinn, buy goods, palace, palm). Optional sell of goods at end of turn.
