## 1. Goal and a round, in my own words
Most points wins; coins ARE points. Each round everyone bids coins for turn-order spots (2 players = 2 spots each, so 2 turns each). On a turn you pick up all the people on one tile, walk them across neighbouring tiles dropping one per tile, and the last one must land on a tile that already has its colour; you then take all people of that colour there and they do their job (Advisors = points + majority bonus, Sages = pay for djinns, Masons = coins per nearby blue tile, Traders = goods, Shadows = kill someone). If the tile you emptied is empty you put a camel on it (land points) and get the tile's action (palace, palm, market buy, djinn). Ends when someone places their last camel - or, as happened to me, when the board seemingly runs out.

## 2. Why I won/lost points (examples) and why I lost
- Bidding: paid 18 for 1st spot in R4 and got a +12 move -> net loss. Every coin bid is a point gone (r2-skipper/096-wait.png).
- Advisor majority swing: +10/-10 several times when Teal's Shadow took one of my Advisors or I took theirs (r2-skipper/052-wait.png, 102-wait.png). Biggest invisible number in the game.
- Nakhla djinn (palms score 5) made my palm plants worth a lot: +27 in R7 (r2-skipper/174-wait.png).
- First move: I followed a "+14" tile badge myself but only got +6 because I didn't empty the tile -> no camel claim (r2-skipper/026-wait.png).
Lost 204-237: Teal had 3 djinns early (36 vs 14 from djinns) and kept the Advisor majority at the end (15 vs 5), plus goods sets. I never collected goods until too late and wasted coins on bidding early.
