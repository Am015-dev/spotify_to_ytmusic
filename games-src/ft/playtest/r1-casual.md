## 1. Goal and what happens in a round (my words)
Gather the most points by the time someone uses up their camels. Each round: first you bid coins for turn order (each player places 2 markers in 2-player). Then on your turn you lift all the meeples off one tile and drop them one per tile along a path; the last one has to land on a tile with the same colour. You take all of that colour from there and use them: yellow Advisors / white Sages are points (Sages also pay for djinns), blue Masons earn coins for nearby blue tiles, green Traders take market cards, red Shadows remove a person or steal a rival's Advisor/Sage. If the tile ends up empty you put a camel on it (tile points). Then the tile itself does something: palace, palm, buy goods, summon a djinn. You can sell goods at the end of your turn. Coins are points.
