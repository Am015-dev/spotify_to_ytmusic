## 1. Goal and a round, in my words
Get the most points (coins count as points). The board is a 5x6 grid of tiles full of coloured people. Each round both players bid coins for turn-order slots (2 slots each with 2 players), then take turns. On a turn you pick up all the people on one tile and drop them one per tile along a path; on the last tile you take every person of the colour you dropped last and do their thing: Traders = goods cards, Masons = coins per nearby blue tile, Advisors/Sages = kept for points, Shadows = kill someone. Then the tile itself acts (bazaar = buy goods, shrine = summon a djinn with Sages). If the tile ends up empty you put a camel on it and own it (tile points, palms, palaces). At the end of your turn you may sell a set of goods. The game ends when someone runs out of camels or no tile can start a move.
