**Total: 4.0 — FAIL** (stopper: yes. Rule broken: a landing at speed equal to the brake value counts as a crash. The published game allows it, the game's own "Speed ≤6" chip promises it, and rules-notes item 9 says so. Uncapped average 6.0.)
### Findings
- **Row 1 / 8** — Did: played the default quick game (Port Alder, normal) by touch, following the strong suggestion, to the final round. Happened: the log reads "Landing speed 6 against brakes 6", then "LOST: the landing failed (speed)" and "Too fast: speed 6 is not below the brakes (6)". In the published game the red brake marker moves *past* each brake number (after the 4 it sits between 4 and 5). So speed equal to the last brake set is a legal landing. `engine.js:72` `slowOk` uses `s < brakeVal`, changed in commit 0305f5e2 "landing rule". It contradicts the game's own checklist chip "Speed ≤6" (`engine.js:469` / `ui11.js:16`) and rules-notes section 8 item 9. Players are told they are safe, then lose. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-01-speed-chip-vs-strict-brake.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-03-loss-too-fast.png`
- **Row 6** — Did: played 5 full default games by touch, following the game's suggestions. 2 games used the quick advice the hint line shows; 3 used the full-search advice. Happened: 1 landing in 5. In the 375x667 game the plane entered the final round tilted 2 left. The teammate put a 3 on Axis, and the suggested pilot move was a 3 (a coffee spent to turn a 4 into 3). Level dice do not fix a carried tilt, so "Not level at landing". In the same round the teammate spent a die on Concentration and its last die on a Radio that hit an empty space, leaving the 4th flap out. In another game the teammate's second engine die made speed 7 against brakes 6 ("Ravi used Engines: Speed 7. Too fast!"). A new player following the board will mostly lose the first airport. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-02-loss-axis-not-level.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-01-speed-chip-vs-strict-brake.png`
- **Row 5** — Did: looked at every in-game screen at 390x844 and 375x667. Happened: the landing checklist chip row (Level / At airport / Speed / No planes / Gear / Flaps) runs off the right edge ("Flaps 4/4" clipped). The painted panel labels and track numbers are about 12 px canvas text, below the 14 px bar (the DOM audit can't see them). The partner's hidden "?" dice are 27x27. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-01-speed-chip-vs-strict-brake.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-11-375-first-turn.png`
- **Row 5** — Did: finished the tutorial, then started the real game (375x667). Happened: a leftover tooltip "Sets the brakes to 6." floats over the Gear 1-2 / 3-4 / 5-6 labels during the round 1 briefing. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-04-stale-tooltip.png`
- **Row 4** — Did: first placement turn of a real game. Happened: the full-width primary bar at the bottom is "Reroll (1)". A newcomer's most obvious tap spends the only reroll token on turn 1. Spending coffee also needs a hidden second tap ("Spend a token? Tap again."). Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-05-reroll-primary-hint.png`
- **Row 1 (published-game feel)** — Did: compared the round flow with the published rules. Happened: hidden dice are handled well (partner dice show "?", placed dice are public, no talking during placement). But the briefing is only a "Roll my dice" button: no strategy talk with the teammate before the roll (the published game's phase 1), and the teammate never says what it plans. The co-op becomes "guess what Ravi does". Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-10-goal-card.png`
- **Row 3** — Did: Quick game > Computer as a new player. Happened: an offer dialog, then a Goal card with 4 rule bullets plus weather icons appears before the first tap. It's readable, but it's a text block over the board. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-10-goal-card.png`
- **Row 2 (positive)** — Did: tutorial by touch at 390x844. Happened: 27 steps (axis, turns, engines, coffee, gear, altitude, radio, reroll, coffee spend, tilt crash, flaps, overshoot, brakes, final round, speed, win). Each step is one short line with one highlighted tap, 0 wrong-tap traps and 0 errors. It ends with "Play a real game". Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-06-tutorial-coffee.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-07-tutorial-end.png`
- **Row 7 (positive)** — The landed and crashed scenes, round recap cards and dice flights feel like a game. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-09-landed.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/final-approach-08-title.png`

Reviewer check: the final-approach stopper is confirmed in code. `engine.js:72` has `slowOk = brakeVal >= 2 && s < brakeVal`, but the branch's own rules notes (point 9) say a speed equal to the last brake value must land. `ui11.js:92` also disagrees with itself: `cap` uses `<` while `good` uses `<=`.
