### Findings
- **Row 6** — Did: played two full 20-turn games on the default Play button (Normal rival), always taking the pair worth the most points right now (about what the game's own hint badges suggest). Happened: lost 44-83 at 375x667 and 32-57 on animals at 390x844. Bram reached 6 lone hawks and an elk line of 4 every game. A new player on the default level is very unlikely to win. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-02-ai-wins-83-44.png`
- **Row 5** — Did: scrolled the result screen. Happened: every explanation line ("lines of 2, 1", "pairs 1, 2, 3, 4 = …", "largest area 7 + bonus 2") is 13 px dark brown (#3d3220) on the dark green panel, so it is close to invisible. The final totals are not in the header either; they sit at the bottom of a long table. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-01-result-unreadable.png`
- **Row 5** — Did: placed tiles by touch at 375x667. Happened: the empty spots are 29x33 px (38x44 at 390x844, 34x39 in the tutorial), below 44 px. Late-game token cells shrink to 39 px as the map grows. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-03-small-spots-375.png`
- **Row 3/4** — Did: started a real game without the tutorial. Happened: play mode shows only the two score chips and the acorn count. The five animal scoring rules (pairs, lines, runs, lone, neighbours) are hidden behind an unlabelled book icon, so a stranger can't see what to aim for. Point previews are off unless you find the Hints toggle. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-05-no-goal-in-play.png`
- **Row 5/7** — Did: finished a turn. Happened: the "+2 River Runs" score pop and the "Your turn" banner float over the tiles at the same time and overlap each other and the tokens. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-04-text-over-board.png`
- **Row 2** — Did: walked the tutorial to the end. Happened: the step tagged as the acorn "replace" rule says "Free pick takes any tile and any animal". The second acorn power (swap any number of market animals) is never shown or tried, so only one of the two acorn powers is taught. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-07-tut-acorn.png`
- **Row 4** — Did: tapped Bram's score chip in the middle of my turn (tile held). Happened: the view switches to Bram's valley, my held tile and the Place/Back buttons vanish, and the market dims. No line says how to get back (you have to tap "You"). Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-06-rival-view-midturn.png`
- **Row 7** — Did: turned a two-terrain tile in the tutorial. Happened: the ghost tile is about 55 px across and the painted terrains (peak, forest, meadow, wetland, river) blur together. "Forest meets forest" is hard to judge by eye; you rely on the step text. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-08-tut-rotate-small.png`
- **Row 8** — Did: tapped the acorn counter (top right, looks like a chip). Happened: nothing; its explanation is a hover title, which never shows on touch. This is the only dead tap found; it does not stop play. Shot: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-05-no-goal-in-play.png`
- **Positive (Row 1/7)** — The rival's turn plays out step by step in its own valley with a caption and score pop, then returns to yours (~3.3 s). The tutorial's result walk-through and its "Play a real game" button work. Shots: `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-09-ai-turn.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-10-tut-end.png`, `games-src/reviews/G5-shots/wildmark-11-title.png`
- Rules checked with no deviation found: tile supply, the four-pair market, the four-alike auto sweep (repeats), the optional three-alike sweep once per turn, acorn on keystone, both acorn powers (spend any number, before picking), a token on any empty matching tile or back to the bag, 20 turns, set-A point tables, the largest-area habitat score, the 2p majority (+2, tie +1 each), acorns 1 point each, the acorn tie-break.
