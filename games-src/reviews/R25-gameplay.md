How I tested: `VIS_SLUGS="," build-all.py --quick --out /tmp/r25 tidewake` (needs `npm i` in games-src for three.min.js), then the phone-check run separately because `--quick` skips it. Touch only: Playwright hasTouch + isMobile, page.touchscreen.tap at element centres, with an elementFromPoint check before each tap. Tutorial from the title button "New here? Learn in 5 minutes" to the end card at 390x844 and 375x667. Then 21 Quick games (you vs 2 computers, default settings, "Just play" on the offer card) from the title to the result screen, 12 at 390x844 and 9 at 375x667, as a new player in two ways:
- **star** (12 games): "Safest start", then "Use ★" and Place every turn.
- **ignore** (9 games): a random gold mark, then a random tile, 0-3 random Turns and Place, trying again when Place is greyed out.

Up to 4 games ran side by side, so the measured waits may be a little high. Across 2 tutorials and 21 games: 0 page errors, 0 console errors, 0 stuck states. I re-checked the rules against a rulebook summary online (notes kept locally only).

**Gate: FAIL.** The build passes, but the `--quick` scenario gate is **6/8**: tw-board and tw-art fail with "setup failed: GXS.wait timed out (G.phase==='play' && G.step==='act' …)". phone-check is **0/6 scenarios**: every one fails on "text >8 words: 10w 'Your turn tap gold mark on…'", and 390x763, 375x553, P>L, L>P and stale also have 3-6 dead taps on the board canvas.
