## The 10 worst problems (before)
1. Whose turn it is is a small grey line at the bottom ("Ines is playing"); no banner, the active seat only gets a thin outline.
2. Crewmates' cards just appear: no caption says who played what or why, and the trick winner is a 1 s pop with no reason.
3. Cards carry only a number and an abstract glyph; the colour name (Coral, Kelp, Tide, Amber, Sonar) is nowhere, so "follow suit"
   means guessing which glyph is which. Two overlapping hand rows hide all but the number on the top row.
4. Seat stat "10 won 0" is cryptic (card-back icon + hand size + tricks won).
5. Signal chips "📡 ● only [6]" and task minis "①[1]" are unlabeled glyph pairs; nobody can read them in a minute.
6. Tutorial is 11 steps and 8 of them are "read, then Next"; the bubble covers the top bar, "Skip tutorial" sits on the menu buttons.
7. First real turn opens with an optional "flare" decision before the player has played a single card.
8. Instruction line is 14 px grey; the trick name tags (Bram/Kito/You) are ~10 px and cover the card numbers.
9. Desktop 1440x860: cards and seats are tiny in a thin strip; the middle of the screen is empty.
10. Help opens on rule cards; there is no five-line "How a turn works" first.
