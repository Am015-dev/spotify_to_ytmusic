Keep games on this device so they open without internet, and move your saved games between your phone and your computer.

Online

Take games with you

Switch on Keep on this device and the game is stored here. Offline you can play against the computer or pass one device around. Online play with friends needs internet.

Checking storage…

    The shelf itself, its covers and these pages are stored too, so the shelf opens offline as soon as one game is kept. Phones may clear stored data if they run low on space; if a game is gone, just download it again. Saves are not affected by this, but see below for backups.

    Move your saves

    Saved games stay in the browser they were made in. Use this to copy them from your phone to your computer, or back.

    Saves on this device

      Method 1: a saves file

      …or drop a saves file here

      Send the file to yourself by email, a chat app or cloud storage, then load it on the other device.

      Method 2: device to device

      No file needed. Both devices must be online and open this page.

      On the other device, tap Receive and type this code, or scan the picture.

      Only accept saves from a device you own. Nothing is changed until you choose Merge or Replace on the next step.

      iPhone: the Home Screen app and Safari keep separate saves. This tool moves saves between them too: in the app, tap Receive and type the code instead of scanning.

      Saves found

        Merge adds new saves and overwrites matching ones with the incoming copy; saves only on this device are kept. Replace all makes this device match the file exactly. Your current saves are backed up first.

        Privacy: your saves never go to a server. Device-to-device transfer only uses the same public peer-to-peer matchmaking as online play; the saves travel straight between your two devices.
