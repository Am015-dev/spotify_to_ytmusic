Card notes from mission card images (source kept in the private research repo), my paraphrase.
M1 Boot Camp I: 24 blue (1-6), no red/yellow, no equipment. Defaults: dial by player count; char card each, Twin Probe 1/game; setup info token clockwise from captain on a blue wire. Rules: dual cut, solo cut.
M2 Boot Camp II: 32 blue (1-8), 2 yellow drawn from 1.1-7.1. No equipment. Yellow info token not usable in setup. Yellow wires all same value; say "this is yellow". If yellow wrongly pointed (fail), use yellow info token.
M3 Boot Camp III: 40 blue (1-10), 1 red drawn from 1.5-9.5. Equipment from now on: random, count = players; if Equip 2 (Handsets) or 12 (Equal Tag) drawn, discard and replace. Red: cut = explode. Reveal red: if only red remain at START of turn, MUST reveal; then no more actions. Equipment unlock: cut 2 wires of card value; once use; flip facedown.
M4 Field Day One: 48 blue (all), 1 red, 2 yellow. 2p: 1 red, 4 yellow. (no new rules; reminder)
M5 Field Day Two: 1 red, 2 of 3 yellow. 2p: 2 red, 2 of 3 yellow.
M6 Field Day Three: 1 red, 4 yellow. 2p: 2 red, 4 yellow.
M7 Last Lesson: 1 of 2 red (no yellow). 2p: 1 of 3 red.
M8 Graduation: 1 of 2 red, 2 of 3 yellow. 2p: 1 of 3 red, 4 yellow. No special rules. Success -> open box 9-19.
M9 Pecking Order: 1 red, 2 yellow, Number cards, Sequence card (side A). Shuffle Number cards (1-12), draw 3 faceup in a line (a,b,c), Sequence card side A above leftmost. Can't cut any b-value wires until >=2 a-value wires cut; can't cut c until 2 a and 2 b cut. Others any order. After 2 a cut flip a facedown, shift Sequence card to next. 2p: 2 red, 4 yellow. FAQ: if player only holds uncuttable wires -> explode.
M10 No Coffee Today: 1 red, 4 yellow, 15-min timer (2p: 12 min). Equip 11 (Coffee Break) replaced if drawn. No clockwise order: anyone says "Snip!" to become active after previous turn ends. Same player can't take 2 turns in a row (except 2p; FAQ: or only player with wires left).
M11 Colourblind: 0 red, 2 yellow, 1 Number card drawn faceup on mission card. The 4 blue wires of that value act as RED: cutting one = explode; may reveal them only when they're the only wires left in hand (like reveal red). 2p: 4 yellow; captain places no setup info token.
M12 Paperwork: 1 red, 4 yellow, Number cards. One Number card faceup on each equipment card (equipment number visible). Equipment usable only after cutting 2 of its own value AND 2 of the number card's value; after cutting a pair matching a covering Number card, discard that Number card. 2p: 2 red, 4 yellow. FAQ: a pair cut unlocks both equipment of that number & number card of that number.
M13 Triple Trouble: 3 red, (no yellow). Reds not shuffled: deal 1 red facedown to each player starting captain clockwise (2p: captain gets 2, one per stand) [with 4-5p only 3 players get one]; then deal blues as usual. Setup info token: drawn at random instead of chosen; if no matching wire, place it faceup beside hand; yellow token redrawn. Special action: cut all 3 reds at once by pointing at 3 wires among ALL uncut wires (any stands incl own?); any not red -> explode. With 4-5p, possible even with no red in hand. Player with only reds MUST do this, cannot Reveal Red. 2p: captain no setup info token. FAQ: equipment can't target reds; reds can't be chosen with detectors.
M14 The Rookie: 2 red, 2 of 3 yellow. Characters dealt randomly facedown; who gets Captain card = "rookie". Rookie failing a dual cut -> explode. Rookie can't use Equip 9 Damper. 2p: 3 red, 4 yellow.
M15 Lost in Translation: 1 of 3 red, no yellow, Number deck. Equipment (standard count) placed FACEDOWN unseen. Number deck facedown, flip top. When all 4 of visible Number value are cut: flip 1 equipment faceup, immediately usable regardless of number; flip next Number card. If revealed Number already fully cut, discard & replace (no equipment). 2p: 2 of 3 red.
M16 Groundhog Day: 1 red, 2 of 3 yellow, Number cards, Sequence card side B. Like M9 but requires ALL 4 a cut before any b; all 4 a and all 4 b before c. 2p: 2 red, 4 yellow.
M17 Fibber: 2 of 3 red, no yellow. Characters dealt randomly; Captain-card holder = "liar". Setup: liar places 2 info tokens which must be FALSE (value != wire), not in front of red. All liar's info tokens mean "not this value". When a dual cut on liar's hand fails, liar places token with the STATED (wrong) value. Liar can't use Equipment cards but can participate in Handsets(2) and Sweep(8). 2p: 3 red.
M18 Bat-Signal: 2 red, Number deck; only Equip 8 Sweep on board, always usable (repeatable). No setup info tokens. Each turn: reveal top Number card; run Sweep on that value; active player designates who (incl. self) must do a cut action using that value. Deck empty -> reshuffle. Number card discarded once its 4 wires cut. Only-red at start of turn -> must Reveal Red. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: only designates player, next turn goes to left of active player.
M19 Cave Lair: 1 red, 2 of 3 yellow, phone (audio file Mission 19 via QR). Rules delivered by audio (unknown). Failure: replay. Success -> open box 20-30.
M20 Odd One Out: 2 red, 2 yellow, "X" tokens. Last wire dealt to each stand isn't sorted: placed at far right regardless of value, with X token. Equip 2 Walkie replaced if drawn. Equipment (all incl. personal, Full Scan 5, Sweep 8) cannot be used on/ignores X wires. 2p: 2 of 3 red, 4 yellow.
M21 Even Stevens: 1 of 2 red, even/odd tokens replace info tokens everywhere (setup, failed action, Equip 4 Sticky Note). 2p: 2 red.
M22 Ruled Out: 1 red, 4 yellow. Setup: instead of info token, each player takes 2 tokens of values NOT in their hand, placed next to stand (yellow token allowed; 2-stand players put 1 beside each stand; fewer if <2 missing values). When first 2 yellow cut: each player from captain clockwise chooses 1 Info token from board supply and gives to left neighbour, who places it correctly (or beside stand if no such wire).
M23 Small Town: 1 of 3 red, Number card. No equipment at setup: deck of 7 random equipment facedown on board. 1 random Number card faceup by mission card. The 4 wires of that value must be cut simultaneously via special action: identify all 4 (no equipment / Twin Probe); success = cut, fail = explode. Until done: at end of each round (before captain's turn) discard top equipment deck card. Once done: all remaining equipment deck cards faceup and immediately usable. 2p: 2 of 3 red.
M24 Head Count: 2 red, x1/x2/x3 tokens replace info tokens (setup, failures, Sticky Note). Token shows value present 1/2/3 times on that STAND (incl. cut wires); x2/x3 placed on any wire of that value. Not in front of red. With Sticky Note, token may go in front of a cut wire. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: Walkie-talkie on wire with token -> token discarded.
M25 Big Ears: 2 red. No speaking numbers aloud; must imply creatively (spelling, noises, fingers, charades). Saying number aloud -> detonator +1. 2p: 3 red.
M26 Dwindling Options: 2 red, all 12 Number cards faceup. Equip 10 X-or-Y Ray replaced if drawn. Turn: first flip one faceup Number card facedown (choice), then must cut (dual/solo) wires of that number. No visible numbers -> flip all faceup. All 4 cut -> remove card. If active player has no wire matching any visible number -> skip turn, no detonator. Else must cut matching visible number. 2p: ? (check)
 (M26: no 2p line on card)
M27 Putty: 1 red, 4 yellow. Characters dealt as usual to set captain, then all flipped facedown: no Twin Probes (no personal equipment). Equip 7 Recharge replaced if drawn. When first 2 yellow cut: randomly take Info tokens = #players, faceup line; from captain clockwise each picks 1 and places it correctly in front of own hand (or faceup beside stand if no match). 2p: captain no setup info token.
M28 Butterfingers: 2 red, 4 yellow. Captain's character card returned to box (no Twin Probe). Captain can't use Equipment cards or personal equipment; captain's failed dual cut -> explode. Can participate in Handsets(2)/Sweep(8). 2p: 3 red, 4 yellow.
M29 Read My Mind: 3 red, Number cards. Deal 2 Number cards facedown to each player (3 to player on captain's right), rest = deck. Turn: (1) player to RIGHT of active puts 1 of their Number cards facedown; (2) active plays turn; (3) reveal card: if active just cut wires of that value -> detonator +1; (4) active takes the revealed card into hand. Number card discarded once its 4 wires cut. When a player has only 1 card, draws from deck one at a time until a card with uncut value. When only 1 value left to cut, last Number card discarded immediately. 2p: captain no setup info. FAQ: detectors: only the actually cut wire counts; empty-handed players' cards go under deck; skip neighbours without cards; Coffee Break interplay.
M30 Runaway Bus: 1 of 2 red, 4 yellow, Number deck (12 facedown), audio Mission 30 before first turn. Rules from audio (unknown). Success -> open box 31-42.
M31 Handicap: 2 of 3 red, Constraint cards A-E faceup. Before info tokens, from captain clockwise each chooses 1 constraint faceup; extras discarded (<5p). Each must apply own constraint; when can't apply at START of own turn, flip it facedown and play normally rest of game. 2p advice: avoid A+B or C+D together. FAQ: once flipped stays off.
M32 Clown Tricks: 2 red, 12 Constraint cards shuffled deck, flip top. All must apply visible constraint. At start of each player's turn captain MAY (after consulting) replace visible constraint with next from deck. If active can't play due to constraint: say so, skip turn, no detonator. Constraints don't apply to Reveal Red. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: deck empty -> continue without constraint.
M33 Casino Royale: 2 of 3 red, even/odd tokens replace info tokens (setup, failed action, "you make a mistake", Sticky Note). 2p: 3 red.
M34 Mole Hunt: 1 red, constraints A-E. Captain decided normally, then character cards (incl Captain) shuffled and dealt facedown; constraints A-E shuffled, 1 facedown each; secretly look. Captain-card holder = weakest link, secret; only they apply constraint, without revealing. While characters hidden, no personal equipment. If weakest link can't play due to constraint: detonator +2 and everyone discards constraint and character cards. Others at start of their turn may accuse: name weakest link + describe constraint (no consulting). Wrong (either) -> detonator +1. Right -> all characters faceup, constraints discarded, personal equipment immediately usable. 2p: IMPOSSIBLE (3-5 only).
M35 Hard-Wired: 2 of 3 red, 4 yellow, X tokens. BEFORE shuffling red/yellow in, each player gets 1 blue wire facedown per stand, placed unsorted at far right with X token; then shuffle rest and deal. X wires cut normally (dual/solo) but only after all 4 yellow cut. Equipment can't be used on X wires (ignore all incl. personal, 5, 8). Equip 2 replaced if drawn. 2p: 3 red, 4 yellow.
M36 Beach Panic: 1 of 3 red, 2 yellow, Number cards, Sequence card side A. Deal 5 Number cards in faceup line (no reorder). Captain chooses (no consulting) which end gets Sequence card, arrow toward line. Values on visible Number cards must be cut in order from sequence card end; each time 2 wires of the adjacent number are cut, remove that card and the cutter chooses (no consulting) to put Sequence card at either end. Other values any time. 2p: 2 of 3 red, 4 yellow.
M37 Moving Goalposts: 2 red, 12 constraint deck, flip first. All must apply visible constraint on their turn. Each time 4 of a value cut -> replace constraint with next. Active can't play due to constraint -> say so, skip, no detonator; if nobody can play for a whole round -> detonator +1 and constraint replaced. Deck empty -> no more constraints. Not applied to Reveal Red. 2p: 3 red.
M38 Blind Spot: 2 red. After dealing, Captain flips 1 own wire without looking, puts it at far right facing teammates. Only captain may cut it, with a regular cut, no equipment/Twin Probe; if that cut fails -> explode. Others can't cut it; if a player's only option is to cut it, they skip turn and detonator +1. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: if it's red, captain reveals it when only reds remain.
DIAL (from M41 card art crop): 7 segments clockwise: purple (back-arrow+warning icon), green 5 bunnies, yellow 4, yellow-orange 3, orange 2, red-orange blank, skull. Start on segment for player count. TTS script (Brawlboxgaming) models position=N players, -1 per fail, 0 = KABOOM; M41/55/60/62 set position 1; M51 5p -> 6 (purple). => N players: N-1 survivable fails, Nth explodes.
M39 The Noble Four: 2 of 3 red, 4 yellow, Number cards. No equipment cards. One Number card faceup on first equipment space. 8 random Number cards facedown as deck. Setup info tokens random (place correctly or beside hand; yellow redrawn). 4 wires of faceup Number must be cut simultaneously by special action: indicate & cut all 4 (no personal equipment), even if you hold none; fail = explode. Until done: end of each round before captain's turn discard top Number deck card. After: deal remaining deck cards facedown equally from captain clockwise; each player places 1 Info token matching a value from their received cards near their stand (ignore if no tokens of value left). [unclear: "near their tile stand" = pointing to a matching wire? likely in front of matching wire]. 2p: 3 red, 4 yellow.
M40 Yippee-Ki-Yay: 3 red, x1/x2/x3 tokens replace info everywhere (like 24). Sticky Note may target cut wire. 2p: captain no setup token.
M41 Tripwire Tango: 1 of 3 red, yellow = #players (max 4) as TRIPWIRES not shuffled: deal 1 facedown to each player (5p: captain gets none). Detonator set to space 1 (one before skull). Setup info tokens random (correct or beside hand). Hidden Compartment (yellow equipment) replaced if drawn. Yellow not cut normally, one by one: special action: point at a teammate's tripwire; success -> revealed, detonator moves BACK 1; fail -> info token placed, detonator +1. Player with only own tripwire (+any red) skips turn. 2p: 2 of 3 red, 2 yellow. FAQ: if designated special wire is red -> explode.
M42 Big Top: 1 of 3 red, 4 yellow, audio Mission 42 before first round. Rules via audio (unknown). Success -> box 43-54.
M43 Robo-Pal: 3 red, the robot standee on board validation track space 1. Deal some wires facedown to the robot (unseen): 2p 5, 3-4p 4, 5p 3. End of each player's turn the robot advances 1 space; at 12 turns around (11,10...) [bounces]. When active player cuts wires matching the robot's current space number, they take a wire from the robot (random? "a wire") without showing, insert in sorted order in their hand (2 stands: choose stand). Win needs all wires cut incl the robot's. Coffee Break doesn't stop the robot. 2p: captain draws setup info token randomly.
M44 Deep Dive: 1 of 3 red, Oxygen tokens: 2 per player in Reserve (on card back). Equip 10 Two-Value Probe replaced; don't use the new character with X-or-Y-Ray personal equipment. To do a cut action, first take oxygen from reserve per depth of value: 1-4 = 1, 5-8 = 2, 9-12 = 3 tokens [yellow? unclear]. At start of Captain's turn all oxygen returns to reserve. If can't play for lack of oxygen: skip turn, detonator +1. No speaking; only sign = thumbs up for needing more oxygen. FAQ: Damper can fake cut zone 1 when all cut; voluntary pass = detonator +1; Damper can cancel pass penalty.
M45 Volunteers: 2 red, Number deck. Equip 10 & 11 replaced; no X-or-Y-Ray character. No clockwise order: each turn Captain reveals 1 Number card; first player (incl captain) to say "Snip!" must cut that value. 4 of value cut -> discard card; deck empty -> reshuffle remaining. Snip without correct value, or saying anything else -> detonator +1. If nobody volunteers, captain picks a player (or self); if they lack the value they place 1 Info token of their choice in front of stand and detonator +1. Player with only reds may say Snip anytime to Reveal Red. 2p: 3 red.
M46 Double-Oh: 0 red, 4 yellow using values 5.1, 6.1, 7.1, 8.1 (fixed). Equip 7 replaced. All 7s must be cut last. Special action: when player has only 7s left at start of turn (presumably only 7s, maybe yellow?), they must cut all four 7s at once (identify the others among teammates); fail = explode. 7 found earlier = normal failed action (info token + detonator +1). 2p: captain no setup info token.
M47 Mental Math: 2 of 3 red, all 12 Number cards faceup in 2 rows. Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. To cut, choose 2 available Number cards, add or subtract them to get value to cut, then discard both. No cards left -> spread all 12 again. Can't/won't play -> detonator +1. 2p: 3 red. [Unclear: whether failed cut still discards cards; whether target must be in 1-12; yellow cuts?]
M48 Three Amigos: 2 red, 3 yellow dealt 1 each clockwise from captain (2p: captain gets 2, one per stand), then rest dealt. Yellow not cut normally. Special action: cut all 3 yellow at once (point at 3 wires); with 4-5p possible without holding yellow. Fail: all indicated wires get Info token, detonator +1 only. 2p: 3 red, 3 yellow.
M49 Bottle Post: 2 red, Oxygen tokens visible in front of each: 2p 7 each, 3p 6, 4p 5, 5p 4. Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. To cut a wire of value V, first give V oxygen tokens to a single teammate (any teammate). Not enough -> skip turn, detonator +1. No speaking; thumbs up only. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: players who are out lose remaining oxygen; voluntary skip = detonator +1. [yellow cost unknown]
M50 Lights Out: 2 red, 2 yellow. Validation tokens and red/yellow markers removed. Everyone examines red & yellow wires in play before shuffling (values known). Setup info: point at wire, then place token beside hand (not in front). Failed cut: info token placed next to stand, not in front of wire. No sharing memorized info. 2p: 3 red, 4 yellow.
M51 Yes, Sir!: 1 red, Number deck. Detonator moved back 1 (as if 1 more player; 5p -> purple space). Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. Active = "Sir/Ma'am": flip top Number; without consulting choose who (even self) must cut that value; chosen says "Yes Sir/Ma'am" and does the cut. Next Sir = left of previous Sir. If chosen lacks the value: they choose 1 Info token to place in front of their stand [true info, choice of wire?] and detonator +1. 4 of value cut -> discard card; deck empty -> reshuffle. Only red at start of turn -> Reveal Red. 2p: 2 red, captain no setup token.
M52 Two-Faced: 3 red. Equip 1 (Unequal Tag) and 12 (Equal Tag) replaced. Setup: each puts 2 Info tokens, both FALSE (value doesn't match pointed wire); pointed wires may be blue or red. All Info tokens false all mission. Failed dual cut: owner places token with the STATED value. 2p: 3 red, 4 yellow.
M53 Robo-Rogue: 2 red, the robot placed just before space 1. Detonator dial not used. Equip 6 Rewind & 9 Damper replaced. End of each turn the robot moves: successful cut +1; successful cut whose value equals the robot's current space -1; failed cut +2. The robot reaching 12 = explode. 2p: 3 red. [Unclear: info token on failure still placed - yes presumably; non-cut actions (reveal red) -> no move?]
M54 Red Tide: 11 red (ALL) not shuffled: facedown pile on card back; audio Mission 54 before first round (rules for red pile via audio, unknown). Oxygen visible in front: 2p 9 each, 3p 6, 4p 3, 5p 2; rest = central reserve. Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. Cut costs by depth 1-4:1, 5-8:2, 9-12:3, paid to reserve. Can't pay at start of turn -> skip, detonator +1. Each time a Validation token is placed, every player takes 1 Oxygen from reserve. Success -> open 55-66. FAQ: must play if able.
M55 Nope Dares You: 2 red, Challenge cards = #players faceup. Detonator set to space 1 (adjacent to skull). Completing a challenge -> discard it, detonator back 1. Challenge = condition (individual stand arrangement, or team cut sequence). 2p: 2 of 3 red.
M56 Haywire: 2 of 3 red, Number card icon in setup (FAQ ERRATUM: Number cards NOT used). Each player flips 1 own wire unseen to far right facing teammates. Each must cut own flipped wire themselves with regular cut, no equipment/Twin Probe; failing that cut -> explode. A player may dual-cut a teammate's flipped wire (choice/necessity) but detonator +1 when they do [even on success]. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: flipped red -> reveal when only reds remain.
M57 Self-Destruct: 1 red, all 12 Number cards faceup each paired with a random faceup Constraint card. Equip "10 (Vaporiser)" replaced [card text says Vaporiser for 10 - maybe 10-10 card? check]. Each time a Validation token placed, put the constraint paired with that number on board (on top of previous; only 1 active). All must apply active constraint. Can't play due to constraint: say so, skip (no detonator); if nobody can play for a whole round -> EXPLODE. Not applied to Reveal Red. 2p: 2 red.
M58 Probe Party: 2 red. All Info tokens returned to box (none used: no setup tokens, no tokens on fail). Equip 4 Sticky Note & 7 Recharge replaced. No new characters: everyone has Twin Probe. Failed cut gives no info. Twin Probe usable every turn (unlimited). 2p: 3 red.
M59 Robot Guide: 2 of 3 red, 12 Number cards faceup in a random line; the robot placed on card 7 facing the longer side. Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. To cut: (1) leave the robot or move it forward (in facing direction only) to a Number card matching a value in your hand; (2) cut using the robot's current value; (3) choose the robot's facing (rotate 180 or keep). If no cut possible with values under/ahead of the robot: skip, detonator +1, rotate the robot 180. 4 of value cut -> flip that Number card facedown [stays in line; presumably the robot can pass over/land?]. Coffee Break skips a whole turn. 2p: 3 red.
M60 Nope Returns: 2 of 3 red, Challenge cards = #players faceup, detonator at space 1. Challenge completed -> discard, detonator back 1. 2p: 3 red.
M61 Pass It On: 1 red, constraints A-E: each player random one faceup; 2p: 2 extra A-E faceup, one at captain's left, one at right; 3p: 1 extra at captain's left. Each must apply own constraint; can't -> skip (no detonator); nobody can play for a whole round -> EXPLODE. Each round before captain's turn team may agree to rotate constraint cards one place, choosing clockwise or counterclockwise (extras included in rotation presumably). Any time a player may discard own constraint and take a random one from F-L, detonator +1. Not applied to Reveal Red. 2p: 2 red.
M62 Meteor Run: 2 red, Number cards = #players faceup; detonator at space 1. Each time all 4 wires of a faceup Number value are cut -> detonator back 1. 2p: 3 red.
M63 Unsinkable: 2 red, Captain takes oxygen: 2p 14, 3p 18, 4p 24, 5p 30. Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. To cut value V, put V oxygen into reserve (on card back). End of turn active gives all remaining oxygen to left neighbour. After each round captain starts turn by taking all oxygen from reserve. Not enough -> skip, detonator +1. No speaking, thumbs up only. 2p: 3 red. FAQ: must play if able.
M64 Double Haywire: 1 red. Each player flips 2 wires unseen; following teammates' instructions, lower value to far left, higher to far right. Must cut own flipped wires themselves (regular cut, no equipment/DD); fail -> explode. Dual-cutting a teammate's flipped wire allowed but detonator +1. 2p: 2 red. FAQ: 2 stands -> only 2 flipped total: lowest at far left of stand 1, highest far right of stand 2.
M65 Hot Potato: 3 red, all 12 Number cards dealt as equally as possible faceup in front of players (5p: captain & left neighbour 3, others 2). Equip 10 replaced; no Two-Value Probe char. A cut must target a value matching one of your Number cards. No matching wire in hand -> skip, detonator +1. Always at end of turn, give 1 of your Number cards to a teammate (your choice). 4 of value cut -> flip that card facedown (facedown cards still in front; only faceup can be given? card says faceup = numbers you can give). Coffee Break lets you skip your turn. 2p: IMPOSSIBLE (3-5).
M66 Bunker Showdown: 2 red, 2 yellow, BUNKER card (double-sided floors), constraint A-E, crew standee, audio Mission 66. Standee on helicopter space of bunker. Shuffle A-E: 4 faceup adjacent to each side of bunker card (N/E/S/W directions), 5th = "ACTION" constraint. After each cut action (success or fail), MUST move standee 1 space toward a constraint (side) that the cut satisfied (e.g. cut 10 -> toward EVEN or 7-12). Cut yellow only when told to. Walls block; if all possible moves blocked, stay. Striped (hatched) space: must do a SUCCESSFUL cut meeting ACTION constraint to do an ACTION (explained by audio), don't move. Dark stairs space: flip card to change floor, standee on staircase, keep orientation N. Icon space: detonator +1. Solo cut of 4 = 2 separate cuts (2 moves/actions). Audio before first round. FAQ: must move after any cut.
