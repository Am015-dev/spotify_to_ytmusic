Short Fuse

A browser version of a co-operative deduction game about defusing bombs. Game rules and numbers follow the published game. Names, card text and art are original: every picture, card and token is drawn in code, and every text is our own.

Fonts: Lilita One and Nunito (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License). 3D: three.js (MIT).
