# Short Fuse: cast and story arc (all words: game/story.js for the tutorial, quick game and help; campaign.json for Story chapters)

## Cast (original; portraits are painted, in games/short-fuse/media/camp-*.webp)
- **Foreman Brix**: the guide. Warm, gruff, dry. Runs the demolition crew, teaches the prologue, speaks in chapters 1-4. Catch phrase: "Everybody breathes."
- **Vesper Quill, the Clockmaker**: the rival. Builds the devices, tapes a brass clock key on each one. Polite, smug, curious about the crew. Reason to compete: he wants to see
  whether a crew can beat a fuse by *talking* (his machines assume nobody trusts anybody).
- **Tally** (clipboard, counts the fuse), **Wren** (Act 2-3 teammate), **The Training Rig**, **The Weak Link** (secret saboteur, boss of chapter 7).
- Your computer crew in a quick game: **Teal** and **Plum** (and Amber, you).

## Prologue (the tutorial, see TUTORIAL-COVERAGE.md): "The Training Rig"
Quill taped a ticking practice rig to the shop door. Brix teaches you on it, one real turn at a time. Quill speaks twice: when the first guess misses ("Tick, tick") and at the red wire ("Never point at red").
Ends when the rig falls silent and Quill's brass key drops on the step: "You are on the crew." Leads into chapter 1.

## Three acts (Story mode, 10 chapters; each has an intro scene, an outro scene, and a praise/taunt line on the result screen for a win or a loss)
1. **Boot Camp** (c1-c4): New hire on Brix's crew. Blue wires, then yellow, then the red one, then the graduation rig (boss).
2. **Calling Cards** (c5-c7): Devices turn up around town signed with a brass key. A fibber on the crew (false tokens), and the Weak Link boss (secret rule, secret face).
3. **The Clock Tower** (c8-c10): Quill's last works. Ground floor is a gift (no reds), then the foreman's blind spot, then the Masterpiece boss (twelve numbers, twelve rules).

## Quick game voice
One flavour line on the brief card ("Quill left his brass key on this one. Prove him wrong."), one result line on the end card (win: dry and warm; loss: Quill laughing or Brix: "Everybody breathes. Again.").
