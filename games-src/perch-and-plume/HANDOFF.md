# Perch & Plume HANDOFF (W342)

Bird-collecting engine-builder for 1-5 players (solo vs the Rival Flock). Branch `shelf/W342-perch-and-plume`, not deployed.

## What plays
- Title (Story, Quick game, How to play, tutorial button first on a first visit, Continue when a save exists).
- Quick game: 1-5 players, each seat Computer or Person (hot-seat with a pass screen), easy/normal/hard. 1 player = solo vs the Rival Flock.
- Full base rules (`rules-notes.md`): starting hand (keep 5 of birds + food, 1 of 2 bonus cards), 4 rounds of 8/7/6/5 actions, the
  three growing rows with their trade spaces, dice birdfeeder (reroll when empty / all one face), tray of 3, egg cost by column,
  any-2-for-1 food, brown powers right to left, white when played, pink between turns, round goals (green scoring), bonus cards, final score.
- Centrepiece: using a row fires its brown powers bird by bird from right to left; each bird pulses, its result pops up and its
  eggs / cached food / tucked cards appear on that card at that moment (`flow.js` `playFx` + `setSlot`).
- Story: 10 chapters, 3 acts, a boss per act (`campaign.json`, `CAMPAIGN-DESIGN.md`), map, saved progress, "make it easier" after 2 losses.
- Tutorial: 13 staged steps (`tutor.js`), offered first on a first visit and from the story map's Tutorial button and the Menu.
- Help: Rules drawer, Goals drawer (goals, live counts, bonus cards), Log drawer, opponents' boards (tap a chip), All cards reference,
  first-time coach bubbles and the hint bulb (the computer's own choice + a why). Synth sound effects with an on/off switch; music
  slots (`music/<slot>-a|b.mp3`, silent until files exist).

## Files
`src/` data.js (generated by gen-data.py), engine.js, ai.js, art.js (asset slots + placeholders), render.js, flow.js, screens.js,
shelfkit.js (sound, music, help, story, boot), tutor.js, scn.js, head.html, body.html. `build.py` -> perch-and-plume.html.
Tests: rules-test.js (node), smoke.js, play.js (real taps: full game, tutorial, story c1-c2), shot.js (screens).

## Found -> fixed (playing it myself)
1. Hard AI drew cards 274 of 520 turns and lost to easy (cards valued even when never playable) -> hand and food only count as far as the turns left can use them; hard now averages 62 vs 40 before.
2. 5-player games stalled when deck, discard and tray were all empty during a draw -> the draw is skipped with a log line.
3. Rival Flock's hard bonus was counted as tucked cards (card-count invariant broke) -> separate bonus points.
4. PC: empty slots stretched to 180x190 px -> slot width follows the row height (card shape).
5. Tray cards collapsed to thin bars -> cards fill their tray boxes.
6. Title showed a rounded placeholder box as background -> background slots draw nothing until the picture exists.
7. Round toasts overlapped the result table -> cleared when the result opens.
8. Board had 3 instruction-like lines (dock title "Your turn", prompt, coach bubble) -> dock title is "Round n of 4 · you · n actions left", bubble texts are explanations.
9. Eggs scenario used a bird filter that matched nothing -> birds without brown/pink powers.
10. guard.py: numbered ui files not recognised -> named files in an explicit build list; game added to modmap.GAMES.
