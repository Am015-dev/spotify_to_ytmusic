# Gloamwar: screen layout to copy (structure only; our own art and names)
No official PC screenshots found: use the common conventions of polished digital map wargames.
1. The MAP fills the screen (pan/zoom, pinch on phone). Regions show nation colour, army counts as stacked figure tokens with a number badge, strongholds/cities as icons. Selected region/unit gets a glowing outline and a floating action menu next to it.
2. Top bar: slim: turn/round, track meters (the corruption/progress track as a horizontal bar with the marker), and icon buttons: menu, log, rules, cards. Nothing else.
3. Bottom: an action-dice tray (the dice you rolled, tappable, spent ones dim) and a card hand fan; tap a die or card to inspect in a panel that stays until closed (X >= 44 px) and shows what it can do right now.
4. Side panels (opened from icons, closeable): fellowship/progress, nations' status, event decks, rules, log. Never leave them permanently on screen on a phone.
5. A single contextual prompt line ('Pick an army to move.') at the top of the map; legal targets highlighted, everything else dimmed.
Phone: map is the whole screen; tray and hand as slim bars; panels as bottom sheets with a X.
