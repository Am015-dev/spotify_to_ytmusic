# Gloamwar: rules notes

Gloamwar copies the rules and component structure of the top-ranked two-player epic fantasy war game (second edition). Everything creative is our own: the world, map geography, nations, characters and their abilities, and all 96 event cards. Sources: the official second-edition rulebook text and the official action-dice reference sheet (read for mechanics only; nothing copied). Marks: **[unverified]** = could not be confirmed from text, chosen from memory and logged here.

## Sides and goal
- Two sides: the **Dawn League** (5 nations) against **the Gloam** (3 nations).
- Dawn wins at once when the Company takes the 5th step of the Ashen Road with taint below 12, or at the end of a turn holding Gloam cities/strongholds worth **4 VP**.
- Gloam wins at once when taint reaches **12**, or at the end of a turn holding Dawn cities/strongholds worth **10 VP**. City 1 VP, stronghold 2 VP, towns and fortifications 0.

## Turn
1. Recover dice (the Dawn takes back dice in the hunt box; record how many) and each side draws one card from each of its two decks. Hand limit 6.
2. Company phase (Dawn): declare the Company (move it up to its unseen steps; in a Dawn-held city or stronghold heal 1 taint and wake that nation), change the guide, or enter the Ashen Road from Blackgate or Wraithpass.
3. Hunt allocation (Gloam): 0 to (companions in the Company) dice into the hunt box; at least 1 if the Dawn took dice back this turn; always may put 1.
4. Roll: Dawn rolls all its dice; Gloam rolls the rest; every Gaze result goes to the hunt box.
5. Actions: Dawn first, then alternate one die each. A side with fewer dice left may pass. When one side is out, the other uses the rest. Lamps: once per turn change a die to another face (Dawn: not to Will; the used Lamp passes to the Gloam, who may turn one die into the Gaze, once).
6. On the Ashen Road, if the Company neither moved nor hid this turn: +1 taint. Then the military victory check.

## Dice
- Dawn faces: Character ×2, Muster, Muster/Army, Event, Will of the Dawn. Starts with 4 dice, +1 for Kestrel Crowned, +1 for Orrin the Kindled (max 6).
- Gloam faces: Character, Army, Muster, Muster/Army, Event, the Gaze **[unverified: the rulebook text only confirms the Dawn die; the Gloam split is one of each]**. Starts with 7, +1 per minion in play (max 10).
- Character: move the Company (+1 step, hunt, die into hunt box), hide it, separate companions, move companions (Dawn); fly all Gloamriders and move minions (Gloam); move or attack with one army that has a leader or character; play a character card.
- Army: move up to two armies one region each, or attack with one army (siege and sortie included), or play an army card.
- Muster: stir one friendly nation one step (sleeping Dawn nations stop one short of war), or raise troops in free settlements of nations at war (1 elite, or 2 regulars, or 2 leaders, or 1 regular + 1 leader, each in a different settlement; Gloamriders only in Pale Throne strongholds), or bring a minion in (Gloam), or play a muster card.
- Event: draw a card from either deck, or play any card. Will of the Dawn: any face, or bring Kestrel Crowned / Orrin the Kindled into play.

## Politics
- Track of 4 boxes (3, 2, 1 steps, At War). Start: Greenhold, Deepforge, Hearthlands, Varrn 3 steps; Aurelmont 2; Pale Throne and Corvel 1; Sundered Hosts 2. Greenhold and all Gloam nations start awake.
- +1 step each time an army of the nation is attacked and each time one of its settlements is captured. Woken by: an attack, an enemy army entering its land, the Company declared in its city/stronghold, a companion who can wake it arriving there (Orrin, Kestrel, Pell, Hobb wake any nation; Darian, Sylwen, Brokk their own).
- Nations not at war cannot attack, cannot muster with dice, and their units cannot enter another nation's region.

## Armies and battle
- Stack limit 10 units; behind stronghold walls 5. Dawn losses are gone for good; Gloam losses return to reserves.
- Battle dice: one per unit up to 5, hit on 5+ (natural 1 misses, natural 6 hits). Leaders and character leadership re-roll misses up to 5. Each round both sides may play one card for its combat half (initiative order, defender first on ties).
- Cities and fortifications: attacker needs 6s in round 1. A stronghold's defenders may fall back behind the walls before a round (the attack ends; the attacker may move in and lay siege). Storming the walls: attacker hits on 6, one round only unless the attacker turns an elite into a regular to press on. Sortie: the garrison attacks the besiegers as a field battle.
- One hit removes a regular or turns an elite into a regular; two hits remove an elite. Leaders and characters die with their last unit. After each round the attacker may stop; if not, the defender may retreat to a free neighbour. A winner may advance; entering an enemy settlement captures it.

## The Company and the Hunt
- Starts at Lanternvale, hidden, progress 0, taint 0, guide Orrin, seven companions.
- Moving: +1 step, then the Gloam rolls its hunt dice (max 5): each 6 succeeds, with +1 per Dawn die already in the box this turn. One re-roll each for a Gloam-held stronghold, a Gloam army, Gloamriders in the Company's region. Success draws a hunt tile.
- Tiles: number = taint; the Gaze = number of successes (on the Ashen Road: every die in the hunt box). Reveal marks reveal the Company: it moves up to its steps (not into a Dawn city/stronghold) and must hide before moving again. Damage may be cut by letting the guide or a random companion fall (cut = level).
- Hunt pool: 16 standard tiles **[unverified split: Gaze ×4, 0 ×2, 1 ×2, 1+reveal ×2, 2, 2+reveal ×2, 3 ×3]** plus 8 special tiles (4 Dawn, 4 Gloam; every Gloam special tile stops a road step) added by cards and entering the pool on the Ashen Road.
- Ashen Road: no hunt roll, every move draws a tile, a stop tile blocks the step, 5 steps to the Kiln. Companions cannot leave there.
- When no companions remain the Hollow Wretch joins as guide.

## Characters (our own designs, same levels and leadership as the original's)
- Orrin (3/1), Kestrel (3/1), Darian (2/1), Sylwen (2/1), Brokk (2/1), Pell (1/1), Hobb (1/1); Kestrel Crowned (3/2), Orrin the Kindled (3/1), the Hollow Wretch (0/0).
- Minions: Vael the Brass Magus (never leaves Corvel Tower), the Wraith-Marshal (flies like a Gloamrider), the Voice of the Throne (moves 3). Each adds a Gloam die. Entry conditions are ours, in the spirit of the original.

## Simplified on purpose
- Casualties are taken automatically (regulars first, then elites stepped down), not chosen hit by hit. The choice almost never matters and it saves several taps per battle.
- An attacking army attacks with all its units of nations at war; there is no separate rearguard split before a battle. Army moves offer "all" or "leave one regular behind".
- A revealed Company draws extra tiles for Gloam strongholds only at its start and end regions, not along the whole path (the Dawn always chooses a path around them).
- Event decks never reshuffle (as in the original); played cards are discarded face up.
- Online and hot-seat play are not built; you play either side against the computer.
