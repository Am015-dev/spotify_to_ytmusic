## Final report

**Result:** lost 20 to 23 against Olive (computer, normal). The game lasted 5 rounds and about 60 actions. Guided game was on and I pressed Suggest on most turns.

### 1. The goal and a round, in my own words
Goal: finish with the most stars. Each turn you tap a kiln (a round plate of 4 tiles) or the courtyard in the middle. You take every tile of one colour from it and put them all on one of your 5 racks. Rack 1 holds 1 tile and rack 5 holds 5. A rack takes only one colour, and only a colour that row of your mosaic doesn't already have. What's left in a kiln slides into the courtyard. Tiles that don't fit go to the breakage strip, which costs -1, -1, -2, -2, -2, -3, -3. The round ends when every kiln and the courtyard are empty. Then each full rack moves one tile into its mosaic row and scores. The game ends after the round in which someone completes a mosaic row. At the end you get +2 per full row, +7 per full column and +10 per glaze (colour) placed five times.

### 2. Why I won or lost points
- **+ placing a tile:** +1 when it touches nothing. Otherwise +1 for each tile in the row and column it joins ("2 across + 3 down" = +5). My best placements were +7 and +6 in the last round.
- **- breakage:** extra tiles, tiles that fit no rack, and the forced last pick of a round. This cost me -27 over the game, against -22 for Olive. Two forced dumps made most of it: 5 garnet in R2 and 5 cobalt in R4.
- **- Sun token:** the first player to take from the courtyard gets it. It sits in your first breakage slot (-1), and you go first next round.
- **+2 complete row** at the end. Each of us had one.
- Columns (+7) and full glazes (+10): neither of us earned any.
- My score floored at 0 in round 2: +8 tiles, the summary showed broken -8, and I stayed at 0.
- My tiles scored 45, more than Olive's 43, so I lost only on breakage.

### 4. "What just happened?" moments
- The broken values in the round summaries didn't match the previews (-12 shown, -8 applied in R2; -10 shown, -11 applied in R4).
- After the Sun token appeared in my breakage, my score didn't visibly change, because I was already at 0.
- Round 3 ended right after my pick. Olive's last move (taking the last cobalt) was never shown.
- The rack 5 tile colour wasn't the one I meant (see 8 above).
- The +5 for a single obsidian in R2 felt like magic until I read "2 across + 3 down".

### 5. Too much on screen
- The bottom panel holds turn text, the win reminder, my whole board, the last move line, a round title and Olive's bar. During the rack chooser, my mosaic is hidden, so I can't see why "mosaic row has it" applies.
- The mosaic ghost icons are tiny at phone size, and the colours are hard to tell apart in the faded state (frost and cobalt especially).
- The round summary is fine, but the "Row N: +x = a across + b down" lines crowd the right column.

### 6. Options I couldn't reach or that were hidden
- The menu "Close" button didn't respond to a text tap. I had to tap the x icon.
- The Sun token rule is only explained in the rack chooser footer, and only on a turn where you'd take it.
- "Unmarked mosaic" and "Prism tiles" variants were never explained in a way I'd understand.
- There was no undo after placing a rack.
- Graphics ("Auto · Low"), Tile list and "See the boards" were reachable, but casual me never needed them.
- I couldn't find any way to see in advance how much the end-of-round leftovers would cost me.

### 7. Fun verdict: 3/5
The tiles look gorgeous, the kilns are tactile, and the Suggest button with its "+4 / 1 break -1" previews let me play without reading rules. Placements that join rows feel great (+7!). What hurt was the breakage: two rounds wiped out by a forced last pick I had no real say in. Losing 20-23 when my tiles outscored Olive's felt unfair rather than like my own mistake, and the game never taught me how to avoid it. Final score: Coral 20, Olive 23 (loss).
