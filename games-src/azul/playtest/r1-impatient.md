## 1. The goal and a round, in my own words
Goal: get the most points by building a 5x5 tile wall. On your turn you take every tile of one colour from one kiln, or from the courtyard in the middle. The other tiles in that kiln slide to the courtyard. You put the tiles on one of 5 racks (rack N holds N tiles of one colour). Extras go to the breakage row and cost points. The first player to take from the courtyard also gets the Sun token, which costs -1 but lets you start next round. When every kiln and the courtyard is empty, each full rack moves 1 tile onto the wall. It scores 1 point, plus the length of the connected lines it touches. Broken tiles subtract points. The game ends after the round in which someone finishes a wall row. Then bonuses come in: +2 for each row and more for columns and full colours (I got about +10 that I couldn't see listed).

## 6. Fun verdict
**3.5/5.** Once I figured out "tap a tile, tap a rack", it was smooth and quick. The preview popup ("fills +5", "2 tiles -2") let me play well without reading any rules, and the round summaries with +N badges made scoring feel fair. Points off: the "best" badge sometimes seems wrong, the AI moves too fast to follow, breakage spikes felt harsh, and the ending (a clipped breakdown, a big unexplained bonus) was anticlimactic.
**Final score: Coral (me) 63, Olive (computer, normal) 54. Win.**
