## Report

### 1. The goal and a round, in my own words
Goal: score the most points by filling your 5x5 palace mosaic. Each round, the kilns are filled with 4 tiles each. On your turn you take every tile of one colour from one kiln (the rest of that kiln slides to the middle courtyard), or every tile of one colour from the courtyard. The first player to take from the courtyard also gets the Sun token: −1, but they go first next round. Tiles go onto one drying rack (rack N holds N tiles of a single colour). Whatever doesn't fit falls into breakage, which costs points. When every kiln and the courtyard is empty, each full rack moves one tile into its fixed spot in the mosaic and scores for the tiles it touches. When someone completes a horizontal mosaic row, the game ends after that round, with bonuses for rows, columns and colour sets.

### 6. Fun verdict: 3/5
The drafting sheet (colour chips, rack buttons with "fills +N" and breakage cost, "★ best") makes every turn quick and readable, and Olive denying me tiles adds real tension. It loses points because the guided game stops after step 1, "best" sometimes contradicts the obvious rack, scoring numbers appear without explanation, and the ending came as a surprise because I misread the ghost tiles. I'd play again now that I know the rules. A first-timer might feel lost around rounds 2 to 4.
