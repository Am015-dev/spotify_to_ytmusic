## Final report

### 1. The goal and a round, in my own words
Score the most stars. Each turn you grab ALL tiles of one colour from one kiln (plate) or from the centre courtyard; the rest of that kiln slides to the centre. You drop them on one of 5 racks (rack N holds N tiles, one colour per rack, and that colour can't already be on that wall row). Leftovers that don't fit break (minus points). When every tile is gone the round ends: each FULL rack sends one tile to its spot on the 5x5 wall and scores for the line(s) it touches; unfilled racks carry over. First to take from the centre gets the Sun token (goes first next round, but it takes a breakage slot). Game ends after the round in which anyone finishes a wall row; then +2 per full row, +7 per full column, +10 per colour with all 5.

### 2. Why I won/lost points
- + tile placed alone: +1 (row1 cobalt, row5 cobalt, row3 garnet)
- + tile touching a line: +N for the run across plus run down (+2 "2 down", +6 "3 across + 3 down", +5 "5 across")
- - breakage: -1/-1/-2/-2... escalating per broken slot (forced -1 last garnet R4, -2 two saffrons R5, -1 cobalt overflow R3)
- - Sun token occupies a breakage slot (R3 "incl. Sun token -2")
- + end: full row +2 (I got 2), full column +7 (Olive 21, me 0), all 5 of a glaze +10 (Olive 10, me 0)
- Lost 38 vs 94: I filled rows across the top while Olive stacked columns; end bonuses (+31) plus Olive's bigger connected clusters each round (+20/+21/+22) buried me. I also ended the game while far behind.
