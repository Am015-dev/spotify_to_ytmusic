## Final report

### 1. Goal and a round, in my own words
Score the most stars. Each round the five kilns (plates) are filled with 4 tiles. On your turn you tap a tile: you take every tile of that colour (glaze) from that kiln (or from the middle courtyard) and put them all on one of your five racks (rack N holds N tiles of one colour); leftovers from a kiln slide into the courtyard. You keep going until every tile is gone. At round end each FULL rack moves one tile into the matching row of your 5x5 mosaic and scores 1 + 1 for each tile it touches in a straight line across/down. Tiles that didn't fit go to the breakage row and cost points. Game ends after the round in which someone completes a mosaic row; then +2 per full row, +7 per full column, +10 per colour done 5 times.

### 2. Why I gained/lost points
- Gained: setting a tile alone (+1); setting next to existing tiles (e.g. "+5 = 3 across + 2 down", "+8 = 4 across + 4 down"); end bonus +2 for my one complete row (row 2).
- Lost: overflow tiles to breakage (-1, -1, -2 ... escalating per space); Sun token (taking from the courtyard first) puts a -1 in your breakage row; Round 4 disaster: last 6 Garnet forced on me with no legal rack -> -14 in one round.
- Olive won 31-28 mostly on an extra complete row (+4 vs +2) and more tiles set (55 vs 45), despite breaking more (-28).
- Totals per round: me 2, 7, 13, 12, 28 final.
