## Final report

### 1. Goal and a round, in my words
Get the most stars. Each round you take turns grabbing all tiles of one colour from a kiln (the rest slide into the middle courtyard, which you can also take from). You drop them on one of 5 racks (rack N holds N tiles of one colour). When the kilns and courtyard are empty, every full rack slides one tile into your 5x5 mosaic in that row (a fixed spot for that colour) and you score; partial racks wait. Extra tiles you can't fit break and cost points. The game ends after a round where someone finishes a mosaic row, then end bonuses.

### 2. Why I won/lost points
- + Tile set into mosaic: +1 alone, or count of tiles in the touching line across + down (e.g. "+5 = 3 across + 2 down").
- - Breakage: overflow/unfittable tiles, escalating -1,-1,-2,-2,-2,-3,-3 (I lost 3 in R2 when 2 Cobalt had nowhere to go).
- - Sun token: first to take from the courtyard each round gets it, costs -1 (counted as broken) but you start next round.
- End: Rows +2 each complete row, Columns +7 each, Full glazes (all 5 of a colour) +10. I got 2; Olive got 2+14+10 = 26. That is why I lost: I led 37-33 before the end bonuses.

### 7. Fun verdict: 3/5
The pick-a-colour, pick-a-rack loop is fast and the rack panel with "fills +N / -N / best" is excellent for an impatient player; Suggest makes it near-autopilot. The round score breakdown ("3 across + 2 down") taught me scoring by example. But I lost to bonuses I never saw coming: I was ahead 37-33 and lost 39-59, which felt unfair rather than clever. If the bonuses were visible as I played (column/glaze progress), it would be 4/5.
