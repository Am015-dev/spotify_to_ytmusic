# Cold play (F4, 2026-11-20 UTC)

A separate tester with no knowledge of the history played by touch at 375x667 (tutorial + 2 games, one on Normal) and by mouse at 1366x768. It played the F4 build before the merge with the parallel F3 commits (the rival mirror, pick consequences, denial cheers, wall preview, bulb and PC layout were all in it). No game was played to the end. Verdict as given:

- Could you play it? Yes. Tutorial was short and clear, and I could play without help. None of the three games was finished: I stopped phone game 1 at the end of round 2, phone game 2 at the start of round 4, and the PC game in round 2. That means I never saw a final score or end screen.
- Fun 1-5: 3.5. The art is lovely. The play is quick and tactile.
- Would you play again? Probably. Choosing tiles is satisfying, and the +N previews make scoring easy to read. I never saw an ending, so I can't say whether it holds up over a full game.
- Did you feel you were playing against the other player? Yes, clearly.
  - A panel shows her wall and says things like "Tamsin needs 1 black for rack 3". Hovering a colour on PC, or tapping a colour on phone, shows what my take would do to her.
  - I took the 2 tiles she needed for a rack. A banner said "You forced 2 tiles to Tamsin's floor: -3, Denial pays off". I also took 3 reds she needed, and her next red move came up short.
  - She did the same back. She took the starter tile and blue tiles I was heading for, and later took a single blue straight to her floor, which looked like a block on me.
  - On phone game 2 she led 22 to 3 by round 4. The computer is strong at Normal.
- What confused you:
  1. On the phone I was never sure whether my screenshot was taken mid-animation. I had to wait 3-8 seconds for her turn.
  2. The kiln and the middle pool look alike. It took me a while to see that taking from the middle costs a -1 starter tile.
  3. The difficulty default differs: Easy on PC, Normal on phone. I'm not sure which phone game 1 used.
  4. I was careless and dropped tiles on my floor several times in phone game 2. The tiny "-1" and "-2" boxes along the floor are easy to miss.
- Phone vs PC: PC is nicer, because hovering shows what she needs and the opponent panel is always visible. On the phone that panel is cramped. The phone layout is fine otherwise, and the taps were big enough.
- Results: Phone 1 (difficulty unclear, probably Normal): 10 to 2 for me after round 2, unfinished. Phone 2 (Normal): 3 to 22 for her at round 4, unfinished. PC (Easy by default, I didn't change it): 2 to 0 for me in round 2, unfinished.
