DOORKICK DUNGEON - MUSIC AND SOUND PROMPTS
For a text-to-music tool (Suno, Udio, Stable Audio, ElevenLabs Music...). One prompt per track.
Rules: no vocals, never name artists/games/scores, check the tool's licence allows public game use,
and log each file in games-src/munch/ASSETS.md. Export WAV or 320 kbps MP3.

======================================================================
MUSIC LOOPS
======================================================================

1) music.tavern  (menu / title, new)
Cosy medieval tavern folk instrumental, 100 BPM, D major, 6/8 jig feel. Lute and fiddle lead with a light hand drum, a tin whistle answering, soft crowd-murmur ambience far in the background. Warm, cheeky and welcoming, like a game night about to start. Seamless loop, 90 seconds, no vocals.

2) music.main  (in game, replaces the current loop)
Light-hearted fantasy dungeon-crawl instrumental, 92 BPM, A minor with a playful major lift. Pizzicato strings, plucked lute, bassoon and tuba walking bass, soft tambourine. Sneaky and curious, slightly comic, never tense, sits under dialogue and card sounds without getting in the way. Seamless loop, 2 minutes, no vocals, no big crescendos.

3) music.fight  (fights with a level 12+ monster, new)
Comic-heroic fantasy battle instrumental, 132 BPM, E minor. Driving frame drums and taiko, low brass stabs, fast fiddle runs, a cheeky bassoon motif answering the brass. Exciting but funny, like a pub brawl with a dragon. Seamless loop, 60 seconds, no vocals.

4) music.victory  (win screen, new, not looped)
Triumphant medieval fanfare that turns into a tavern celebration, 120 BPM, D major. Brass fanfare opening, then lute, fiddle and hand drums in a merry jig, a crowd cheer at the end. 20 seconds with a clean ending, no vocals.

5) music.defeat  (lose screen, new, not looped)
Comic sad-trombone medieval lament, 70 BPM, D minor. A lone lute and a mournful bassoon, ending on a wry two-note tuba shrug. Funny, not depressing. 10 seconds with a clean ending, no vocals.
