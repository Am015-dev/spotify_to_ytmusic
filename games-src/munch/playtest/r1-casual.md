## 1. Goal and a turn
First to Level 10 wins (last level must come from killing a monster). On your turn: set up gear from hand, kick open the door. If a monster, fight it (add potions/gear/help from a rival or run away); if a curse it hits you; if nothing, loot the room (draw a card) and optionally fight a monster from hand. Then tidy hand to the limit and end turn. Between rivals' turns you can curse them or boost their monsters. Win a fight = level + treasure; die = lose all cards.
