Audio

Music: ten instrumental tracks made with Treblo (treblo.com). Art: paintings, portraits and fortune cards made with Google Flow. Sound effects, with thanks to these public-domain (CC0) creators:

  • Splashes, bubbles, pot and boil sounds: rubberduck (OpenGameArt, CC0)
  • Sound effects: Casino Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Music Jingles, RPG Audio, Sci-fi Sounds, UI Audio by Kenney (CC0)
  • Painted table: PixiJS (MIT licence)

All sounds were trimmed, loudness-normalised and converted to MP3 for this game. Names, card text and art are original.
