# Cold playtest F4 - Cauldron Fair (phone 375x667 touch: tutorial + full 9 days; PC 1366x768 mouse: days 1-3)
## Could I play it (phone / PC)
Phone: yes. Tutorial (30 steps) finished; the glowing target was always clear, and a full 9-day game finished in about 3 min of play (lost 31-60). PC: yes, days 1-3 by mouse, with no errors.
## Fun 1-5
3. Draw-or-stop with a live "60% explodes" meter is tense and readable. But my days 5-8 all exploded for 0 points and the game shrugged; the rival pulled away and I didn't feel I could fight back.
## Would I play again
Maybe. The art and pace are good. I'd come back only if I felt my bag choices mattered (see shop).
## What confused me
- The shop says "You have no yellows/blues/greens yet" even after I had bought that colour (Sunroot bought on day 2, still said on day 3; same for Wren Feather and Mossback). It looks wrong and makes me distrust the hint.
- My rubies were spent for me ("Drop moved +1, tap to undo") without my asking.
- Tapping a second chip of the same colour quietly swaps out the first one; nothing says why.
- Tutorial 18/30: a rat pop-up ("Close this to use all of it... Use none") sits on top of a bubble that says "draw". Two instructions at once.
- The day summary reads "Level at 0 points" right next to "+1".
- Tutorial end: the coach bubble covers the winner banner.
## What felt slow (with seconds)
Nothing. After Stop, the summary shows for about 1 s, then the shop opens by itself (0-1 s wait). The rival brews instantly. Each phone day took 10-30 s. If anything, the summary goes by too fast to read.
## Broken or clipped
- PC 1366x768 shop: the chips stick out below the bottom edge of each stall, so prices under the chips are cut off or hidden. Only Dusk Sigh shows its "8". On PC I could not see what things cost.
- Phone: nothing off-screen. The end screen shows the winner ("Odo wins the fair with 60 points"), the day-by-day table, and Play again at y=519, all without scrolling. No JS errors.
## Did the shop feel like a real decision
Not really. "Suggested" usually spends almost all coins on one chip (e.g. 9 of 10, 12 of 14), so "up to two chips" rarely happens. Only on day 7 did it suggest two. I stopped thinking and tapped Suggested. Its stale "you have none" reasons make it feel like autopilot, not advice. Stall info is short and good ("After a white: undo it").
