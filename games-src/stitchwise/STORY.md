# Stitchwise: story (W381)

All text is in `campaign.json` (cast, acts, chapter intros/outros, `outroLose`, and `story`: tutorial prologue, Aunt Wren's coach lines,
judging lines, Quick game lines). Original names only.

## Cast
- **Aunt Wren** (guide, 🧵 teal): retired fair champion who teaches you; warm, dry, a long grudge against Marden.
- **Marden** (tutorial and Quick normal rival): won the Parish Fair ribbon three years running, swears no newcomer will take it.
- **Poppy** (Quick easy) / **Dame Poppy** (Story 1): kind, brings cake, buys whatever fits.
- **Aurelia** (Quick hard) / **Queen Aurelia** (Story final boss): the champion who never leaves a square empty.
- Story opponents: Tilly Thimble, Granny Fernsby (boss, act 1), Odette Loom, Bram Seam, Marguerite Bodkin (boss, act 2), Ivo Selvage,
  Hester Pinwheel, Tobias Quillon, Queen Aurelia (boss, act 3).

## Arc: The Quilter's Year
- **Prologue (tutorial):** Aunt Wren takes you to the Parish Fair to beat Marden. Chapter 1 *Your first turn*, 2 *Pay day*, 3 *Fair
  surprises*, 4 *The judging* (climax: the empty squares are counted down, Aunt Wren's verdict).
- **Act 1 · Parish Fair** (learn the cloth): Dame Poppy, Tilly Thimble, boss Granny Fernsby.
- **Act 2 · County Fair** (tempo and income): Odette Loom, Bram Seam (moth twist), boss Marguerite Bodkin.
- **Act 3 · Grand Fair** (tidy to the last square): Ivo Selvage, Hester Pinwheel (head start), Tobias Quillon (tidy quilt), boss Queen Aurelia.

Every Story chapter has an intro (portrait + 2-3 lines with the stake and the twist rule), a win outro and now a loss outro
(`outroLose`, shown on the Story result with the speaker's name); bosses speak before and after.
Quick game: a one-line flavour intro per opponent in the panel until your first move, and a result line in the game's voice.
