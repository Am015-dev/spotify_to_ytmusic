A jump > 20 m onto a spot the player can see is hidden that frame, and the car respawns out of sight. Player contacts (< 8 m, hitT, tumble) are exempt. Out of sight a lag still closes at 1.5 m a step: a snap there was a 7-13 m step in why/solid-cars (d). ?iv=0 = off.
