# HANDOFF crash-6 (2026-10-17): instanced + cd meshes in low-mem GPU streaming, leak fix. src/98mf_memfree.js
1. 'cd' meshes (LDraw props, 23 MB, skipped by memFree) are now packed and streamed like crash-5 statics, with radii from their own cull distance (out at cd+400, back at cd+150; hidden beyond cd anyway). Shared cd geometry is left alone. Instanced meshes (50 MB) get packed arrays only (normal Int8, colour Uint8, index Uint16; MEMF_ipack). They are not distance-streamed because their instances follow the car or are drawn through the WB near proxies (originals are never drawn).
2. Leak: MEMF.gu was a Map from geometry to mesh, so every lazy zone freed by STR_lzFree kept its packed geometry forever. It is now a WeakMap. A heap sampling profile (tools/crash6/tGrow.js) shows only ~20 MB of JS objects alive from minute 1 to 5; the rest of the growth is typed arrays of the area being driven (lazy zones, LF tiles, gndGen), mostly built during the race.

Heap growth after minute 2: +36 before, +38..45 now (target ≤30 missed). GPU buffers 73 -> 165 before, 52 -> 139 now. Target ≤650 is at the edge.
