## What makes them read as trees
- **Silhouette first**: a canopy clearly wider than the trunk, bumpy round outline (main tier + 4-6 satellite clumps), not a flat stack of leaf plates (edge-on from the road = "sticks"; that was the asset-1 problem).
- **2-4 canopy tiers** getting smaller to the top (deciduous), stepped cones (conifer), flat umbrella (stone pine), tall column (cypress), drooping fronds (palm).
- **Darker underside, lighter top**: vertex colour ramp per clump (underside ~45 %, top ~130 %), so tiers separate even under flat light.
- **Trunk taper + 2 branch stubs**; birch white with black bands; palm ringed tan trunk.
- **Studs on top faces** (LEGO read) and **1x1 plate accents**: blossom (chestnut, almond), fruit (orange), autumn spots, flowering bushes.
- **Colour variation**: per-variant palette (green / lime / autumn orange / red / olive) + per-instance warm/cool + lightness jitter and size jitter.
- Mario Kart World / Animal Crossing / LEGO Fortnite share the same rules: big simple canopy masses, saturated greens, strong top-light gradient, low poly.
