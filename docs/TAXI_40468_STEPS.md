# LEGO 40468 Yellow Taxi: step list from the official PDF (garage-14, 2026-10-10)

**Source.** https://www.lego.com/cdn/product-assets/product.bi.core.pdf/6607919.pdf (44 pages, 32 steps; parts list pp. 42–43).
I rendered every page at 150 dpi and the steps at 300 dpi, and looked at each step. The renders are in
`docs/shots/taxi40468/pdf/NN.jpg` (NN = PDF page; 01 = box art, 41 = final step, 42–43 = parts list).

**Coordinates (one system for every part).**
- **x** runs across the car, in studs: x = −3 … 3 (the body is 6 studs wide). **z** runs along it: front = −z, z = −7 … 7 (the 2×14 chassis).
- **y** is in plates, with y = 0 at the bottom of the chassis plate.
- A part is given as its box `x0..x1 / z0..z1 / y0..y1`. "facing −z" means its studs or slope face the front.
- Half-stud values (±2.5, ±1.5) are real: the set uses jumper plates (15573) to shift the cabin and roof by ½ stud.
- SNOT parts on the bracket faces sit off the stud grid. Their offsets are given in studs (z) and plates (y).
- LEGO proportions: stud 8 mm = 1, plate 3.2 mm = 0.4 stud, brick = 3 plates. In game units: stud = GB_U 0.6, plate = GB_PH 0.24.

Colours: Y yellow, W white, K black, DSG dark stone grey, MSG medium stone grey, BL blue, G dark green, TC trans-clear, TR trans-red.

## Notes
1. **The set has no chequer stripe.** 40468's livery is solid yellow, black "TAXI" door stickers, a green/blue roof sign and black bumpers' DSG edges. A chequer stripe would not match the real set, so none is added.
2. **Front/back.** The PDF builds the back first (steps 4–11), then turns the car round (step 12) and builds the front.
3. **Uncertain positions.** These were read from 2D views and could be ½–1 stud off: the 3 MSG jumpers under the rear window (19),
   the rear window slopes (24) and the 2×3 hood tiles (29). The 2×3 tiles only fit as drawn if they overlap the 2×4 plate. In-game they
   are 2×2-wide hood tiles over x −2..2, z −5..−3 (closest fit).
4. **Hidden parts.** The 1×3 MSG spacers (3) and the bearing elements (31) are under the car; they are built but rarely seen.
