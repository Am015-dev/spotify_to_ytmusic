# Garage research notes (pGAR1/pGAR2), 2026-10-06

## Minifigure proportions (pGAR1)
Verified:
- A minifigure is 4 cm (40 mm) tall including the head stud. Brick Architect: 25 plate-units × 1.6 mm. https://brickarchitect.com/scale/
- A minifig torso is as wide and as deep as a 1×2 brick (about 16 × 8 mm). Same source.
- The main body (torso) of the original LEGO minifigure drawing is 12.82 mm tall. Search result citing the technical drawing; https://www.flickr.com/photos/61628236@N05/8354305365

The model uses 1 unit ≈ 32.5 mm:
- legs + hips 0.44 (14 mm), torso 0.42 (13.6 mm), torso top 0.76 × the bottom width, depth 0.24 = half the width;
- head Ø 0.29, 0.28 tall, plus the stud;
- total 1.23 (40 mm).

## LEGO 2K Drive vehicles (pGAR2)
Verified:
- The vehicle automatically switches between a street car, an off-roader and a boat depending on the terrain. Racinggames.gg / Push Square guides.
- Street, off-road and water vehicles are equipped separately and can be mixed and matched. Up to 3 loadouts.
- Stats: Top Speed, Acceleration, Handling, Health, (Melee Power), Weight.
- Weight classes: Super Light, Light, Medium, Heavy, Super Heavy, Massive. https://www.ggrecon.com/guides/lego-2k-drive-how-to-build-best-cars/
- Perks give stat boosts and other effects. Up to three can be equipped. Same source.
- Vehicles are bought with Brickbux in Unkie's Emporium (e.g. 10,000 and 14,000 Brickbux). https://screenrant.com/lego-2k-drive-best-vehicles-cost-brickbux/ and https://lego.2k.com/drive/faq/

NOT verified:
- The official rarity tier names. A search summary said "Neat / Cool / Awesome / Super Awesome", but no page I could open confirmed it. So the game uses its own tiers, COMMON / RARE / EPIC / LEGENDARY, and does not claim they are 2K's.
