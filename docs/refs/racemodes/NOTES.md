# Race-mode references (web research, 2026-10-17)
Scope: demolition-racer variations for a city racer (Frankfurt/Athens), brick cars, LEGO smashables, items, AI rivals, touch+PC.

## Modes

### Wreckfest: Derby (deathmatch / last-car-standing)
- Rules: two variants: timed score (damage + wrecks) or single life, last car running wins. Stars: primary (last standing) + secondary (damage dealt).
- Arena: closed bowl/oval arena, cars start ringed. Fun: pure crunch, no skill gate on lines.
- Map: "Brick Derby" in a plaza/stadium; HP bar per car, bricks fly off as damage; last car or most points at timer.
- Src: https://www.ludo.guide/guide/wreckfest/getting-started/game-modes-explained

### Wreckfest: Banger Racing / Banger Bash
- Rules: lap race where contact is rewarded; Banger Bash scores points for takeouts + race position on a figure-8.
- Win: most points. Fun: race + brawl hybrid, comebacks via hits.
- Map: normal city circuit, but takedown = +points, position = +points; no new track needed.
- Src: https://egmnow.com/wreckfests-banger-racing-car-pack-released-alongside-free-banger-bash-mode/

### Wreckfest: Figure-8
- Rules: lap race on an "8" with a crossover; avoid or cause T-bones at the X.
- Layout: oval + X cut in the middle; at-grade crossover (Suzuka uses a bridge).
- Map: route two streets through one plaza intersection; rivals AI must yield/ignore; smashables on the X.
- Src: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_8_racing ; https://speedsport.com/more-racing/other-series/the-magic-of-figure-8/

### FlatOut 2: Derby
- Rules: destroy all rivals or cause most damage; winner = most damage not longest survivor.
- Map: same as Wreckfest derby; add per-hit damage score popups.
- Src: https://flatout.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Modes ; https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/FlatOut_2

### FlatOut 2: Stunt (driver-launch minigames)
- Rules: ram a ramp, driver is ejected as projectile, steer flight (aerobatics add drag) to hit targets: bowling pins, dartboard, field goal, hoops, ski-jump (score target 700).
- Win: score. Fun: short, silly, repeatable (20 s).
- Map: launch the minifig out of the car into a pin cluster of LEGO bricks/bowling at a plaza; one tap to aim.
- Src: https://flatout.fandom.com/wiki/FlatOut_2 ; https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/FlatOut_2

### Destruction AllStars: Mayhem
- Rules: FFA deathmatch; points for damage, wrecks, KOs; timer. Later twists: Most Wanted (3 wrecks marks you, bounty), Team Targets.
- Map: arena derby with score for hits; marked leader = bounty.
- Src: https://primagames.com/tips/all-game-modes-destruction-allstars ; https://gematsu.com/2020/11/destruction-allstars-gameplay-trailer-game-modes-detailed

### Destruction AllStars: Carnado
- Rules: wrecking/damaging earns gears stored on car; score by driving into central tornado to bank them. Risk/reward carry.
- Map: central "brick tornado/fountain" to deposit carried studs; rivals hunt carrier.
- Src: https://primagames.com/tips/all-game-modes-destruction-allstars

### Destruction AllStars: Stockpile (Frenzy)
- Rules: wrecks drop gears on the floor; teams bank them in bases; most banked wins. Frenzy adds a car-killing tornado.
- Map: drop-on-smash studs, deposit zones in two squares.
- Src: https://primagames.com/tips/all-game-modes-destruction-allstars ; https://gamingnexus.com/News/49466/Destruction-AllStars-launches-new-season-of-content

### Destruction AllStars: Gridfall
- Rules: floor tiles collapse over time, space shrinks; limited lives, more earned via points.
- Map: city block on a baseplate; edge plates fall off like bricks; last car wins.
- Src: https://primagames.com/tips/all-game-modes-destruction-allstars

### Burnout Paradise: Road Rage
- Rules: 8 players, two teams. Runners drive to checkpoint then finish seeing only the next checkpoint; hunters see all, ambush.
- Win: runners finish, or hunters wreck them. Fun: asymmetric chase on open streets.
- Map: player = runner, AI police/rivals = hunters (or reverse); city route, timer.
- Src: https://shacknews.com/article/52228/new-burnout-paradise-multiplayer-modes ; https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=882

### Burnout Paradise: Marked Man
- Rules: random player marked for 90 s; +1 survive, +1 per takedown of the marked; marked cannot see hunters on map, slower, no boost. Rotates.
- Map: AI pack hunts the player; survive 90 s to win; or bounty on leader.
- Src: https://shacknews.com/article/52228/new-burnout-paradise-multiplayer-modes

### Burnout Paradise: Stunt Run
- Rules: 8 players, 2 min, score stunts: barriers, billboards, ramps, rolls, flat spins; takedowns multiply; being taken down loses combo.
- Map: score smashing LEGO props + ramps with a combo meter in a 2 min city loop.
- Src: https://shacknews.com/article/52228/new-burnout-paradise-multiplayer-modes

### Burnout 3 / Paradise: Crash mode (Showdown)
- Rules: one car, one busy junction; cause max monetary damage in pileup. Aftertouch steers wreck, Crashbreaker explosion, pickups x2/x4/halve. 100 junctions in B3.
- Paradise Showdown: choose crash site, flip car into traffic.
- Map: one junction filled with AI traffic and LEGO stuff; launch car, steer in slow-mo, tap for detonate; score = bricks scattered. Cheap and very viral.
- Src: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnout_3:_Takedown ; https://mobygames.com/game/14831

### Mario Kart: Balloon Battle
- Rules: pop opponent balloons with items; MK8D start 5 balloons, lose all -> half points, respawn 3.
- Map: arena with item boxes; balloons = HP.
- Src: https://www.mariowiki.com/Battle_Mode ; https://mariokart.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Mode

### Mario Kart: Bob-omb Blast
- Rules: only item is bombs; hit = +1, hit taken = -1; first to 3-4 wins.
- Map: bombs only pickups, small plaza.
- Src: https://www.mariowiki.com/Battle_Mode

### Mario Kart: Shine Thief
- Rules: hold Shine until timer ends; drop when hit/falling; carrier slower.
- Map: carry a golden brick/trophy; carrier slower; rivals chase. Easy AI: chase carrier.
- Src: https://www.mariowiki.com/Battle_Mode

### Mario Kart: Coin Runners
- Rules: two teams, 3 min, collect scattered coins; hit = lose coins.
- Map: studs scattered, hits drop studs. Reuses LEGO stud pickups.
- Src: https://www.mariowiki.com/Battle_Mode

### LEGO 2K Drive: Race / Brick Brawl / World Challenges / Quests / OTGs
- Race: Circuit (laps) or Point-to-Point (checkpoints).
- Brick Brawl: 3v3 red/blue, few minutes, +1 per wrecked opponent.
- World Challenges: collect lost parts fast, Red Brick Green Brick; Quests: delivery/recover tasks from NPCs; On-the-Gos: quick tasks via blue gates.
- Map: this is our target feel: gates start 20-30 s mini events anywhere on the map. Event names Smash/Getaway/Pickup not confirmed as official.
- Src: https://lego.2k.com/drive/features/modes/ ; https://support.2k.com/hc/articles/15735213738899 ; https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/lego-2k-drive-preview/

### Hot Wheels Unleashed (2 Turbocharged modes)
- Elimination: timer, last place cut; Drift Master: drift points over lap, hit resets; Waypoint: free route finding with jump; Grab the Gears: collect and bank gears in arena; Clash Derby: monster trucks derby; Time Attack, Quick Race.
- Map: Elimination on existing track (cheap, strong tension); Drift Master with combo.
- Src: https://gamerjournalist.com/all-game-modes-in-hot-wheels-unleashed-2-turbocharged/ ; https://traxion.gg/five-new-game-modes-unveiled-for-hot-wheels-unleashed-2-turbocharged/
