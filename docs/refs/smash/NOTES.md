# Smash vs bump: how arcade racers handle props and traffic

Research pass 2026-10-17, time-boxed. Gaps are marked "unconfirmed": no source found, do not treat as fact.

## LEGO 2K Drive
- Smashing destructible objects fills the boost meter faster than the passive refill and also restores vehicle health, so smashing is a reward, not a penalty.
  https://racinggames.gg/article/lego-2k-drive-beginners-guide-5-essential-tips-and-tricks-you-need-to-know
  https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/lego-2k-drive-tips-and-tricks-for-beginners
- Breakability is by size: you cannot knock down buildings, but almost everything else breaks. Large structures stop you.
  https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2023/05/lego-2k-drive-review-get-all-your-motors-bricking/
- Brickbash (Class B unlock): hold boost for over 3 s to shove opponents aside. Quickbash (Class A) is a stronger burst that obliterates opponents in the path.
  https://racinggames.gg/misc/lego-2k-drive-beginners-guide-5-essential-tips-and-tricks-you-need-to-know
- Unconfirmed: exact light-tap behaviour and speed loss. Official page https://2k.com/games/lego-2k-drive/features/driving-techniques/ returned 403.

## Burnout Paradise
- Dedicated Smash objects (chain-link fences with yellow hazard signs, plus gates and billboards) are driven through. A patch added a flashing effect so they are easier to spot.
  https://i1.trueachievements.com/game/Burnout-Paradise/walkthrough/3
- Traffic checking: you can slam same-direction traffic. Cars with high strength check traffic without being punted around.
  https://gamicus.fandom.com/wiki/Burnout_Paradise
- Boost is earned by speed, oncoming-traffic weaving and near misses (Speed cars); stunt and aggression cars earn it differently.
  https://gamicus.fandom.com/wiki/Burnout_Paradise  https://burnout.fandom.com/wiki/Burnout_5
- Damage has two outcomes: "driveaway" (all four wheels kept, keep driving) or "wrecked" (wheel lost, engine too damaged, or off the map). Cars deform and tear into several pieces.
  https://gamicus.fandom.com/wiki/Burnout_Paradise
- Unconfirmed: a numeric speed threshold for breaking a Smash.

## Hot Wheels Unleashed
- Reviewers call collisions weak: crashes into walls or rivals feel unsatisfying with little sense of impact and little penalty. Others report cars knocked off-track or spinning out after bad landings.
  https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/hot-wheels-unleashed-review-nintendo-switch-pc-ps4-ps5-xbox-one-series-x
  https://www.theouterhaven.net/2021/09/hot-wheels-unleashed-review-bite-sized-arcade-racing-fun
- Drifting builds boost; most cars have 1 to 3 boost charges. Track furniture includes turbo tiles, bumpers and guardrails.
  https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/hot-wheels-unleashed-review-stunt-track-racer/
- The sequel adds a lateral dash to barge opponents; handling varies with weight and surface.
  https://nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch/hot-wheels-unleashed-2-turbocharged
- Unconfirmed: any destructible props. The coverage found shows none.

## Mario Kart 8 (Deluxe)
- Star: invincible to items and hazards, plows through course obstacles and drivers, about 1/3 faster, no off-road slowdown. Star-on-Star contact is only a small bump.
  https://mariokart.fandom.com/wiki/Starman
- Weight matters in bumps: a heavy character can shove a light one into a wall.
  https://press-start.com.au/guides/2017/05/02/how-to-avoid-blue-shell-mario-kart-8-deluxe/
- Unconfirmed: light-tap slowdown values for normal course props. Normal contact is a bump with no breakage.

## Crash Team Racing (Nitro-Fueled)
- Crates are smashed by driving into them. Item crates give weapons. Wumpa crates give fruit; 10 fruit raises top speed and strengthens items.
  https://comic-watch.com/uncategorized/crash-team-racing-nitro-fueled
- TNT crates land on your head and count down. Nitro crates explode on contact. Both spin you out inside the blast radius.
  https://comic-watch.com/uncategorized/crash-team-racing-nitro-fueled
- Original game guide: a hit slows you down and removes your hop for a moment. The source does not say which hazard.
  https://www.gamepressure.com/crash-team-racing-nitro-fueled/starting-tips/zdc52c
- Unconfirmed: whether a light tap breaks a plain crate. The sources say you smash crates by driving into them.

## Takeaways for Mainhattan Overdrive
- Tap = nudge. Light contact gives a small bounce and a few loose bricks, with at most about 5% speed loss. Mario Kart bumps and LEGO 2K breakability by size both point this way.
- Full smash needs speed or boost. Above a speed threshold, or with boost or Brickbash active, the prop breaks completely and the car plows through. This matches the Star and Brickbash behaviour.
- Smashing is a reward: each break fills boost and heals a little (LEGO 2K). Do not charge a large speed penalty on small props. Keep a hard stop only for buildings and large solid props.
- Break by size class: small props (cones, bins, signs) always break, medium (benches, cars) need speed or boost, large (buildings) never break.
- Vehicles and traffic burst into bricks and loose pieces, not a whole flipped object. Weight matters: a heavy car shoves a light one and keeps momentum.
- Mark breakables visually (a Burnout Smash-style cue) so players know what to hit, and keep Hot Wheels' lesson in mind: a hit with no impact feel is a failure, so use sound, a small camera kick and a brick burst even on taps.
