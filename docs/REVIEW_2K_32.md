### How the gauntlet `hit` row should pick its target (a script fix, not a game fix)
1. Only cars on the player's street: |bearing| ≤ 0.35 rad (now 1.0), and the car's offset from the player's heading line ≤ 4 m.
2. Only cars that close: closing speed ≥ 3 m/s measured over 0.3 s, and the target's cv ≥ 2 m/s. No standing or parked cars, which the hub recycles.
3. No car that turns off before the meeting point: skip a car if the distance left on its edge (`(1−c.t)` × edge length to `c.b`) is less than the predicted meeting distance.
4. Pick only while the player is on the street and not on water (`router.off(st) ≤ 1.5`, not a boat). Otherwise drive back to the street first.
5. Re-pick, without counting a miss, if the target changes edge (`c.a>c.b`) before it is inside 12 m. The attempt counts from 12 m.
6. Vanish = contact if the target had `hitT>0` on its last read (add `hitT` before the vanish check, `__GH.car` already returns it) or `prev.d < 7 + closing speed × sample step`. At 35 m/s the 2-frame step is 2.3 m, so 8.5 m and 7.4 m vanishes were probably smashes.
7. "No car in front" is no attempt: try up to 3 more pick windows before counting. Report attempts and real misses separately.
