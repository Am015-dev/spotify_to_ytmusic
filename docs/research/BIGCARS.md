# Big templates: real LEGO reference sets (verified 2026-10-08)

Units: stud 8 mm, plate 3.2 mm, brick 9.6 mm (0.8 cm per stud). Official LEGO sizes are "over X cm" (minimum, includes tyres and mirrors).
Brickset size order is H × L × W. In-game templates use the Speed Champions 8-wide scale, so City sets (6-wide) are scaled ×4/3 in length and width.

Traits used in the templates:
- Truck 60440: bonneted US tractor (not cab-over), tilting bonnet, sleeper behind the cab, clear 2×6×2 screen, grey/black grilles, one grey exhaust stack, mud flaps, step panels; box trailer with white sides, landing legs. Game template = the tractor + a short box body on one chassis (a hinged trailer is out of scope).
- Bus 60407: double-decker, red with black window bands and a yellow/black stripe, curved front, glass band on the lower deck, 2 axles.
- Limo 60102: white stretch limo = two car chassis end to end, 4 doors, two roof sections, red brake light on the roof, orange front indicators. Built 8-wide (the 4-wide set was called "too narrow").
- Monster truck 60180: short pickup body high on giant balloon tyres (most of the height), blue/white body, orange rims, 6-wide cab.
