| Trait | Source |
|---|---|
| Every vehicle is one of three types: **Street, Off-Road, Water**. The vehicle you drive swaps between them by terrain. | [Checkpoint review](https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2023/05/lego-2k-drive-review-get-all-your-motors-bricking/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_2K_Drive) |

| A vehicle card shows 5 stats: Top Speed, Acceleration, Handling, Health, Weight. | same Push Square guide |

## Our collection (v87s+ garage tab RIDES → COLLECTION)
- Three type tabs: 🚗 STREET · 🛻 OFF-ROAD · 🚤 WATER, each with its owned/total count.
- Big card grid: a 3D render of each vehicle, its name, a rarity stripe in the tier colour, ★ favourite, and EQUIPPED or 🔒.
- One tap on a card equips it for that type (mix and match like the 2K loadout). A second button on the card opens a street car in the builder.
- Sort: RARITY · A–Z · NEW. Filter: ALL · OWNED · ★ FAVOURITES. The header shows "owned 9 / 13 · locked 4".
- ALL_OPEN=true, so nothing is really locked today; the counts still use the real rules.
- Phone: at most 5 visible buttons outside the grid; text is 12 px or larger.
