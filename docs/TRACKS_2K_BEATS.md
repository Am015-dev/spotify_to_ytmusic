## General design rules (SEEN unless marked)
- **Grid 8, laps by length**: long lap ~60-70 s → 2-3 laps; short lap 15-30 s → 5 laps. Race ≈ 2-3 min.
- **Something every ~5-10 s** (INFERRED from frame density): no frame shows an empty road with no chevron, item row, rival or set piece in view.
- **Item boxes**: rows of 4-5 glowing discs spanning the full road/river width (ref2k_4, ref2k_6).
- **Corner signalling**: chevron boards on the outer edge of every bend (yellow on land, orange at night). On water they sit on plank walls (ref2k_1).
- **Ramps** are marked with giant painted chevrons on the ground (green/yellow, ref2k_2/3). Transforms happen automatically at the water edge.
- **Checkpoints/finish**: white posts with cyan glow and a glowing floor line (ref2k_5/6).
- **Breakables** (fences, hydrants, barricades, trees) refill boost. They line the road edges in rows.
- **Width**: town street 4 lanes (~4-5 car widths); rivers ~8-15 car widths; dirt tracks ~4 car widths plus open verge.
- **Framing**: there is always a near wall (cliff, fence, shopfronts) on one side and a far landmark (mesa, truss bridge, moon, blimp, duck) in view. The skyline sits low, so ~40 % of the frame is sky.
- **HUD**: big position top-left, lap x/y + timer top-right, standings list, round minimap bottom-left, boost bar bottom-centre, gap meter ("340m").
